## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** tell the AI what to add, change or remove in a questionnaire and to have it propose follow-up questions without touching answered ones, **so that** the question list keeps up with new information while I stay in control.

This plan's slice: the run record holds questionnaire proposal runs and the AI reads exactly the right context for them: the request, the marked notes and files, and the questionnaire with its answered questions fixed, with the instruction in its own slot.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Per-operation parsing** (W-1). `parseRunProposalFor(operation, value)` and `parseRunDecisionsFor(operation, value)` dispatch by the run's `operation`, never by `schemaVersion` (Phase 12's envelope in the same `proposal` column is `schemaVersion: 2`, the questionnaire envelope `schemaVersion: 1`): `questionnaire-proposals` and `question-rewrite` use `parseQuestionnaireProposal` and `parseQuestionnaireDecisionEvents` (15-02); the Phase 12 operations keep `parseRunProposal` and `parseRunDecisions` (v1). Both keep `AI_RUN_BOUNDS` (`proposalBytes` 1 MB, `decisionEvents` 5,000) for every operation. `completeAiRun(strapi, runId, input)`'s `input.proposal` widens to `AiRunProposalEnvelope | QuestionnaireProposalV1` and is checked through `parseRunProposalFor` against the stored run's `operation`. `appendAiRunDecisions(strapi, runId, events)` takes `readonly (AiRunDecisionEventV1 | QuestionnaireDecisionEventV2)[]`, loads the run's `operation` and validates both the stored log and the appended events through `parseRunDecisionsFor`; a v1 event on a questionnaire run, or a v2 event on a Phase 12 run, is refused with `AiRunRecordError` and nothing is written. The harness's `completeAiRunFixture` and `appendAiRunDecisionsFixture` (typed today to Phase 12's `AiRunProposalEnvelope` and `AiRunDecisionEventV1[]` in `strapi-http-harness.ts`) widen to the same unions.
- **Summary version.** `ContextSummaryV4` (the number 15-01 recorded; today's tree ends at `ContextSummaryV3`) = `Omit<ContextSummaryV3, "schemaVersion"> & { schemaVersion: 4 }` plus `questionnaire: { scope, questionsOffered, answered, answerContent, openItemsListed, rejectedItemsListed, rejectedItemsTotal, removedListed } | null` and `instruction: { source: "analyst" | "plain-language-preset" | "default-task" } | null`; `ContextSummary` gains it. `buildAssessmentContext` always emits it; generation and draft runs carry `questionnaire: null`; generation carries `instruction` (`analyst` or `default-task`), the draft `null`. In `ai-run-record.ts`, `SUMMARY_KEYS_BY_VERSION` gains `4` (v3's keys plus `questionnaire` and `instruction`), `parseContextSummary` parses both new sections closed for v4 and projects v1, v2 and v3 with both new keys null (beside today's empty notes and attachments projections), and `ContextSummaryRead` widens so every version reads with `questionnaire` and `instruction`.
- **Attachment bounds** (X-1). `ATTACHMENT_CONTEXT_BOUNDS` `satisfies Record<AiRunOperation, number>` and `parseSummaryAttachments` requires `bound === ATTACHMENT_CONTEXT_BOUNDS[operation]`, so widening `AiRunOperation` needs a bound for each new operation: `questionnaire-proposals: 30_000` and `question-rewrite: 30_000` (Phase 14 D-33's questionnaire bound, about 30% of `CONTEXT_BOUNDS.questionnairePromptCharacters`). The spec's `AiRunContextAttachments.bound` enum and the web's `z.union([z.literal(30_000), z.literal(60_000)])` already hold 30,000, so neither changes.
- **Run read.** `AiRun.operation` enum gains the two operations, and so does `AiRunContextSummary.operation` (its own closed enum in the spec), and the web's shared `operationSchema` in `ai-assistance-schema.ts`, which parses both; `AiRunContextSummary.schemaVersion` gains `4`; `AiRunContextSummary` gains required nullable `questionnaire` (`AiRunContextQuestionnaire`, closed) and `instruction` (`AiRunContextInstruction`, closed). `AiRun.instruction` (12-10) already carries the text.
- **Builder inputs** (X-2). `buildAssessmentContext(records, input)` today takes `{ operation, policy? }`, renders `renderAnalystInstructionSlot(null)`, sends answers only for `assessment-draft`, always offers the Risk Type library and types `summary` as `ContextSummaryV3`. Its input gains `instruction?: SlotInstruction | null`, `instructionSource?: InstructionSource | null` and `questionnaire?: QuestionnaireProposalContextPart` (Task 2's output: its reference values, its `answers` section, its allowed sets and its summary section). The builder renders `renderAnalystInstructionSlot(input.instruction ?? null)`; appends the questionnaire part's reference values to `referenceData` and its `answers` section (submitted answers by `Qn`) to the requestor block, while the draft's own answers stay `assessment-draft`-only; returns the questionnaire allowed sets on a new optional `AssessmentContext.questionnaire` beside `allowed`; offers no Risk Type library for `question-rewrite` (no `riskType.*` reference values, and the summary's `riskTypes` reads `{ offered: 0, total, names: [] }`); and types `summary` as `ContextSummaryV4` with `questionnaire` from the part (or null) and `instruction` from `instructionSource` (or null). Questionnaire generation passes its instruction and source through this same path (D-12): `buildAssessmentContextFor` accepts them for `questionnaire-generation` too, and 15-08 wires the job input to it.
- **Questionnaire part** (`buildQuestionnaireProposalContext(input)`, pure). Input: `scope`, `targetQuestionDocumentId` (rewrite), the questions in display order (active and removed, each with `documentId`, `questionKey`, section `{ key, title, order }`, `order`, `questionText`, `helpText`, `answerType`, `choices`, `required`, `visibility`, `removed`, `answered`, and `answerLabel` only when answer content is included), `answerContent` (`included` or `withheld`), `openItems` (proposed texts and kinds), `rejectedItems` (proposed text, reason, decided-at), `instructionSource`. Output: the `referenceData` sections (questions as `Q1…Qn` in display order with `answered="yes"` or `"no"`, section titles, answer types, choice labels, required flag and visibility as `Qn equals {label}`; already proposed as `P1…`; rejected by the analyst as `X1…` with their reasons, the 100 most recent first; removed by the analyst as `R1…`), the `answers` `RequestorDataSection` (submitted answers only, by `Qn`), the allowed sets (`ProposalAllowedSets` plus `refs: Map<"Qn", documentId>`, `answeredRefs`, `removedDocumentIds`, `controllerChoiceLabels: Map<"Qn", label[]>`, `sourceRefs` = request fields, `note-{n}`, `file-{n}`, answered `Qn` and `instruction`) and the summary section. Rewrite scope: the target and its section in full, other active question texts only, no answers, no lists. No names, ids, dates or record numbers in any rendered text (Phase 12 D-04).
- **Bounds** (`QUESTIONNAIRE_CONTEXT_BOUNDS`): `activeQuestions: 150`, `openItemsListed: 200`, `rejectedItemsListed: 100` (ranked, with the total in the summary, never silent), `removedListed: 200`; any other excess throws `AssessmentContextTooLargeError`, and the prompt still meets `CONTEXT_BOUNDS.questionnairePromptCharacters`.
- **Loader.** `buildAssessmentContextFor(strapi, { operation, requestId, questionnaireId?, targetQuestionId?, instruction?, instructionSource? })` passes the instruction and its source to the builder for `questionnaire-generation` and the two new operations, and for the two operations reads the questionnaire with its questions, the answered flag from `isAnsweredValue`, answer content only when every active visible question holds a submitted envelope (`projectQuestions`' submitted-batch rule), and the questionnaire's completed runs of the two operations (at most 101, Document Service filtered by `questionnaire.documentId`, `operation $in`, `status: "completed"`, fields `documentId`, `operation`, `proposal`, `decisions`) to derive open and rejected items through the fold, skipping damaged runs; it includes the request part, the selected systems and assets and the Risk Type library for `questionnaire-proposals` only, and the marked notes (13-16) and opted-in file text (Phase 14) for both.
- **Slot** (W-1). The shipped shape stays: `renderAnalystInstructionSlot` returns the empty string for null and `<analystInstruction>` around `promptDataTag("instruction", instruction)` otherwise, and the outer tag name becomes the exported constant `ANALYST_INSTRUCTION_TAG = "analystInstruction"`. What changes is the parameter type, narrowed to `SlotInstruction | null` (only `parseAnalystInstruction` and `PLAIN_LANGUAGE_PRESET` produce one), and the builder passing a non-null instruction through it. Today's slot already escapes a hostile closing tag, so the guardrail cases that only re-check escaping are regression guards that pass before the work; the RED cases are the ones that need the change (the built context carrying the instruction, and the preset).
- **The notes phrase** (D-29; orchestrator decision 3). `contextTooLargeMessage(markedNoteSequences, includedFiles)` keeps every form it serves today (pinned in `assessment-context.unit.test.ts`). A unit assertion pins that every form that names marked notes (one note, several, shortened with `and {n} more`, the over-255 fallback, each with and without files) contains `marked as changing the AI inputs`, and that no other form does (no notes and no files; one file; several files). The web's job surfaces recognise a notes-caused refusal by that phrase alone (15-10).
