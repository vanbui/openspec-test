## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** contest anything in an assessment I am asked to accept and see each point answered, and as a Security Analyst I want to rework the whole assessment and resubmit it, **so that** acceptance rests on content both lines have challenged and agreed.

This plan's slice: the owner sends the assessment back, everyone sees the return, the analyst sees what blocks resubmission and release, and in the next round each decider sees what stands and what changed.

<objective>
Build the send-back confirmation and the returned round's surfaces (D-11, D-13; X-51; 17.2-UI-SPEC §6), the contest items in "Review and submit" and release readiness (D-12; §7), and round n+1's "stands" and "decide again" with the server's comparison under X-50's decision-round headings (D-14; §8; orchestrator amendment 2), on 17.1's round panel, decision form, decision trail and editor.

Purpose: SC1's send-back in the UI, SC2's "cannot resubmit while either is unanswered or before marking the assessment reviewed again", SC3's "the owner sees which decisions stand and, for the changed risk, each changed field before and after, and decides it again", SC5's "the Requestor who owns no risk can raise a point but not send back".
Output: one dialog, changes to five 17.1 components and the participant view, and component tests.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>
