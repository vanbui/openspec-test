## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** contest anything in an assessment I am asked to accept and see each point answered, and as a Security Analyst I want to rework the whole assessment and resubmit it, **so that** acceptance rests on content both lines have challenged and agreed.

This plan's slice: everyone the contest loop waits on is told in the app, each notification opens the point or the assessment, and it is read when its reader acts.

<objective>

Purpose: NOTIFY-04 criteria 1 to 3 and SC4's "the analyst and the owner are told in the app at each step"; D-17's reply kind (Orchestrator Confirmation).
Output: four registry entries, the point-scoped reader, the emissions and reads in two services, the web parser and the tests.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes (the kind enum). Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Kinds** (registry entries in `notification-kinds.ts`, facts in `notification-facts.ts`, sentences and destinations in `notification-projection.ts`, all per 17.2-UI-SPEC §10):
  - `contest-point-raised`: recipient part `security-analyst`; the assigned analyst only; subject the assessment; facts `{ pointNumber, roundNumber }`; destination the analyst page `#point-{k}`; named `{Raiser} raised contest point {k} on {target}: {assessment number} · {request title}`, part-named `A participant raised a contest point: …`, record-level `Contest point raised: …`; icon `MessageSquareWarning`.
  - `contest-point-answered`: the raiser, in the first of their current parts in the order `risk-owner`, `executive-approver`, `requestor`; facts `{ pointNumber, entrySequence, answerOutcome }`; destination the participant page `#point-{k}`; part-named `A Security Analyst answered your contest point {k} ({Changed|Kept}): …` (never named, 19.1 UI-D6); record-level `Contest point answered: …`; icon `MessageSquareQuote`.
  - `contest-point-replied`: the raiser (their part), the assigned analyst and every other analyst who answered the point (`security-analyst`), never the author; facts `{ pointNumber, entrySequence }`; destination `#point-{k}` on the part's page; named `{Author} replied to contest point {k}: …` (an analyst author is named only to analysts), part-named `{A participant|A Security Analyst} replied to contest point {k}: …`; icon `MessagesSquare`.
  - `assessment-returned`: the assigned analyst (`security-analyst`, destination `#review-and-submit`) and every participant other than the sender in their part (destination the participant page `#acceptance-round`); facts `{ roundNumber }`; named `{Sender} sent the assessment back in acceptance round {n}: …`, part-named `A {risk owner|executive approver} sent the assessment back: …`, record-level `Assessment sent back: …`; icon `CornerDownLeft`.
- **Reads.** `readContestPointNotifications({ strapi, trx, assessmentId, pointNumber, kinds, recipientIds })` in `notification-repository.ts`: select the recipients' unread rows of `kinds` whose subject is the assessment, parse `facts` in code with `parseNotificationFacts`, mark read the ids whose `pointNumber` matches; rows whose facts fail to parse stay unread (research §5). Acting: `answerContestPoint` reads the answerer's `contest-point-raised` and `contest-point-replied`; an analyst's `replyToContestPoint` reads their own `contest-point-replied`; the raiser's reply, resolve and withdraw read their `contest-point-answered` and `contest-point-replied`. Reversal: `withdrawContestPoint` (the raise's Undo included) reads every `security-analyst` recipient's `contest-point-raised` for that point. `submitFinalAssessment` reads every unread `assessment-returned` on the assessment, through 19.1's `readNotificationsOnActing` (the assigned analyst's acting read and the participants' reversal read in one call, research §5). Each read runs in the act's transaction.
- **Emissions.** `notify` inside the act's `withAssessmentLock` transaction (it throws outside one): raise → `contest-point-raised`; answer → `contest-point-answered` every time; reply → `contest-point-replied`; `closeAcceptanceRoundAndReopen` on `returned` → `assessment-returned` after the shared reversal reads.
- **Web.** `notifications-schema.ts`'s closed kind list gains the four kinds and its destination parser `#point-{k}`; `notification-item.tsx` maps the four icons.
- **Scope note (the pilot's pacing rule, recorded for the plan check).** 13 files in three tasks: the registry's four files, the two emitting services, the kind enum and the web parser that must change with it.
