## Recorded planning decisions

- **Operation** `GET:/api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/history` (`CSRM_ACCEPTANCE_OPERATIONS.getAssessmentHistory`, operationId `get-risk-assessment-history`), query `rounds` (`recent` default, `all`) and `show` (`everything` default, `contest-points`, `decisions-and-rounds`); any other key or value is 400. Response `{ data: { windows: HistoryWindowView[], earlierWindowCount } }`.
- **Viewer.** The policy refuses the service identity (`SERVICE_IDENTITY_REFUSED_OPERATIONS`). A human Security Analyst reads as `analyst`; anyone else through `findReadableAssessment` (participants from the gate on, through the released record; 404 otherwise, so Maya and a former participant read 404).
- **Queries** (`getAssessmentHistory`; research §4's table, about seven, each bounded by the assessment): rounds of both kinds; submitted round responses of the selected windows' rounds; decision rows of the assessment (through `projectRiskDecision`, comparison included, so the history and the acceptance read never disagree); contest points of the assessment; their entries; for analysts the assessment's `reviewLog` through `parseReviewLog`; for analysts the risk history rows with `occurredAt` between a round's close and the next round's opening, reduced to distinct risks. People are resolved in one batched lookup. Events are placed with `placeHistoryEvent`, analysts' windows split with `splitAnalystWindow`, windows chosen with `selectHistoryWindows`.
- **Events** (`HistoryEventView`, a closed union by `kind` from `HISTORY_EVENT_KINDS`): common `{ kind, at, actorName, part, roundNumber }`, plus per kind `message` (round opened, returned), `reason` (recalled), `riskId` and `riskNumber` (decisions, voids, rework rows), `decision` (`act`, `choice`, `revision`, `approvesOwnerName`), `comparison` (voids), `pointNumber`, `targetLabel`, `text` (raised reason, reply, answer), `answerOutcome`, `undoneKind` (`resolved` or `withdrawn`), `viaUndo`, `reviewAction` (analysts), `changedRisks` (`[{ riskId, riskNumber }]`, analysts). Bodies come back whole (print needs them). The server resolves names and labels; the web words each kind (D-15).
- **Participant exclusions.** `review-logged` and `rework-risks-changed` never; decision rows and voids on a currently withdrawn risk and `risk-withdrawn` voids never (17.1 D-39); points on such a risk or a removed control implementation always, with `targetLabel` from the snapshot and no withdrawal reason, actor or time (D-10).
- **Windows.** `HistoryWindowView`: `{ kind: "round" | "between", round: { kind: "response" | "acceptance", number, outcome, openedAt, closedAt }, between: { fromRoundNumber, toRoundNumber } | null, events }`; newest window first, events oldest first.
- **Volume.** The integration file seeds 50 risks, three deciders, three acceptance rounds and 100 points with 10 entries each, and asserts one `rounds=all` read completes in under 5,000 ms on the test database with at most 12 queries, counted through the knex connection's `query` event during the read.
- **Scope note (the pilot's pacing rule, recorded for the plan check).** 12 files in three tasks.
