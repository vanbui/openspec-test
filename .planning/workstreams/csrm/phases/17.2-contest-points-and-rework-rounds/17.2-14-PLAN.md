## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** contest anything in an assessment I am asked to accept and see each point answered, and as a Security Analyst I want to rework the whole assessment and resubmit it, **so that** acceptance rests on content both lines have challenged and agreed.

This plan's slice: everyone taking part reads the whole acceptance story round by round on one page, and the lists and the analyst's Home show where contest points wait.

<objective>
Build the history route per actor (D-15, D-23; X-52; 17.2-UI-SPEC §9 and orchestrator amendment 4) on the typed read from 17.2-10, linked from both assessment pages, and the list and Home figures for contest points (D-16; §11).

Purpose: SC4's "the history shows both rounds, both points with their replies and answers, and the void, with who and when", ASSESS-26 criterion 4 in the UI, and COLLAB-04's points visible from the lists.
Output: two routes with loading states, the timeline and its wording module, the header links, the list column, filter and captions, the Home figure, and component tests.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.

- **Timeline** (`history-timeline.tsx`, server-rendered, with a small client island only for `Show full text`, `Show text` and `Print`): the `Show` `SegmentedControl` as links writing `show`; one `section` per window with h2 `Acceptance round {n}`, `Response round {n}` or `Between rounds {n} and {n+1}`, the round's outcome badge from the one mapping, and `{opened date} to {closed date}` or `Open since {date}`; events in an `ol`; `Show earlier rounds ({m})` (outline small) links to `?rounds=all#round-{k}` where `round-{k}` is the first newly shown h2's id (the h2 is focusable with `tabIndex={-1}` and the fragment moves focus); compact density through Phase 16's density store; `Print` loads `?rounds=all` and calls `window.print()`; print styles expand clamps and hide controls (§4 Print).
- **Wording** (`history-domain.ts`, zod-free, registered in `parser-free-modules.ts`): `historyEventSentence(event, actor)` returns §9's sentence per kind (for example `{name} sent the assessment back, closing acceptance round {n} as returned.`, `{name}'s decision on {risk number} from round {n} was voided because the risk changed.` with the attribution `Recorded when round {n+1} opened · {instant}`, `{name} raised contest point {k} on {target}.`, `{name} undid the {resolution|withdrawal} of contest point {k}.`, `{c} {risk|risks} changed between rounds {n} and {n+1}:`); `historyEventIcon(kind)` per amendment 4's mapping recorded in must_haves; `historyGroupHeading(window)`; `historyEmptyState(filtered)`. Void events offer `Show what changed` with the event's served comparison through `FieldComparison` (`As decided`, `Now`). Rework rows link each risk to its Phase 16 history sheet (X-29). Quoted text renders as plain text in typographic quotes.
- **Header links.** Both assessment pages render `History` (outline small, `History` icon) in their header from the gate on (participant: whenever the page renders; analyst: always), linking to the actor's route.
