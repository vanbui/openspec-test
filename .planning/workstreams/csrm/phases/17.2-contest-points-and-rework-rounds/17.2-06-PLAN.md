## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** contest anything in an assessment I am asked to accept and see each point answered, and as a Security Analyst I want to rework the whole assessment and resubmit it, **so that** acceptance rests on content both lines have challenged and agreed.

This plan's slice: an open contest point holds back marking reviewed, submission, and release and close through one predicate, and the lifecycle, holder and queue tell the analyst what to do next.
