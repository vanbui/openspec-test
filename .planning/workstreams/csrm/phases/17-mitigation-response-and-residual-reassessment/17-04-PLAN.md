## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: first-line people can find a person by name without seeing the directory. The existing search is hardened in place for every caller and every search is logged (G-17; ASSESS-23).

<objective>
Harden `GET /api/v1/users?search=` in place (D-52): two to 100 characters, no other parameter, at most 20 eligible people through Phase 19.1's `isEligiblePerson` with a set-level form (D-16), two fields only. Refuse the service identity through `SERVICE_IDENTITY_REFUSED_OPERATIONS` (D-53). Log every search with one `csrm.people.search` line through Phase 14's event log (D-54). Publish the bound, restate the two FunctionApi descriptions (D-65), move the matrix row, and make every web person picker wait for two characters (D-55).

Purpose: SC3 ("The people search needs two characters, returns no email and no service identity, and logs each search") and ASSESS-23 criteria 1 to 3; criterion 4's callers (response control owners and 17.1's treatment owners) use this one operation.
Output: the hardened operation, its log line, its contract and the web rule.

Edits Strapi source? Yes (dev servers stopped for the Strapi runs). Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

A unit case asserts every returned row would also pass `isEligiblePerson`. `searchActiveUserDocuments` in `risk-workflow.ts` delegates to it.
- **Event line.** `recordPeopleSearch(logger, { actor: { documentId, displayName }, term: string | null, resultCount: number, outcome: "searched" | "refused", reason?: string }, at = new Date())` in `security-event-log.ts`, writing event `csrm.people.search` at level info through `logSecurityEvent`. The controller passes the term for `searched`, `TERM_TOO_SHORT` and `UNKNOWN_PARAMETER`, and null for `TERM_TOO_LONG` and `TERM_MISSING` (research A6). The actor comes from `ctx.state` after the identity middlewares, never the query.
- **Policy.** `GET:/api/v1/users` joins `SERVICE_IDENTITY_REFUSED_OPERATIONS` and the matrix mirror; nothing else changes in the grant path.
- **Contract.** Spec `/v1/users` parameter `search` gets `minLength: 2` (from 1) and the description `Two to 100 characters of a display name. Returns at most 20 active people, never a service identity, as documentId and displayName only. Every search is logged.`; a 400 response remains. Catalogue (docs copy, then copied over): the "Search Users" FunctionApi of `function.risk_assessment_requests.new_request` and the "Search Active Assessment Owners" FunctionApi of `function.risk_assessments.risk_assessment_details` keep their ids, names and URLs, with the description `Finds active people by at least two letters of their name for a person field; returns names only, never a service identity, and logs every search.`
- **Web.** Every input schema for the people search (`activeUserSearchInputSchema` in `assessment-actions.ts`, the owner search schema in `risk-actions.ts`, and any other found by `git grep -n "'/v1/users'" apps/csrm/src`) becomes `.min(2)`; the picker component that calls the people search (today `OwnerCombobox`; follow Phase 10's record picker if it moved there, and record it) sends nothing below two trimmed characters and shows the polite status line `Type at least 2 letters of a name.` instead of searching.
