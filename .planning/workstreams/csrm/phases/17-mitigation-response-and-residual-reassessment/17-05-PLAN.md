## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: a Security Analyst names the owner of every risk in one step and of one risk on its own, with every person relation refusing an ineligible person and the assigned analyst, and every change in the risk's history.

<objective>
Serve owner nomination (D-17): the batch operation and the risk update's `riskOwner`, with one validation (D-16, D-20): eligible people only through Phase 19.1's `isEligiblePerson`, never the request's assigned analyst, never a withdrawn risk. Validate `treatmentOwner` and `controlOwner` through the same predicate (D-16). Record every owner change in Phase 16's risk history with its source (D-19). Publish, grant and prove the operation (D-02, D-64).

Purpose: SC1 ("The analyst nominates the Requestor as owner of every risk in one step, then a different first-line person for one risk; … the assigned analyst cannot be named") and ASSESS-21 criteria 1, 2 and 4's naming half. The actor-is-owner rule is 17-06's; the round lock reaches nomination through the guard in 17-09.
Output: one new service, one operation with its contract chain, eligibility on every person relation and the two new history sources.

Edits Strapi source? Yes (dev servers stopped). Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
