## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: the approved storage exists and the one participation rule is decided in code. Rounds, responses and items can be stored, legacy assessments read as before, and every later plan reads participation and the stage from one pure module and refuses with one closed reason vocabulary.

Output: four schema files, one pure module, the closed reasons, two error schemas and three test files.
