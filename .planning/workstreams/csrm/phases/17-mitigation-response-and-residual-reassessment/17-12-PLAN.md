## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: each participant is told in the app when a response waits for them, and the assigned analyst when one arrives, through Phase 19.1's notification centre, and nobody is told about an assessment they may not read.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Registry rows.** `NOTIFICATION_PARTS` gains `risk-owner`. `response-requested`: parts `requestor`, `risk-owner`; subject `assessment`; recipients `response-participants`; analyst read null; names the actor in no part (17-UI-SPEC UI-D21). `response-submitted`: part `security-analyst`; subject `assessment`; recipients `assigned-analyst`; analyst read `CSRM_ASSESSMENT_OPERATIONS.findOne`; names the actor (the responder). `assessment-released` gains the recipients rule `release-participants` (the Requestor in part `requestor` and, when `sharedWithParticipantsAt` is set, each current owner of an active risk in part `risk-owner`; one per person, the Requestor part first) and the part `risk-owner`.
- **Facts.** Both kinds: `{ schemaVersion: 1, actorDocumentId, roundNumber }` (`roundNumber` a positive safe integer), with a closed parser that fails safe as 19.1's do.
- **Event and recipients.** `NotificationEvent` gains `roundNumber?` and `participants?: { documentId: string; part: "requestor" | "risk-owner" }[]`; `notify` resolves `response-participants` from `participants` (one per person, the actor, the service identity and inactive users dropped through `isEligiblePerson`).
- **Readability.** `response-requested` (both parts) through `whereAssessmentReadable`; `assessment-released` for both parts through `whereReleasedAssessmentReadable`; `response-submitted` through 19.1's analyst branch for the kind's analyst read. The count oracle gains both kinds.
- **Wording (17-UI-SPEC §12, verbatim, no closing full stop).** `response-requested`/`requestor`: part-named `A Security Analyst released a risk assessment for your response: {record}`, record level `Response requested: {record}`. `response-requested`/`risk-owner`: `A Security Analyst asked for your response as a risk owner: {record}` · `Response requested: {record}`. `response-submitted`/`security-analyst`: named `{Responder} submitted a response to round {n}: {record}`, part-named `A participant submitted a response to round {n}: {record}`, record level `Response submitted: {record}`. `assessment-released`/`risk-owner` uses the release sentences. `{record}` is `{assessment number} · {request title}`.
- **Destinations.** `response-requested`: `/app/assessments/{assessmentId}#your-response`, or `/app/review/assessments/{assessmentId}#your-response` when the recipient holds the human analyst read (the controller passes `csrmAuthorization.humanAnalyst` to the projection as `recipientIsHumanAnalyst`); `response-submitted`: `/app/review/assessments/{assessmentId}#participants`; `assessment-released`/`risk-owner`: the participant page.
- **Read paths.** `NOTIFICATION_ACTING_READS` gains `submit-response` (kind `response-requested`, subject the assessment, the submitter, matching `roundNumber`). `NOTIFICATION_REVERSAL_READS` gains `close-response-round` (`response-requested` of that round, any recipient; `response-submitted` of that round, the assignee) and `open-response-round` (`response-requested` of earlier rounds, any recipient).
- **Emission points.** `openResponseRound`: after the responses are created, `notify({ kind: "response-requested", participants, roundNumber, … })` and the `open-response-round` reversal read. `closeResponseRound`: the `close-response-round` reversal read. `submitMyResponse`: `notify({ kind: "response-submitted", roundNumber, … })` and the `submit-response` acting read. All inside the operation's transaction.
- **Contract and web.** The spec's `NotificationKind` enum gains both values; the web's closed kind parser gains them; `notification-item.tsx` maps `response-requested` to `MessageSquareReply` and `response-submitted` to `Inbox` (12px, `aria-hidden`).
