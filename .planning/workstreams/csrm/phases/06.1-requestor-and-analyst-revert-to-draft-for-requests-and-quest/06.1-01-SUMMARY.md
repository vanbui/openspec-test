# Phase 06.1 Plan 01: Request Reversal Summary

Implemented the protected request return-to-draft workflow with persisted latest-reversal facts and a typed contract.

## Delivered

- Added a locked server-side request reversal operation that accepts a trimmed reason, returns an eligible submitted request to draft, clears `submittedDate`, and records the reason, time, and reverting identity.
- Restricted reversal to the Requestor and a human Security Analyst through a dedicated API-only Function per aggregate. The service analyst role deliberately holds neither reversal Function, preventing automation from returning submitted work to draft.
- Published matching route, catalogue, OpenAPI, and generated web declaration entries, including the authoritative `revertedBy` relation in responses.
- Populated the reverting identity on successful mutation responses. Without that relation, the typed web boundary rejects an otherwise completed reversal as a failed result.

## Verification

- The committed workflow, authorization, catalogue-parity, and response-body coverage establishes the owner, human analyst, service-role denial, eligibility guards, and returned audit projection.
- Phase-level suite and typecheck evidence is recorded in the verification report.

## Deviations from Plan

- Reversal authority is operation-specific instead of being attached to a shared details Function, so automated service roles cannot inherit this state-changing capability.
- The authoritative success response explicitly populates `revertedBy`; persisting the relation alone was insufficient for the typed client boundary.

## Self-Check: PASSED

The request operation, transactional workflow, dedicated grant, typed contract, and regression coverage are present.
