## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: the approved stored shapes and every closed vocabulary the later operations write into, proven on a fresh database, so each later plan adds behaviour without touching the schema, the reason lists or the notification registry again.

Output: four schema files, the boot proof, the reason and error vocabulary, the notification registry entries and the harness additions.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>
