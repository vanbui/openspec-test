## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: every list, queue, lifecycle and count says whose turn it is: the business while decisions wait, the analyst once they are made, nobody once the request is closed; a decider finds their decisions waiting in "My risk assessments" and a counted Home item.

<objective>
Extend the published lifecycle with the review, acceptance and closed steps, the next actions and the holder (D-49); move the review queue and My requests' holder in the SQL and the oracle together (D-49; Phase 7 D-21, D-30); give "My risk assessments" the executive-approver part, the awaiting-acceptance stage, the viewer's pending acts and a closed awaiting filter with equal counts (D-50; UI-D13); and give the analyst list its `peer=me` filter and the ready-to-release fact (UI-D14). Change the web parsers and the lifecycle key mirror in the same plan.

Purpose: SC4's "from their Home" (the Home and the sidebar read these lists), SC5's "Closed" lifecycle end, and D-49's honest holder at every stage.
Output: the lifecycle, the queue and holder SQL with their oracles, the list repository, the analyst filter and the parsers.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No (17.1-16 changes the specs that assert the stepper's count and the Home).
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Lifecycle** (research "Lifecycle step keys"; 17.1-UI-SPEC §11.4). Keys after `assessment-drafted`: `released-for-response`, `assessment-reviewed`, `awaiting-acceptance`, then `closed` ("Closed") or `assessment-released` ("Assessment released"); `m` is 10. Facts gain `reviewCurrent` (17.1-02's currency plus the peer approval where `peerApprovalRequirement` needs it), `acceptanceRoundOpen`, the per-viewer pending act counts and `allRisksDecided`; `assessmentReviewedAndReady` is replaced by `reviewCurrent`. A request in `approved` shows `released-for-response` and `awaiting-acceptance` as `skipped` (`Not used`).
- **Next actions and holder** exactly as the 17.1-UI-SPEC §11.4 table (anchors `#review-and-submit`, `#acceptance-round`, `#your-decisions`; `{names}` per 17 UI-D22); holder `requestor` when the Requestor has a pending act, `participants` when only others do, `analyst` when every active risk is `decided`, `none` when closed.
- **Pending-act predicate** (research "Lifecycle, Holder, Queues and Lists"): over active risks of an assessment with an open acceptance round, `(acceptance_state = 'awaiting-owner' AND risk_owner = person) OR (acceptance_state = 'awaiting-executive' AND executive_approver = person)`; a person's decide count adds Critical `awaiting-executive` risks they approve, and their approve count is High `awaiting-executive` risks they approve.
- **Queue** (`selectReviewQueueBucket` and `review-queue-repository.ts`): open acceptance round with any active risk `awaiting-owner` or `awaiting-executive` → `awaiting-requestor`; open round with every active risk `decided` → `requires-attention`; `closed` → `released` (already). The equivalence fixtures gain one case per state.
- **My risk assessments** (`assessment-participation-repository.ts`): `filters[part]` gains `executive-approver`; `filters[stage]` gains `awaiting-acceptance`; `filters[awaiting]` takes `response`, `decision` or `any` (17-08's boolean awaited-response filter becomes `response`); items gain `parts` with `executive-approver`, `pendingDecideCount` and `pendingApproveCount`; `countMyRiskAssessments({ awaiting: "any" })` is the sidebar figure (UI-D13: assessments, not acts); released items and the rating summary include `closed`.
- **Analyst list** (`buildAssessmentCollectionQuery` in `assessment-scope.ts`): `filters[peer]=me` selects assessments whose `peerApprover` is the viewer with a pending ask (`peerApprovalRequestedAt` set and no valid approval); items gain `stage` `awaiting-acceptance` and `readyToReleaseAndClose`.
