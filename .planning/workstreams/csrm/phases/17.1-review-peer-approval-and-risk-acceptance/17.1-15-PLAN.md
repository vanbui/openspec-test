## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: in the browser, a risk owner opens their decisions, accepts a risk with a rationale or commits to treating it with a plan, a treatment owner found by name and a resolve date, prepares several, and records them in one confirmation; the executive approver approves or decides the exception; nobody else sees a control.

<objective>
Build the decider's surfaces on the participant page and the analyst-participant panel (D-26 to D-38, D-47, D-62, D-63; 17.1-UI-SPEC §6, §7, §5.4): the decision area (X-44) with the authority line, the recommendation, Accept and Treat with their rules, the phone sheet, preparation kept for the session, the record bar and its confirmation, High approval, Critical proposal and exception, revisions and voids, the participant round panel, the stage notices and the AI notice, and family V4.

Purpose: SC4 and SC6's "the UI shows them no decision control" in the browser; ASSESS-20 criteria 1, 2 and 5; ASSESS-24 criterion 4's "decides again".
Output: the decider's half of `features/acceptance`, three modules, the page's stage-aware parts, the refusal copy and the regenerated inventory.

Edits Strapi source? No (only `served-operations.json` is regenerated). Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (`performance.spec.ts` only).
</objective>
