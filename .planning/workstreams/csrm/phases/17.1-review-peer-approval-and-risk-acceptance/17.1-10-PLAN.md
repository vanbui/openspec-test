## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: once every risk has a valid decision, the assigned analyst releases the assessment and closes the request in one step; each risk takes its owner's decision, the next review date follows the earliest decision, and everyone taking part is told.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Body** `{ roundNumber: integer ≥ 1, lastDecisionId: string }`, closed parser (400 on anything else).
- **Order** in `withAssessmentLock` (serialise, transaction, row lock), never the editable guard (the round is open by design): `assertAssignedAnalyst`; `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`; the actor named on a risk → the G-11 refusal; no open acceptance round → 409 `RELEASE_NOT_READY` with the `acceptanceRoundOpen` key; `roundNumber` not the open round → 409 `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_CHANGED`; the newest decision row of the round not `lastDecisionId` → 409 `DECISION_CHANGED`; `projectReleaseAndCloseReadiness` over each active risk's `validDecisionOf` with its fingerprints checked against the risk as loaded → not ready → 409 `RELEASE_NOT_READY` with `details.risks: [{ riskId, riskNumber, waitingOn }]`.
- **Writes** (sequential awaits, research Pitfall 16): the assessment (`assessmentStatus: "approved"`, `approvedBy` the analyst, `approvalDate` and `assessmentDate` now, `nextReviewDate` from `nextReviewDateAtRelease`); the request `requestStatus: "closed"`; the round (`outcome: "completed"`, `closedAt`, `closedBy`); per active risk the fields from `mapDecisionToRiskFields` through an inner writer `applyDecisionToRiskInRelease` that bypasses the analyst update parser and the edit transition, with `recordRiskHistory` source `decision` (the field changes, so the recommendation stays on the record); `notify` `assessment-released` to every participant (17-12's rule, executive approvers included through the participation predicate); the `release-and-close` acting read. After the transaction, re-read and confirm with `isReleased` as today.
- **Exception fields.** The risk update refuses `isExempted` and `exemptionReviewDate` with 409 `DECISION_FIELD_NOT_WRITABLE` while the assessment is unreleased; `treatmentAction`, `treatmentPlan`, `treatmentOwner`, `treatmentResolveDate` and `treatmentStatus` stay writable as the recommendation.

The web parser follows.
- **Web caller.** `releaseAssessment(documentId, { roundNumber, lastDecisionId })` in `assessment-service.ts` and `releaseAssessmentAction(documentId, tokens)` in `assessment-actions.ts`; the editor's interim release control passes `detail.acceptanceRound.current.number` and `detail.acceptanceRound.lastDecisionId` (17.1-14 replaces the control with the round panel).
