## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: in the browser, the analyst names executive approvers, recommends a treatment without ever "accepting", watches the round's progress with editing paused, recalls with a reason the deciders will read, sees which decisions a resubmission would void, and releases and closes in one confirmed step.

Build the analyst's acceptance surfaces (D-62, D-63; 17.1-UI-SPEC §2.6, §4, §5, §9 analyst overview): the typed web layer for executive approver naming, recall and the acceptance read; the Participants section's approver coverage and sheet and the Risk form's approver field (D-15 to D-17); the Risk form's recommendation wording and the exemption fields' retirement (D-20, D-44); the round panel's analyst form with recall and release and close (D-22, D-25, D-41, D-42; X-46); the paused editor with each risk's read-only Decision group (D-23, D-31; X-45) and the between-rounds markers (D-40; X-47); families V2, V3 (recall), V5 and V6.

Purpose: SC3 and SC5 in the UI, SC6's "the UI shows them no decision control" for analysts, ASSESS-20 criterion 2's naming, ASSESS-25 criterion 1's confirmation.
Output: the analyst half of `features/acceptance`, two components, the editor's round state, the risk form's changes, the refusal copy and the regenerated inventory.

Edits Strapi source? No (only `served-operations.json` is regenerated). Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (`performance.spec.ts` only).
