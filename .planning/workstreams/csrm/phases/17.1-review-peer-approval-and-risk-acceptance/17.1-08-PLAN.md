## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: each named risk owner, and for High and Critical risks the named executive approver, records their decision through the API under the authority the rating needs; nobody else can, and nothing recorded is ever overwritten.

<objective>
Serve the decision operation (D-03 item 5) to the named people only (D-31), with Accept and Treat (D-27, D-28), the High and Critical sequences (D-29, D-30), immutable rows with fingerprints and snapshots (D-32, D-37), the cached state (D-33), recording and revisions (D-34, D-35), review dates (D-36), and the notifications (D-54, D-55). Void standing acts when a risk is withdrawn (D-39). Make executive approvers participants and publish the stage `awaiting-acceptance` (D-18, D-47, D-49). Publish, grant and prove the operation (D-02, D-59, D-68).

Purpose: SC4 in the API, SC6's "the assigned analyst, another analyst and the service identity cannot decide a risk: the API refuses", ASSESS-20 criteria 1 to 3 and 5, ASSESS-24 criterion 4's withdrawal voids.
Output: one service, one route, one Function, the participation part and stage, and the proofs.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Operation** `POST /api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/risk_decisions`, body `{ items: RiskDecisionItem[] }` (17.1-01 "Decision item"; 1–200, one item per risk), response `{ data: { applied: [{ riskId, decisionId, act, revision, acceptanceState }], unchanged: [{ riskId, decisionId }] } }`. In `withAssessmentLock` (serialise, transaction, lock), never the editable guard (decisions are allowed during the round); a released assessment answers `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`.
- **Refusal order** (research "The Risk Decision"): not a participant and not a Security Analyst → 404 (17-07's readable check); the service identity is refused before the handler by `SERVICE_IDENTITY_REFUSED_OPERATIONS` (403); a Security Analyst named on no risk of the assessment → 403 `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`, named on another risk → 403 `DECISION_NOT_PERMITTED`; a participant not named on an item's risk → 403 `DECISION_NOT_PERMITTED`; a missing risk id → 404 for the batch; then, per item, 409 `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_NOT_OPEN`, `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_CHANGED`, `RISK_WITHDRAWN`, `DECISION_NOT_AVAILABLE` (with `because` from `allowedActFor`), `DECISION_CHANGED`, `ACCEPT_NOT_AVAILABLE`; field errors 400; a treatment owner who is not an eligible person → 409 `OWNER_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Any refusal leaves persistence deep-equal.
- **Row writes.** `authority` = `acceptanceAuthority(rating)`; `reviewDate` = `reviewDateFor(rating, utcToday(now))` on the completing act only (owner decide for Low and Moderate, approve for High, executive decide for Critical); `fingerprint`, `fingerprintVersion` and `snapshot` from `decisionFingerprint` and `decisionSnapshot` of the risk as loaded in the transaction; `revision` 1, or the previous standing act's revision + 1 with `supersedes`; `approves` on an approve; `recordedAt` from the transaction's clock. The risk's `acceptanceState` from `deriveAcceptanceState` after the rows. No `update` or `delete` call on `api::csrm-risk-decision.csrm-risk-decision` exists anywhere in `apps/backend/src` (a source scan in the test).
- **Notifications.** One `decision-recorded` per batch to the assigned analyst (`{ roundNumber, actCount, allDecided }`; none while unassigned); `executive-approval-requested` to the approver for each High owner decide and Critical owner propose (a revision reads the earlier one by the `revise-high-decision` reversal and writes a new one); the actor's own round-scoped `acceptance-requested`, `decision-voided` and `executive-approval-requested` read by the `record-risk-decisions` acting read.
- **Withdrawal voids.** `withdrawRisks` (16-05) calls `voidStandingActsForWithdrawnRisk` in its transaction: one `void` row with `voidCause: "risk-withdrawn"`, `round` null, `actor` the analyst; `acceptanceState` null; no notification. Reinstatement writes no row (the next submission sets `awaiting-owner`).
- **Participation.** `PARTICIPANT_PARTS` gains `executive-approver`; `participationOf` and `whereViewerParticipates` count a person named as `executiveApprover` on an active risk, with those risks; `deriveAssessmentStage` returns `awaiting-acceptance` while an acceptance round is open. The specification's `ParticipantPart` and `AssessmentStage` enums and the web parsers gain both values.
- **Function** `function.risk_decisions.record_api` (`.api.01`), `role.requestor` only (the birthright); the literal in `SERVICE_IDENTITY_REFUSED_OPERATIONS`.
- **Scope note (the pilot's pacing rule, recorded for the plan check).** 17 files in four tasks: one operation, the participation part it needs for its 404 and 403 rules, and the chain files any API plan touches; a split would add a plan serialised on the same hot files.
