## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** have a data-driven dashboard, reports, and alerts covering my review queues, high and critical risks, and overdue treatments, **so that** I can prioritise analyst work from live workload signals.

<objective>
Serve `GET /api/v1/dashboard/summary` and build the analyst dashboard at `/app/review` (DASH-01, D-15).

The summary carries (D-34):
- the review-queue counts: the D-21 buckets, plus the questionnaire and Risk Assessment counts that match their review lists;
- open exposure by residual rating;
- the High and Critical residual Risks;
- the treatment counts;
- recent activity.

The page reads that summary, the alert summary and three bounded alert pages in parallel, so each widget fails independently. It renders the KPI strip and seven widgets from UI-SPEC §2 with the plan 05 primitives, and adds the "Analyst dashboard" navigation item.

Output:
- the dashboard summary server, with its pure activity merge and bounded activity reads;
- the published contract, regenerated client and closed parser;
- the dashboard service, presentation and components;
- the route in its own group, with its boundaries;
- the partial-failure fixture selector;
- the navigation item;
- the dashboard slice of `e2e/analyst-insights.spec.ts`.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **The route lives in a group: `app/(app)/app/review/(analyst-dashboard)/{page,loading,error}.tsx`.** A `review/loading.tsx` would wrap the three review queues and become the first loading boundary Next prefetches for every `/app/review/*` navigation, flashing the dashboard skeleton before Assessment Requests. The group keeps the UI-SPEC §1.5 intent (the dashboard has its own boundaries) and leaves the children's boundaries in charge.
- **Treatments due** (D-34). Its truncation note reads `summary.treatmentsDue.total`, the D-34 treatment counts. Its rows come from one bounded `GET /api/v1/alerts?kind=risk-treatment-overdue&kind=risk-treatment-due-soon&pageSize=5` read, which uses plan 04's repeatable `kind` filter. The integration test proves the two totals are equal.
- **Upcoming due dates** reads `?urgency=due-soon&pageSize=5`, and its note uses that page's `pagination.total`. **Top alerts** reads `?pageSize=5`, and its note uses that page's total. **The KPI urgency tiles** read `alerts/summary.byUrgency`.
- **Widget 3's footer link** is the UI-SPEC's `Open Risk Register` → `/app/risks?state=open&sort=residualRating:desc`. That list's total is all open Risks, not the widget's High/Critical total, because the register has no multi-rating filter. The UI-SPEC specifies this link, so it is kept. The browser proof for widget 3 asserts membership (each row's Risk appears in the register), not total equality. Plan 09 records the exception in the contract-gap note.
- **Recent activity has seven kinds** (UI-SPEC §2.5.1):
  - `request-submitted`: requests currently in the review scope with a `submittedDate`;
  - `request-returned-to-draft`: requests currently in the review scope with `revertedAt` (a request still in draft is outside the analyst scope, D-14);
  - `questionnaire-generated`;
  - `questionnaire-released`;
  - `questionnaire-returned-to-draft`;
  - `assessment-draft-generated`;
  - `assessment-released`, which requires the complete release triple.

  The generated kinds carry no actor, because their phrase names none. Questionnaire submission and `governanceLog` events are excluded: they have no column-level timestamp and actor, as 07-RESEARCH §Q5 found. Plan 09 records the gap. The limit is 8 (UI-SPEC §2.4).
- **The partial fixture** `__e2ePartial=analyst-top-alerts-error` is resolved by `resolveE2eAnalystDashboardPartial`, only in the attested runtime and only after the real reads succeed (UI-SPEC §6.4).
