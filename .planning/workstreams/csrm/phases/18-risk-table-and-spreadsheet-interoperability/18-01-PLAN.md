## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want** an assessment's risks in a table I can edit, copy and paste like Excel or a SharePoint list, **so that** reviewing and updating many risks takes minutes rather than a long scroll.

This plan's slice: the records every later Phase 18 plan builds on. The design direction gains X-53 to X-61, the glossary gains three terms, and three contract-gap records with five dated amendments fix every stored shape, operation, bound, reason and rule. The pilot approves them before any schema, contract or route changes.
