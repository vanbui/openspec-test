## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want** an assessment's risks in a table I can edit, copy and paste like Excel or a SharePoint list, **so that** reviewing and updating many risks takes minutes rather than a long scroll.

This plan's slice: the detail sheet. Everything the per-risk card did, from AI proposals to responses, decisions and contest points, opens from the table as a side sheet, and no single-risk form can overwrite another analyst's newer change.

Turn the per-risk card into the table's detail sheet (D-06, X-13) with every per-risk surface unchanged, its on-open read (D-27), the matrix picker in its form (D-20, D-22), bases and field conflicts on every single-risk form (D-14), the held read-only state while the table has unsaved changes to the risk (D-16, UI-D14), and the toolbar's assessment-wide actions (D-06). Update the release helper and journey spec that fill the risk form.

Purpose: D-06's per-risk surfaces move to the table before the Overview drops its cards (18-17).
Output: the sheet, the form changes, the toolbar menu and their tests.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (dev servers stopped).

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Sheet (`risk-detail-sheet.tsx`, `next/dynamic`).** `RiskDetailSheet({ assessment, risk, rows, order, pending, onClose, onNavigate, … })`: X-13 `Sheet`, 640px from 768px (bounded by the viewport), full screen below; title row h2 `{risk number}`, the statement clamped to two lines, both rating badges (with the `Overridden` note), `Previous risk` and `Next risk` (icon-only circles, `ChevronUp` and `ChevronDown`, `Alt+ArrowUp` and `Alt+ArrowDown`, in the table's current order), `History` (16-12's trigger) and `Close`; body: `RiskEditor` with the same props the Overview passes, plus the data `loadRiskSheetAction(riskId)` returns, each part behind a skeleton after 300ms. The URL's `risk` parameter opens and closes it; focus returns to the row's Risk number cell.
- **On-open read.** `loadRiskSheetAction({ assessmentId, riskId })` in `risk-table-actions.ts` calls the existing typed reads for that risk only (its control implementations, its open AI proposals, the rounds view filtered to it, its contest points) and returns them; no new operation.
- **Form changes (`risk-editor.tsx`).** Likelihood, consequence and rating fields become one `MatrixPicker` trigger per stage showing `ratingReading`; Save sends `bases` for every changed field (and the stage's rating and reason when the stage is overridden); a 409 `RISK_CHANGES_CONFLICT` marks each field with X-56's line `Yours: {value} · Theirs: {value}, saved by {name} {instant}` and `Keep mine` and `Take theirs` under it. Props `riskScale` (passed by `AssessmentEditor` and the sheet) and `heldBy: { count } | null`: when set, the form is read-only under the info alert `{n} unsaved table {change|changes} to {risk number}`, body `Save or discard them to edit this risk here.`, actions `Save changes` (saves the whole change set through 18-11's path) and `Discard changes to {risk number}`. Closing with unsaved form input shows 10 §6.3's choice inside the sheet: `Leave {risk number} without saving?`, `Discard changes`, `Keep editing` (initial focus).
- **Service and actions.** `assessment-service.ts` `updateRisk(riskId, input, bases?)` sends `{ data, bases }`; `assessment-actions.ts` and `risks/risk-actions.ts` pass bases and return `{ ok: false, reason: "RISK_CHANGES_CONFLICT", conflicts }` on 409. `risk-detail-editor.tsx` (the register's form) builds bases from the values it loaded and shows the conflict per field; its scale fields stay text inputs with family X1's field copy on refusal (D-03 publishes the scale only on the analyst assessment read).
- **Toolbar.** `More risk actions` (outline small, `Ellipsis`) in editable mode holds `Suggest more risks` (16-13's `ProposalsStartForm` in a sheet), `Set one risk owner` (17-15's `NominationSheet`), `Set one executive approver` (17.1's naming form) and `Reassess residual risk for all` (17-16's `ResidualReassessmentForm`), each with the props the Overview gives it. The cell menu gains `Open {risk number}`.
- **Specs.** `completeRiskForRelease` (`e2e/helpers/assessment-release.ts`) sets each stage through the matrix picker (`Inherent rating for {risk number}` dialog, choose the cell, `Apply rating`) instead of typing; `assessment-release-workflow.spec.ts`'s direct likelihood fills do the same. The Overview still shows the cards until 18-17.
