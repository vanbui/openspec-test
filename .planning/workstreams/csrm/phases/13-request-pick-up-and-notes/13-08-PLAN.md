## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** pick up a request and exchange notes with the Requestor on it, **so that** ownership is clear and new information has a home at any point.

This plan's slice: every Security Analyst on a request can follow and retry its generation jobs and sees who started them, so handing a request to a colleague never strands its work.

<objective>
Widen the generation-job reads and retries to every human analyst who may read the request, change `canRetry`'s meaning, attribute a retry to the retrying analyst, project the initiator's name, declare the job id bounds and update the catalogue descriptions (D-18); change the two job cards (UI-SPEC §13); record R-08-36's line (D-19); and create the pick-up journey spec with its first test, a colleague following Bob's job.

Purpose: REQ-08 criterion 3 and success criterion 2's second half; UAT-014's job-visibility half and UAT-041.
Output: widened access end to end, two cards, one register line, one new spec.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? Yes. Regenerates `served-operations.json`? No (no route or grant changes).
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Access.** The questionnaire workflow's `actor()` and the draft workflow's `draftActor()` take `{ documentId, humanAnalyst }` from the controller (`ctx.state.idamboxUser.documentId`, `ctx.state.csrmAuthorization.humanAnalyst`) and pass `canReadAllQuestionnaireGenerationJobs` and `canReadAllDraftJobs` equal to `humanAnalyst`, never a constant, so a later route reusing a workflow cannot widen by accident.
- **canRetry.** In `latestForRequest` and `latestForQuestionnaire`, `canRetry = job.status === "failed" && viewerMayRetry` with `viewerMayRetry` the `humanAnalyst` fact. Both job components gain `canRetry: boolean` (the same rule on the by-id reads), `initiatedBy: ActiveUserSummary | null` and `initiatedByViewer: boolean`, all three required: the flat `QuestionnaireGenerationJob` once, and `AssessmentDraftJob`, a `oneOf`, in each of its four variants (`AssessmentDraftJobQueued`, `AssessmentDraftJobRunning`, `AssessmentDraftJobCompleted`, `AssessmentDraftJobFailed`). The generation-jobs read's wrapper `canRetry` keeps the same value for 10-13's parser, and its spec description (today "Whether the viewing analyst initiated the job …") is reworded to the new meaning.
- **Attribution.** Both registries' `retry(jobId, actor)` set `job.initiatingAnalystDocumentId = actor.documentId` before `launch`, so `execute` writes the retrier as `generatedBy` and Phase 12's new run names them as `initiatedBy`.
- **Name.** The controllers resolve the initiator with `resolvePeople` (13-03) at projection time, one bounded read per response; the registry keeps only the documentId.
- **Bounds.** `jobId` path parameters on `/v1/questionnaire_generation_jobs/{jobId}`, its retry, and (through the `AssessmentDraftJobId` parameter component) `/v1/assessment_draft_jobs/{jobId}` and its retry declare `minLength: 1, maxLength: 255`. The job components declare every bound the web job parsers in `assessment-schema.ts` enforce: `minLength: 1, maxLength: 255` on `jobId`, `requestId`, `providerName`, `providerVersion` and `failureCode` of `QuestionnaireGenerationJob`, on `jobId` and `questionnaireId` of all four `AssessmentDraftJob` variants, on `assessmentId` of `AssessmentDraftJobCompleted` and on `failureCode` of `AssessmentDraftJobFailed`; and `minLength: 1, maxLength: 2000` on `failureMessage` of `QuestionnaireGenerationJob` and `AssessmentDraftJobFailed` (the server's `nonBlank` and the web's `documentIdSchema` and `max(2_000)`). The flat questionnaire job's per-state fields (`startedAt`, `completedAt`, `failureCode`, `failureMessage`, `questionnaire`) stay narrowed under their existing union-restructure reason, which is not Phase 13's. Task 1's `initiatedBy` parser adds no bound that `ActiveUserSummary` does not declare, since `getRequestGenerationJobs` is held to the boundary modes. With the bounds declared, the six `narrowedPaths` entries citing "D-27, Phase 13" in `getRequestGenerationJobs`'s registration (`assessment-service.ts`: `data.questionnaireGeneration.job.jobId`, `.requestId`, `.providerName`, `.providerVersion`, `data.assessmentDraft.job.jobId`, `.questionnaireId`) and their comment are deleted, `getAssessmentDraftJob` and `getQuestionnaireGenerationJob` leave `boundaryGrandfathered` in the parity test, and the parity test's backlog count falls by exactly two (41 to 39 at HEAD `7d889a3`), with a sentence in its comment naming this plan.
- **Catalogue.** In both copies: `Returns an initiating analyst's retained questionnaire generation job.` becomes `Returns a retained questionnaire generation job.`, and `Retries an initiating analyst's failed questionnaire generation job.` becomes `Retries a failed questionnaire generation job.`; the draft job FunctionAPIs' descriptions lose the same words if 12-10 left them. No `roleIds` change.
- **Cards** (UI-SPEC §13): `Started by {name}.` replaces `Started by another Security Analyst.` whenever `initiatedByViewer` is false (queued, running or failed); a failed job offers `Retry questionnaire generation` or `Retry draft generation` whenever `canRetry` is true; `Only the Security Analyst who started it can retry it.` is removed; after the viewer's own retry the card shows no `Started by` line. Once `canRetry` means "failed and this viewer may retry it", it no longer says who started the job, so both cards key the `Started by` line and the polling route on the job's `initiatedByViewer`, never on `canRetry`: `byRequest` becomes `job.initiatedByViewer !== true` (in place of `canRetry !== true` at `questionnaire-generation-job.tsx` and `assessment-draft-job.tsx`), so a job the viewer started keeps polling its by-id read and a colleague's keeps polling the request's jobs read, as today; and the by-id poll result takes `canRetry` from the served job instead of the hard-coded `canRetry: true`. The `Attempt {n}` tag is unchanged.
- **Register.** R-08-36's row gains: `Phase 13 widened job access to every human Security Analyst who may read the request; storage unchanged (D-19).` Its trigger column is unchanged.
- **E2E.** New `apps/csrm/e2e/request-pick-up.spec.ts`, the spec later plans extend: test `REQ08_COLLEAGUE_FOLLOWS_JOB @journey`. The mobile project (`apps/csrm/playwright.config.ts`) runs only the specs in its `testMatch` allowlist and only tests whose title matches `grep: /@journey/`, so this plan adds `'**/request-pick-up.spec.ts'` to that `testMatch` (the `grep` stays), and every test in the spec that is meant to run at 375, this one and those later plans add, carries `@journey` in its title.
