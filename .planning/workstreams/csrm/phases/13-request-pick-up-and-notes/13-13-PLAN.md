## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** pick up a request and exchange notes with the Requestor on it, **so that** ownership is clear and new information has a home at any point.

This plan's slice: an analyst opens `Assigned to me` to see exactly the work they hold, the sidebar tells them how much of it needs them and how much is waiting for pick-up, and quick find says who holds each request.

Purpose: success criterion 2's queue view; success criterion 1 "everywhere it appears" for quick find.
Output: one route with its three files, two navigation counts, one quick-find line, journey steps.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes.
</objective>
