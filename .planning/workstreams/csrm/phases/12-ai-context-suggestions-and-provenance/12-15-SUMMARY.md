# Phase 12 Plan 15 Summary

Executed by the pilot while the execution tool's 5-hour window cooled.

## Accomplishments

- `assessmentProvenanceHeaderProps` and `questionnaireProvenanceHeaderProps` derive the header on the server: who asked, when, provider and model, and line 3 (the outstanding count linked to `#assessment-details` or the first Risk in editor order, `All AI proposals reviewed`, the questionnaire's origin counts in analyst review, or the pre-Phase 12 sentence). Released records omit line 3 and keep the trigger.
- The page derives the header props on the server and passes them to `AssessmentEditor` and `QuestionnaireReview` as `provenance`; the header (no `use client`, no hooks) renders inside their client trees, below the request link (and the stepper) and above every alert and panel. Both skeletons gain a header-height block.
- `AiRunSheetTrigger` loads `AiRunSheet` with a plain `import()` held in state, started on pointer enter, focus or press. The sheet reads the run per open, shows nothing for 300 ms then a skeleton, and renders the populated groups (Run, Data policy, Request, Questions and answers, Information systems, Information assets, Risk Types, Control candidates, Context digest), the empty (404 or no summary), permission (403) and error (Try again) states. Focus goes to the title on open and back to the trigger on close; Escape closes. Below 768 px it fills the screen with a visible Close; from 768 px it is the 640 px side sheet with a hairline start edge, sliding in only when motion is allowed.
- The released view's `Generated by` row is gone; `AiAssistanceNotice` opens the overview whenever the assessment was AI-assisted. The released-for-actor parser now keeps `aiAssisted` (served for a Requestor, derived from `generation` for an analyst).
- The copy guard reads every AI assistance component and rejects `provenance`, `context summary` and `ceiling` in their copy.

## Decisions

- **Budget (measured).** The editor route measured 295,661 bytes at HEAD before this plan. Rendering the header on the server made its sheet trigger a new client reference, and the extra chunk group duplicated shared code: 313,342 bytes. Neither `next/dynamic` nor a plain `import()` changed that (313,352 and 313,556); without the trigger the route measured 295,697. The header therefore renders in the editor's and the questionnaire review's existing client trees, and the header and sheet helpers moved to `ai-provenance-domain.ts` so the policy helpers' refusal copy stays out of the editor (301,000 to 300,470). This departs from the plan's server slot and from `dynamic(` in the trigger; the header still has no hooks and no `use client`.
- The run summary names request fields by key (`request.title`); the sheet shows the request form's labels through `requestFieldLabel`.
- `run record` is not a guarded term: the UI-SPEC's own empty state is `No AI run record`. The three other terms are guarded only in the AI assistance components, because other modules hold closed reason codes such as `AI_CLASSIFICATION_ABOVE_CEILING` as string literals.
- The sheet is built on the Radix dialog directly rather than the shipped `SheetContent`, whose fixed `w-3/4 sm:max-w-sm` side classes and corner close button do not fit X-13's full-screen phone layout and visible Close.
- The trigger is 44 px tall below 768 px (touch target) and the small button size from 768 px.
- The skeleton block is in `AssessmentEditorSkeleton`, which both the route's `loading.tsx` and its Suspense fallback render.
