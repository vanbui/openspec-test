## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** have the AI work from everything the Requestor gave and show where each proposal came from, **so that** I can trust, check and correct its output quickly.

This plan's slice: before asking, the analyst sees how the request's information is classified and whether it may go to the AI; the hub never offers a generation the policy would refuse.

<objective>
Add the request's AI policy status to Phase 10's generation-jobs read (D-29) and render it on both job cards: the allowed caption, the two refusal alerts in place of the actions, the three new failure codes and the draft card's new running copy (UI-SPEC §11).

Purpose: INT-05 criterion 4's "plain message" at the point of asking, and UX-06 criterion 1 (no action the server will refuse).
Output: one contract field end to end, the zod-free policy presentation, both cards' states, tests, and the changed generation journey.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? Yes (`questionnaire-generation-workflow.spec.ts`).
</objective>
