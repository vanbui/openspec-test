## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst or risk manager, **I want to** export any list I can see and to import spreadsheets without fighting the format, **so that** CSRM fits the Excel work around it.

This plan's slice: the request lists export. Bob exports his review queue as it stands, with its assignee column and filters; anyone exports their own requests and never someone else's.

The pilot's pacing rule (planner-context) accepts this.
Serve the Requests export and the My requests export (D-03 group 1, D-04) as thin adapters over the 19-04 pipeline: export variants of the queue's and My requests' own parsers, two views in the registry, routes beside their lists (the Requests export before any parametrised request route), the policy members and underlying reads (D-13), the contract chain and both new Functions in the catalogue (D-02), and the API proofs of every grant and refusal with the matrix rows (D-16, D-38).

Purpose: the request family exports what its lists show, including Phase 13's assignee and "Assigned to me" view, with no export work when those lists change (D-05).
Output: two served operations with their chain and proofs.
