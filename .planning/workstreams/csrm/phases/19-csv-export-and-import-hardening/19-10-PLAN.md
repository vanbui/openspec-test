## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst or risk manager, **I want to** export any list I can see and to import spreadsheets without fighting the format, **so that** CSRM fits the Excel work around it.

This plan's slice: the released Risk Assessment leaves CSRM as a file, through one record-read check. Jane exports her own released assessment; anyone else gets the not-found refusal; the analyst exports it once released; the assessment lists export too.

<objective>
Gather the released-assessment read into one module (D-14) and switch the three released reads and the My risk assessments list to it with no behaviour change; serve the released-assessment export for both actors (D-03 group 2, D-15, D-21) through the fixed-table pipeline; serve the Risk assessments and My risk assessments exports (D-03 group 1, D-04); publish, grant, inventory and prove every grant and refusal (D-02, D-13, D-16, D-38). Proves ROADMAP criterion 2 in the API.

Purpose: one record-read check that Phase 17 widens to participants, 17.1 to `closed` requests and 19.1 composes for notification readability, so exports, the print report and My risk assessments follow with no export change (G-12, G-13; orchestrator constraint).
Output: the predicate module, three served operations and their proofs.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.

Scope note: 27 files at four tasks.
