## Phase Goal

**As an** Admin, **I want to** own reference data and product configuration, separate from analysts' assessment work, **so that** registers, lists and scales are governed.

This plan's slice: a change applies forward. An open acceptance round keeps the criteria it opened under, the next submission uses the new ones, and readiness says what a change makes outstanding.

Prove forward application in the API and guard the round modules statically.

Purpose: D-11's "changes apply forward", the seam 17.1-RESEARCH left open for Phase 22.
Output: the resolver, the changed 17.1 services and projections, the contract and web parser, and three test files.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Resolver (`round-criteria.ts`).** `currentCriteria(now)` builds `RoundCriteriaV1` from `riskBands()`, `riskAppetite()`, `reviewPolicy()` and each key's `storedSetting(key)?.version ?? 0`; `criteriaFor(round)`: an acceptance round's `criteria` through `parseRoundCriteria` (null → `DEFAULT_ROUND_CRITERIA`; malformed throws, logged once, failing the act closed); a response round has none (callers never ask).
- **Capture once.** `submitFinalAssessment` calls `currentCriteria(now)` once at the start of its transaction, passes it to its readiness re-check and its initial `acceptanceState` and notification decisions, and writes it as the round's `criteria`.
- **In-round readers.** `recordRiskDecisions`, `deriveAcceptanceState` and validity in `decision-sequence.ts`, the completing act's `reviewDateFor`, release-and-close readiness and the round branches of the participant and analyst projections take `criteria.appetite` (and `criteria.scale.bands` where a rating is derived) as their explicit argument; the decision's treatment rule (17.1 D-27: an Accept rationale says why treatment is not practicable) keys on `treatmentExpected(rating, criteria.appetite)` instead of the rating. Release keeps reading the completing rows' stored `authority` and `reviewDate`.
- **Readiness (`acceptance/readiness.ts`).** Risk-scope key `executiveApproverNotNeeded` (label `No executive approver needed`) when a risk names an executive approver that `executiveApproverRequired(residualRating)` under the current appetite no longer needs; each item gains `follows`: `risk-appetite` on `executiveApprover` and `executiveApproverNotNeeded`, `rating-bands` on 18's ratings-follow-the-scale key, `peer-review` on `peerApproval`, else null.
- **Projections.** `AcceptanceRoundSummary` gains `criteria: { takenAt, scaleVersion, appetiteVersion, reviewPolicyVersion } | null` and `criteriaDifferFromCurrent` (`criteriaDiffer(criteriaFor(round), currentCriteria(now))` while open, false once closed); `RiskAuthority` gains `treatmentExpected`; the spec enums gain the readiness key and `follows`; `assessment-schema.ts` parses them.
- **Static guard (`round-criteria-guard.unit.test.ts`).** Reads `risk-decisions.ts`, `acceptance/decision-sequence.ts` and `acceptance-projection.ts` and fails on a zero-argument `riskAppetite()`, `reviewPolicy()` or `riskBands()`, or a one-argument `acceptanceAuthority(`, `reviewIntervalMonths(`, `isException(`, `executiveApproverRequired(` or `treatmentExpected(` call (comments excluded), mirroring 17.1's lock-inventory test.
