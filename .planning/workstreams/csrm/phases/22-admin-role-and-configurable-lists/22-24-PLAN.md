**As an** Admin, **I want to** own reference data and product configuration, separate from analysts' assessment work, **so that** registers, lists and scales are governed.

This plan's slice: when the Admin's AI data policy refuses a request, the assigned analyst starts the work by hand from the same card, and everyone reads honestly how the record began.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Service and actions.** `assessment-service.ts`: `startQuestionnaireWithoutAi(requestId)`, `startAssessmentWithoutAi(questionnaireId)`, registered for parity; `assessment-actions.ts`: `startQuestionnaireWithoutAiAction`, `startAssessmentWithoutAiAction` returning the new record's id and route, or the refusal.
- **Component (`manual-start.tsx`).** `ManualStart({ kind, state, requestNumber })`: with `state.viewerMayStart`, the outline pill `Start without AI` (`PenLine`) and, for a failed or interrupted run, the line `Or start without AI and write the {questionnaire|draft} yourself.`; otherwise the line `{name}, the assigned Security Analyst, can start it without AI.`, `Once a Security Analyst picks up this request, they can start it without AI.` (with 13's `Pick up` as today) or family K's `OWN_REQUEST` line; nothing when `available` is false. The confirmation per UI-SPEC §11.2 at the confirm size, the served reason sentence by code, `Start without AI` (primary) and `Cancel`, waiting for the server; success navigates to the editor and toasts.
- **Cards.** Both job cards render `ManualStart` after their refusal or failure alert from `aiPolicy.manualStart`.
- **Header.** `AiProvenanceHeader` renders the manual variant from `generation.manualStart`: `Started without AI by {name} · {instant}` with a 14px `PenLine`, then `Reason: {served reason}` in `--text-caption` / `--color-text-secondary`; X-11's AI parts only once an AI proposal is confirmed.
- **Empty states.** `questionnaire-governance.tsx`: `No questions yet` · `Add the questions the Requestor will answer.` with the existing add action; `risk-table.tsx`: `No risks yet` · `Add the risks this assessment covers.` with the existing add action.
- **Copy.** `aiPolicyRefusalCopy` replaces ` Contact your administrator.` with ` The Admin sets this limit in the AI data policy.`; Phase 14's inclusion refusal line gains the same sentence; family AA4 per UI-SPEC §12.
