## Recorded planning decisions

- **Routes.** Server Components; `generateMetadata` titles `Role holders | IdamBox CSRM` and `Change log | IdamBox CSRM`; `can('access-review:read', granted)` before any read, else `PermissionDeniedState` with UI-SPEC §2's access-review copy; a Strapi 403 renders the same; filters kept in the address (`role`, `status`, `notes`; `area`, `subject`, `changedBy`, `from`, `to`, `batch`, `page`, `pageSize`).
- **Service and schema.** `access-review-service.ts` (`import 'server-only'`): `listRoleHolders(query)`, `listConfigurationChanges(query)`, `exportRoleHolders(query, format)`, `exportConfigurationChanges(query, format)`, each registered in `contract-response-parity.test.ts`; `access-review-schema.ts` closed zod parsers for both responses; `export-actions.ts` gains `exportRoleHoldersAction` and `exportChangeLogAction`; the served inventory lists the web callers (`SERVED_OPERATIONS_WRITE=1 pnpm --dir apps/backend served-operations:write`).
- **Domain (`access-review-domain.ts`, zod-free).** `changedByLabel(changedBy)` (`{name}`, `Identity platform · {operator}`, `CSRM · {source label}` with `Seed data` and `Unattributed`), `changeCellLines(fields)` (up to three `{field}: {value}` lines then `and {k} more`; null reads `Not set`), `changeAreaLabel`, `changeActionLabel`, `heldSinceLine(role)`, `reviewNoteText(note)` (X-79's three sentences), `subjectHref(change)` (the setting page, the reference record, `/app/admin/divisions`, `/app/admin/shared-views`, or role holders filtered to the person; null when `subjectExists` is false).
- **Components.** `RoleHolders` (UI-SPEC §9 order; notes as 12px `CircleAlert` lines); `ChangeLog` (DataTable with sticky `When`, the details sheet titled `Change on {instant}` with the definition list and the full `Field`, `Before`, `After` table and `Show this import’s changes`); `ChangeHistorySheet({ subjectType, subjectRef, title })` (X-29 over the filtered read, entries in X-78's form, `Open in the change log`); `AdministrationHomeSection` (UI-SPEC §3's three rows; `No review notes`, `No changes recorded yet`; `We couldn’t load the administration summary` · `Try again`).
- **Home.** `requestor-home-service.ts` loads the section's two reads (`notes=any&pageSize=10` total; `pageSize=10` newest change) only when the viewer holds `access-review:read`; `requestor-home.tsx` renders the section after `Needs your action`.
