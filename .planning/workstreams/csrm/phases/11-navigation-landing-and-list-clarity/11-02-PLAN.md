## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** have a sectioned sidebar and a Home that leads with my next action, **so that** I always know where my work is and what to do next.

This plan's slice: every actor lands on one Home at `/app`; the Security Analyst's Home is the review summary they used to find at `/app/review`, which now redirects.

<objective>
Make `/app` Home for both actors (D-16): the page reads the session's capabilities, renders the analyst Home (today's analyst dashboard content, moved) for `assessment:review` and the Requestor Home (today's Requestor dashboard flow, rebuilt in plan 11-12) otherwise, before any data read. Retire the `(analyst-dashboard)` route group and redirect `/app/review` to `/app` with a 307 from `next.config.ts` (D-17). Rename the heading, title and navigation item to Home, drop the Analyst dashboard item, and move every E2E sign-in, route list and fixture test to the new route (D-57, UI-SPEC §14.1 E1 to E5).

Purpose: UAT-001 and UX-08 criterion 1: the analyst landed on their personal requests, not on their queue.
Output: the Home page and its boundaries, three `features/home` modules, the redirect and its unit test, the changed navigation item, the sign-in helpers and guard, the new `navigation-and-home.spec.ts`, and the route assertions in five specs.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes: the new spec, then one full run, because every journey signs in through the helper this plan changes. Alone in wave 2 (D-05).
</objective>
