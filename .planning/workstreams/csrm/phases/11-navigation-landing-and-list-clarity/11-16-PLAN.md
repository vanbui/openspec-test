## Phase Goal

This plan's slice: the analyst Home's exposure figures count released Risks, show Risks still under assessment apart, mark accepted Risks, and say so in words.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Server.** `openReleasedRisks(connection) = applyReleasedParent(from csrm_risks r).whereRaw("r.closure_date IS NULL")` and `openUnderAssessmentRisks(connection)` likewise; `released` groups the first by `residual_rating`, `releasedAccepted` adds `where('r.treatment_action', 'Accept')`, `underAssessment` groups the second; `ratingCounts` maps each (null or off-ladder → `notRated`). `openHighCriticalRisks` reads `openReleasedRisks`. `openContextualRisks` is renamed `openDisclosableRisks` and kept only where a widget still needs both stages.
- **Seed.** The new Risk uses the released assessment of the existing fixture, residual rating `Moderate`, `treatmentAction: 'Accept'`, no closure date, a statement starting `Accepted fixture:`; the seed stays idempotent (keyed like its siblings).
- **BarChart (UI-SPEC §9.2, X-10).** The `labelled-values` datum gains optional `secondary?: { href: string; label: string; accessibleName: string }`, rendered after the row link inside the same list item as a text link with the record-link treatment; it sits on the row's line from the widget's `@md` container size and wraps beneath the count below it. Existing callers pass nothing and render as today.
- **Copy (verbatim, UI-SPEC §9.2, §9.4, §11.1).** Risk exposure description `Released Risks only. Open Risks by residual rating, with accepted Risks marked.`; list `Released open Risks by residual rating`; empty `No released open Risks` · `Risks count here once their risk assessment is released.`; under-assessment row `Under assessment` named `Under assessment: {n} open {Risk|Risks} not yet released` with caption `Not yet released, so not counted above.`; High and Critical description `Released open Risks rated High or Critical after treatment, highest first.` and empty `No released High or Critical residual Risks` · `Released open Risks rated High or Critical after treatment appear here.`; footer `View Critical Risks`, `View High Risks`; Treatments due `Risk treatments past their target date or due within {window}, most overdue first. Includes Risks under assessment.`; Upcoming due dates `Due, target and review dates in the next {window}, soonest first. Includes Risks under assessment.`; Top alerts `The most urgent alerts across your scope, including Risks under assessment.`; the attention tile's supporting line `Untreated Critical Risks and ineffective mitigation controls` (UI-D33).
