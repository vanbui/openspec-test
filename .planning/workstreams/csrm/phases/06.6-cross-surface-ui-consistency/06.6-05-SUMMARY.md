# Phase 06.6 Plan 05 Summary

**Analyst questionnaire review, the four Administration collections and the Risk Register now share the record-link, table and state contract; the Risk Register keeps its identifier visible while scrolling.**

## Accomplishments

- Analyst questionnaire review moved onto DataTable with RecordLink identifiers and titles, shared states, and DataTable footer pagination, retiring the interim hand-rolled Previous/Next buttons.
- Administration collection lists open records through RecordLink and render empty, error and permission states through the shared primitives; the four route pages were updated for the renamed permission wrappers.
- The Risk Register opens risks and related assessments through RecordLink, renders its states through the shared primitives, and sets `stickyFirstColumn`.
