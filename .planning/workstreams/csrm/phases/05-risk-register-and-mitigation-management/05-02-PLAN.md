## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** search, filter, sort, and maintain contextual Risks and their mitigation-control implementations in an organisation-level register, **so that** I can track and manage risk exposure across all assessments.


<objective>
Deliver the authenticated, bounded, server-authoritative Risk Register read as the first real vertical API slice.

Purpose: RISK-01 cannot be safely rendered until filtering, global ordering, totals, and analyst-only scope are authoritative before pagination.
Output: Mounted GET `/api/v1/risks` with closed projection and exhaustive backend tests.
</objective>

### Contract Gap

RISK-01 and the roadmap broadly mention date filtering, but the later locked Phase 5 matrix and binding UI contract intentionally contain no date parameters. This plan covers the complete user-authorized Phase 5 contract. The upstream wording remains unresolved product-contract debt documented in `docs/contract-gaps/phase-05-risk-register-date-filter.md`; executors must not add a date filter.
