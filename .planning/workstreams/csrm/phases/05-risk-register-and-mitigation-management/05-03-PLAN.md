## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** search, filter, sort, and maintain contextual Risks and their mitigation-control implementations in an organisation-level register, **so that** I can track and manage risk exposure across all assessments.


<objective>
Freeze the list operation across OpenAPI, catalogue parity, generated types, closed web schemas, and server-only transport.

Purpose: Give the UI a typed, hostile-input-resistant contract without raw fetch or hand-edited generated declarations.
Output: Generated list client plus validated Risk Register query/response services.
</objective>

### Contract Gap

RISK-01's broad date-filter wording conflicts with the later locked Phase 5 matrix and binding UI contract, which intentionally omit date parameters. Preserve RISK-01 traceability as full coverage of the user-authorized contract and keep the unresolved upstream debt explicit in `docs/contract-gaps/phase-05-risk-register-date-filter.md`; do not add date-range OpenAPI or URL parameters.
