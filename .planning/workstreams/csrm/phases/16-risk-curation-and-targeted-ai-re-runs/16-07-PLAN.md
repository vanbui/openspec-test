## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** withdraw wrong risks and ask the AI to rewrite one risk or propose missing ones, **so that** the draft ends up complete and correct without rebuilding it by hand.

This plan's slice: withdrawn risks leave the Risk Register's default view and every insight figure, are reachable only under an explicit `Withdrawn` filter, and a static test fails if any future reader forgets the rule.

<objective>
Apply the active-risk rule's SQL form (D-13) to the Risk Register list and filters, its detail (the `withdrawal` block), the dashboard summary, the alerts, the reports and the exposure figures; add the closed `withdrawn` value to the register's `state` filter (D-08, UI-D9); change the contract and the web parser together (D-02); and add the static reader guard that turns D-14's "later phases read the same rule" into a failing test.

Purpose: success criterion 1 across registers and figures, and a guard Phases 17 to 21 inherit (D-14).
Output: the readers changed, the contract and parser widened, mounted proofs per figure and the static guard.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **SQL helpers.** In `risk-register.ts`, beside `applyDisclosableParent`: `applyActiveRisk(query)` adds `whereRaw(activeRiskSql("r"))`; `applyWithdrawnRisk(query)` adds `whereRaw("r.withdrawn_at IS NOT NULL")`. Both keep the outer alias `r`.
- **Register filter.** `RISK_REGISTER_STATES` becomes `["open", "closed", "withdrawn"]`. Absent state → `applyActiveRisk`; `open` → active and `closure_date IS NULL`; `closed` → active and `closure_date IS NOT NULL`; `withdrawn` → `applyWithdrawnRisk` (the disclosable parent rule still applies). Every count and page total of the list follows the same branch.
- **Register detail.** `getRiskRegisterDetail` populates `withdrawnBy` and projects `withdrawal` exactly as `projectAnalystRiskDetail` does (null when active); `parentLock` keeps its two values.
- **Insights.** Every query in `dashboard-summary.ts`, `alert-repository.ts` and `report-repository.ts` that starts from `csrm_risks as r` with `applyDisclosableParent` gains `applyActiveRisk` in the same chain, including the exposure, under-assessment and accepted figures and the treatment alerts.
- **Spec and web.** The `/v1/risks` `filters[state]` enum gains `withdrawn`; `RiskDetail.withdrawal` (required, `RiskWithdrawal` or null, the 16-06 component). Web: `risk-schema.ts`'s state enum and the detail parser's `withdrawal: riskWithdrawalSchema.nullable()` (imported from `assessment-schema.ts`); the parity guard registers both.
- **Static guard.** `active-risk-readers.unit.test.ts` reads every `.ts` file under `apps/backend/src` and finds reader sites: a line matching `/csrm_risks(\s+as\s+\w+)?["'`]/` in a `from(` or `join(` call, and a `.findMany(`, `.findFirst(` or `.count(` on a binding of `documents(<risk uid or alias>)` or `db.query(<risk uid or alias>)` (aliases resolved per file, as 16-04's no-delete guard does). Each site passes when one of `activeRiskSql`, `applyActiveRisk`, `applyWithdrawnRisk`, `ACTIVE_RISK_FILTER`, `WITHDRAWN_RISK_FILTER`, `activeRisks` or `isActiveRisk` appears within the next 25 lines, or when the file and site are in `EXEMPT_READERS` (a map of `path → reason`), seeded with: `risk-number.ts` (numbers are never reused, so allocation counts withdrawn risks, D-06), `risk-curation.ts` (loads named risks by id to withdraw or reinstate them), `risk-history.ts` (history rows, not risks), the seed modules (create fixtures), and any loader that lists every risk for the analyst detail by design (D-08), each with its reason. A synthetic-source case proves a reader without the rule is flagged and the same reader with `activeRiskSql` passes.
