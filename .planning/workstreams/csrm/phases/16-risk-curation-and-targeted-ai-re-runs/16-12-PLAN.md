## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** withdraw wrong risks and ask the AI to rewrite one risk or propose missing ones, **so that** the draft ends up complete and correct without rebuilding it by hand.

This plan's slice: the interface pieces for withdrawal and history, built for reuse by Phase 18's table: the card actions, the withdraw and reinstate form with Undo, the withdrawn card, the history sheet, the `risk` status family, and X-24's comparison extended for decisions and history.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Status family.** `statusPresentation.risk = { withdrawn: { label: "Withdrawn", tone: "neutral", icon: Archive } }`; nothing else changes in the mapping.
- **Card actions.** `RiskCardActions({ riskId, riskNumber, rewrite, onHistory, onWithdraw, requestingAnalyst })`: from 768px, small pills in this order: `rewrite.trigger` (when given), `History` (ghost, `History` icon, named `History of {risk number}`), `Withdraw risk` (ghost, `Archive`, named `Withdraw risk {risk number}`), each with `data-print="hidden"`; below 768px one circular icon button (`EllipsisVertical`, named `Actions for {risk number}`, `aria-haspopup="menu"`) opening a `DropdownMenu` with the same items at the touch-target height, where `Rewrite this risk` calls `rewrite.open`. `requestingAnalyst` drops the rewrite item.
- **Form.** `WithdrawRiskForm({ mode, riskId, riskNumber, openChanges, hasUnsavedChanges, draftOwnerKey, onSubmit, onKeep })`: an X-5 well from 768px and a bottom `Sheet` with a title row and `Close` below; title `Withdraw {risk number}` or `Reinstate {risk number}`; §6.1 and §6.2 descriptions (with the open-changes sentence, or the requesting analyst's variant when `openChanges` is null); `Input` labelled `Reason for withdrawing` or `Reason for reinstating`, required, with its hint and `{n} of 500 characters` once typed; submit-time errors `Give a reason for withdrawing this risk.`, `Give a reason for reinstating this risk.` and `Keep the reason to 500 characters or fewer. It has {n}.`; actions `Withdraw risk` or `Reinstate risk` (outline, small; `Enter` in the field submits) and `Keep risk` or `Keep withdrawn` (ghost, small; `Escape` too); with `hasUnsavedChanges`, the line `This risk has unsaved changes. Save or discard them before you withdraw it.` replaces the field and submit; the reason is written to the session draft store (`risk-withdraw-reason` or `risk-reinstate-reason`, per person and risk), restored with `Restored from earlier in this session.`, cleared on success, and a `beforeunload` guard holds while it has text.
- **Hook.** `useRiskWithdrawal({ assessmentId })` returns `{ pending, withdraw(risk, reason), reinstate(risk, reason) }`: it moves the card at once (the §5 pending opacity, no transition), calls the action, shows `{risk number} withdrawn.` or `{risk number} reinstated.` with `Undo` (X-14; `Alt+T`), whose success shows `Withdrawal undone. {risk number} is back in the assessment.` or `Reinstatement undone. {risk number} is withdrawn again.`; on failure it rolls back with the `--duration-base` opacity transition, reopens the form with the reason and focus in the field and R1's line as the form error, or shows a danger toast when the form cannot reopen; focus moves as §6.1 and §6.2 say.
- **Withdrawn card.** `WithdrawnRiskCard({ risk, editable, onReinstate, onHistory, details })` per §6.4 and X-27: `id="risk-{id}"`, `tabIndex={-1}`, `--color-bg-surface`, `--color-border-default`, `--radius-lg`, 16px padding; header with the risk number, the `Withdrawn` badge and the residual `RiskRatingBadge`; statement clamped to two lines (`[overflow-wrap:anywhere]`, full text in the DOM); `Reason: “{reason}”` (`--text-body`) and `Withdrawn by {name} · {instant}` (`LocalInstant`); actions `Reinstate risk` (outline, `ArchiveRestore`, editable only), `History` and `Show details` / `Hide details` (`aria-expanded`) revealing `details` (the shared read-only Risk and its controls).
- **History sheet.** `RiskHistoryTrigger` (client) loads `RiskHistorySheet` with `next/dynamic`; the sheet calls `readRiskHistoryAction` on open; title (h2) `History of {risk number}`, description `Every change to this risk and its mitigation controls, newest first.`, count `{shown} of {total} {change|changes}`, density control from 768px persisted per person in the browser (the pattern Phase 15's panel uses), entries as an `ol` with `historyEntryPresentation`, each comparison a read-only `FieldComparison` with `Before` and `After` and no decision column (collapsed behind `Show {n} changed {field|fields}` in compact); `Show older changes` loads 25 more, keeps the scroll position and focuses the first new entry; the honest start from `honestStartLine(recordedFrom)` on the last page when no `Risk added` entry is listed; states per §8.5 (skeleton after 300 ms; `No changes recorded yet`; error with `Try again`; the role-free 403 copy; the 404 line); focus to the title on open and back to the trigger on close; full screen below 768px with `Close history`.
- **Comparison extension.** `FieldComparison` keeps its executed props, `caption`, `density`, `renderEmphasis` and `rows` (field-comparison.tsx, `FieldComparisonProps`), with their behaviour unchanged, so every Phase 15 call site compiles and renders as today; the new props are additions to these. `rows` stays the way Phase 15 callers pass rows, and `groups` is the alternative for grouped comparisons (type the props so a caller passes `rows` or `groups`, never neither); `renderEmphasis`, when given, still decides a value's rendering, and `emphasis` applies only where it is absent. It gains optional props `columns` (`{ before, after }` labels; default `Current` and `Proposed`), `groups` (each `{ key, label, rows, decision?: ReactNode }` rendered as a `tbody` whose final cell, under a visually hidden `Decision` header, spans the group's rows and is top-aligned), `showSame` (matching rows read `Same` with `Equal` in place of `Changed`) and `emphasis` (`wordDiff`-based `ins` and `del` with weight or line-through, dropped when either value exceeds 2,000 characters); `compareFields` keeps its signature. Below 768px each group is a stacked card: an eyebrow with the label, `Current` (or the before label) and its value above `Proposed` and its value, then the decision node at full width. Phase 15's existing tests stay green.
