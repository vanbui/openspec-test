## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** withdraw wrong risks and ask the AI to rewrite one risk or propose missing ones, **so that** the draft ends up complete and correct without rebuilding it by hand.

This plan's slice: withdrawal, reinstatement with Undo, and the risk history read, served end to end in Strapi with their grants, contract, served inventory and six-actor matrix, so an analyst can take a wrong risk out of an unreleased assessment with a reason and see it in history.

Serve `POST /api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/risk_withdrawals`, `POST /api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/risk_reinstatements` and `GET /api/v1/risks/{riskId}/history` (D-03 items 1 to 3) as human-analyst-only operations (D-43), with the batch, Undo, transition and refusal rules (D-05 to D-12, D-21, Orchestrator Confirmations), their catalogue Functions in both copies, the specification and generated client, the served inventory and the matrix rows, including the unmounted delete probes (D-16) and `regenerate_draft`'s updated reason (D-24).

Purpose: ASSESS-09 criterion 1 and 3 and success criteria 1 and 4 in the API; the web service and UI follow in 16-11 and 16-12.
Output: the curation service, routes and controller; contract, catalogue, inventory and matrix changes; mounted proofs.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>
