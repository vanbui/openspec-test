## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** withdraw wrong risks and ask the AI to rewrite one risk or propose missing ones, **so that** the draft ends up complete and correct without rebuilding it by hand.

This plan's slice: the interface for asking the AI and deciding what it proposes: the AI proposals section, proposed-risk cards, Rewrite this risk with its per-change comparison, decisions with Undo, and the run sheet and signal additions.

<objective>
Build the AI proposals section (§2), proposed-risk cards with similarity (§3), Rewrite this risk with the AI rewrite comparison (§4), batch decisions with optimistic feedback, rollback, Undo and focus (§5), the requesting analyst's state (§12.3), and the run sheet's and signal's additions (§10.2, §10.3), all client pieces code-split and zod-free (D-30 to D-41, D-45 to D-48).

Purpose: success criteria 2 and 3 in the interface; 16-14 places these in the editor.
Output: seven new components and hooks, three extended AI assistance pieces, and their tests.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Frame and loader.** `AiProposalsSection({ assessmentId, state, viewerRaisedRequest, children })` (server) renders the `DetailSection` with h2 `AI proposals`, region `id="ai-proposals"` `tabIndex={-1}`, the description, the status line from `proposalsStatusLine` and the §2.4 state line; `AiProposalsSectionLoader` (client) loads `AiProposalsSectionClient` with `next/dynamic` and a skeleton after 300 ms; the client calls `readRiskAiProposalsAction` and renders, in order, the start form or job state, the open groups, `Open AI rewrites`, and `Decided proposals` (collapsed, `Show decided proposals ({n})`).
- **Start form.** Phase 15's `InstructionField` (X-25) with id `ai-proposals-instruction`, the §2.2 hint, session draft kind `risk-suggest-instruction`, `aria-keyshortcuts` with the X-7 hint from 768px; `Suggest more risks` (primary, `Sparkles`, pending `Asking the AI…`); R2 refusals under the field or as the form's error line; the text kept on refusal and cleared once asked.
- **Job state.** `RiskProposalJob({ job, onSettled })` follows `assessment-draft-job.tsx`: polite status (`The AI is looking for more risks` or `The AI is rewriting {risk number}`), `You can leave this page…`, `Started by {name}.` for another analyst, `Attempt {n}`, `Check again` after 30 seconds with the stopped line; completion announces `{n} proposed risks are ready to review.` without moving focus; a failure shows a danger `Alert` (`The AI request didn’t finish` or `The AI rewrite didn’t finish`) with R4's copy and `Retry AI proposals` or `Retry rewrite: {risk number}` when the job's `canRetry` is true.
- **Proposed risks.** Groups are `article id="ai-run-{runId}"` with h3 (`{name} asked the AI for more risks` with the clamped instruction), facts (`{instant} · Provider: … · Model: … · {k} of {n} open`), `{a} left out` toggling `leftOutLines`, `Show what the AI used` and `Reject remaining ({k})`; items are an `ol` of `ProposedRiskCard` (`li`, `id="ai-proposal-{runId}-{itemKey}"`) per §3.2 and §3.4, with §3.3's similarity note, `Compare with …` (opening `FieldComparison` with the other record's columns and `showSame`) and `Reject as duplicate` (reason `Duplicate of {risk number}` or the rejected or open variant); `Add to assessment` waits with `Adding…`, then toasts `{risk number} added to the assessment. Review its AI-proposed fields.` with `Open {risk number}`.
- **Rewrite.** `RewriteForm({ riskId, riskNumber, openChanges })` is a `Popover` from 768px and a bottom `Sheet` below (`aria-haspopup="dialog"` on its trigger, named `Rewrite this risk: {risk number}`); title `Rewrite {risk number}`; the instruction (kind `risk-rewrite-instruction`) with the §4.1 hint and focus; fieldset `What to rewrite` with `Statement and cause`, `Ratings`, `Treatment` checked by default and `Choose at least one part to rewrite.` when none; the supersede caption when changes are open; `Rewrite risk` (`Ctrl`/`⌘` `Enter`, pending `Asking the AI…`) and `Keep as is` (`Escape`). `RiskRewriteSection({ risk, items, run, job, formHasUnsavedChanges })` renders h3 `AI rewrite` with `id="risk-{id}-rewrite"` per §4.2, the group header with `Focus: …` when partial, `Confirm all ({c})` from `confirmableCount` and `Reject remaining ({k})`, and `FieldComparison` with one group per change labelled by `changeUnitLabel`, `emphasis` on long text, sources under proposed statements and the decision cell per §4.4 (Open, Changed since proposed, Accept not allowed now via `acceptPreCheck`, Confirmed, Rejected); with `formHasUnsavedChanges`, the §4.4 line and no `Confirm`; when every change is decided the section closes and focus goes to the card's first field.
- **Decisions.** `useRiskProposalDecisions({ assessmentId })` sends one `decideRiskProposalsAction` per user act (one item, `Confirm all`, `Reject remaining`, `Undo`), applies at once at the pending opacity (Add waits), shows §5.1's toasts with `Undo` sending `restore`, rolls back with the `--duration-base` transition and R3's copy (`Try again` or `Refresh page`), refreshes the section and the Risk on `AI_PROPOSAL_STALE` and `AI_PROPOSAL_ALREADY_DECIDED`, and moves focus per §5.5. Reject opens §5.3's inline X-5 row (`Reason (optional)`, `Reject proposal`, `Keep proposal`); Reject remaining opens §5.4's row.
- **Phones and density.** Below 768px the section renders the §2.6 summary card with `Review AI proposals` and `Suggest more risks` opening the full-screen sheet `AI proposals` (`Close AI proposals`); a `Density` segmented control (`Comfortable`, `Compact`) from 768px, persisted per person, applies to the section and every rewrite comparison.
- **Requesting analyst.** With `viewerRaisedRequest` (the page passes it when the proposals read answers `OWN_REQUEST`), the section renders only the h2 and §2.4's line with `Lock`; `RiskRewriteSection` and the rewrite trigger are not rendered by their callers.
- **Run sheet and signal.** `AiRunSheetBody` gains the `Assessment` group (`Scope`, `Risks listed`, `Withdrawn risks listed`, `Earlier proposals listed`, and for a rewrite `Mitigation controls listed`) and the `Instruction` rows' risk variants and `What to rewrite`, via `sheetAssessmentRows(run)` in `ai-assistance-domain.ts`. `AiInputsChangedBlock` takes the heading from `aiInputsChangedHeadingFor` and, as its `action`, `Suggest more risks` with the caption `To bring this into one risk, use Rewrite this risk on that risk.`, or the requesting analyst's caption with no action.
