## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: a Security Analyst opens a released risk in the register, sees its living treatment, re-rates its residual risk, records control delivery and closes or reopens it with a reason, and is told plainly when separation of duties withholds those acts.

<objective>
Build the register risk detail's post-release panel (D-13 to D-16; 20-UI-SPEC §7): the shared treatment sections for analysts, the residual review sheet, close and reopen confirmations, control delivery, and the withheld states (G-1, G-11), plus the analyst released record's caption and links (D-06; §8).

Purpose: SC2's "the analyst re-rates residual risk and closes the risk with a reason" in the UI, and UAT-046 ("control status updated as work happens").
Output: the panel, three sheets and dialogs, their actions and service calls, the analyst caption, and Vitest proofs.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Panel.** `post-release-panel.tsx` (server-composed with client islands) renders, for a released risk, h2 `Treatment` (20-16's `TrackingPanel` with `viewer: "analyst"`, 20-17's `ProgressNotes` and `EvidenceSection`), h2 `Decision review` (20-17's section, read-only for analysts), h2 `Residual review` (`Last reviewed by {name} · {date}` with the rationale, or `Not reviewed since release`, and `Record residual review`), and the mitigation controls' `Record delivery` (ghost small, named `Record delivery of {control label}`); when `withheldReason` is set it renders §7.5's `Alert` in place of every act. `risk-details.tsx` mounts it after the header and adds `Close risk` or `Reopen risk` (outline) before `Show history`.
- **Sheets and dialogs** (loaded with `next/dynamic`, Phase 10's guard, full screen below 768px). `residual-review-sheet.tsx` per §7.1 with 18's matrix picker and the three lines; `close-risk-dialog.tsx` per §7.2 (`ConfirmDialog` at 640px, the reason field, `Closing…`); `control-delivery-sheet.tsx` per §7.3 with only `Implemented` and `Retired` offered from Planned (only `Retired` from Existing).
- **Typed layer.** `risk-service.ts` gains `closeRisk`, `reopenRisk`, `recordResidualReview`, `recordControlDelivery` (`server-only`); `risk-actions.ts` gains their Server Actions, mapping family Y2's reasons and stale values (§4.5) with input kept.
- **Analyst released caption.** `assessment-detail.tsx`'s released view adds §8's caption and links each risk number to `/app/risks/{id}`; its values stay the released read's.
