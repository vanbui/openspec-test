## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: the approved stored shapes, proven on a fresh boot: the risk's tracking, completion, closure and review facts, the evidence and delivery tables (with a real unique index), and the history vocabulary for post-release rows and the system actor.

- **Writer (`risk-history.ts`).** `RiskHistoryEntry.actorDocumentId: string | null`; `recordRiskHistory` throws `RiskHistoryError` when the actor is null and the source is not `scheduled-run`, when the source is `scheduled-run` and the actor is set, when a post-release kind comes from a source other than `post-release` or `scheduled-run` (and `scheduled-run` only for `status-derived` and `release-baseline`, because the run's first write to an untouched released risk writes its baseline), and when the reason breaks its kind's bound (`progress-noted`, `residual-reviewed`, `decision-reviewed`: required, 1–2,000; `treatment-changed`, `risk-closed`, `risk-reopened`: required, 1–500; `treatment-reopened`: required 1–500 unless `undo` is true). The read projects `actor: null` with `sourceKind: "scheduled-run"` unchanged (16-03 already projects null). No `update` or `delete` is added.
- **Harness.** `CsrmFixtureUid` gains `"api::csrm-treatment-evidence.csrm-treatment-evidence"` and `"api::csrm-reminder-delivery.csrm-reminder-delivery"`.
