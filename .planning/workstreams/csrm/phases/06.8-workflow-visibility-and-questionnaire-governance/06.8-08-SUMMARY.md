## Defects found and fixed after execution

The suites and the browser found seven problems that the typechecks passed.

1. **The questionnaire relation's `state` was never populated.** Nothing writes a `state`
   attribute, so the projection's `isNonEmptyString(questionnaire.state)` always yielded null and
   the hub's governance line claimed "analyst review" for every questionnaire, released or not.
   The relation now derives the state the way the lifecycle does: the release fact decides
   analyst-review versus released (D-14), and the answers decide submitted. Verified in the
   browser: the unreleased fixture reads "analyst review" and the submitted one reads
   "submitted". This also closes the "State not recorded" follow-up recorded on 2026-09-21.
2. **The request detail page dropped most of the record.** Description, division, business unit,
   business purpose, third-party involvement and details, regulatory requirements and the
   Phase 06.1 revert audit all disappeared from the Requestor's own request. The hub is a compact
   summary beneath the stepper (D-10), not a replacement for the record, so the business context
   and the revert audit are restored beneath it.
3. **Record identifiers rendered as plain text beside separate Open controls.** That contradicts
   the locked Phase 06.3 rule that a record opens from its identifier, and it is why the existing
   browser invariant was failing. The questionnaire and assessment cards now open from their
   identifiers. An available record is always named even when no destination is supplied; only an
   unavailable record leaves no trace.
4. **The Phase 06.4 relationship-states browser invariant had been replaced.** The property test
   asserting that a Requestor never sees an assessment identifier they cannot open, the absent
   tally proof and the residual-rating tile assertions were all deleted and replaced with weaker
   happy-path coverage. The original spec is restored, updated only where the hub genuinely
   renamed a section, and new hub coverage is appended rather than substituted. The hub re-adds
   the tally's name and its per-rating accessible labels.
5. **The analyst questionnaire link could never be built.** The fallback href was gated on
   `request.relationships` being absent, which is never true in production, so the analyst hub
   would have shown the questionnaire with no way to open it.
6. **The next required action rendered twice**, once in the stepper and once in the hub. It
   belongs to the hub (D-10); the stepper names the current state and nothing else (D-01).
7. **Presentation defects:** a stray full stop after the questionnaire identifier, a raw
   `in-review` enum where the shared status label belongs, and "1 control implementations".

The unit tests for the analyst review screen asserted a card that this plan supersedes, using
fixtures that omitted the relationships the server always sends. They now carry those
relationships and assert the hub's equivalents, so they describe what production renders.
