## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager or leader, **I want** trends, ageing, cycle times and a heat map, **so that** I can see whether risk and the assessment process are improving.

This plan's slice: the lead analyst opens Reports and sees, on one page, open released risks by residual rating over twelve months, the risk matrix, time to release and stage times with ageing, accepted risks and exceptions by review state, and AI assistance health, then prints it for the risk committee.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Composition.** `LeadershipView` (Server Component) reads `getReport('risk-trend', { period: 12 })`, `getReport('risk-exposure')`, `getReport('cycle-times', { period: 12 })` and `getReport('ai-assistance', { period: 12 })` in parallel (`Promise.allSettled`); each section renders from its read or its compact error (`We couldn’t load {section}` with `Try again`); when all four fail, the page's retryable error. Sections per UI-SPEC §3.2: (1) visually hidden h3 `Summary`, tiles `Released open Risks` (trend's partial total), `Critical residual`, `High residual`, `Reviews overdue` (exposure's `acceptedReviews.accepted.overdue`); (2) the trend section through 21-11's presentation; (3) `RiskMatrixPair` and the under-assessment row through 21-16's presentation; (4) tiles `Median days to release`, `90th percentile days to release`, the stage table and `QueueAgeing` with `showControl: false` (team), the history line; (5) the accepted-and-exceptions table; (6) the AI tiles. Footers `Open the {report title} report` with the period.
- **Index.** `ReportIndex` gains h2 `All reports` and h3 card titles; the page renders the as-at line, h2 `Leadership view` with its description, `LeadershipView`, then the catalogue; `loading.tsx` renders `LeadershipViewSkeleton` and the catalogue skeleton with `Loading reports`.
- **Header.** `ReportsPageHeader` description becomes the UI-SPEC's and its action is `PrintButton`.
- **Print button (`print-button.tsx`, `'use client'`, zod-free).** An outline pill `Print` with `Printer` calling `window.print()`; `report-detail.tsx`'s header actions place it before 19's `Export` on every report page.
- **Print rules (`globals.css`, under `@media print`).** Reuse X-31's `@page`; `[data-print="hide"]` hides the shell, `Print`, `All reports`, chevrons and segmented controls; leadership and report sections `break-inside: avoid`, a section taller than a page breaking between its chart and its table; a `[data-print="only"]` closing note in `--text-caption` with the print instant and as-at date. Tokens only.
