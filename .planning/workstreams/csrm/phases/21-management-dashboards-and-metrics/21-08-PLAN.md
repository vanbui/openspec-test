## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager or leader, **I want** trends, ageing, cycle times and a heat map, **so that** I can see whether risk and the assessment process are improving.

This plan's slice: a Security Analyst opens Reports and finds Cycle times: time to release, how long requests spend in each stage including the response, acceptance and rework rounds, the slowest items, rounds and queue ageing, from recorded history.
