## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager or leader, **I want** trends, ageing, cycle times and a heat map, **so that** I can see whether risk and the assessment process are improving.

This plan's slice: the shared web pieces every Phase 21 page draws with: the trend chart, the risk matrix, the phone forms of bar charts and tiles, the stage and run-outcome badges, and one formatter for every duration, rate and sample line.
