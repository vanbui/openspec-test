## Recorded planning decisions

- **Queue query.** `safeReviewFilters` accepts `stage` (one of `OPEN_REQUEST_STAGES`) and `ageBand` (one of `AGEING_BANDS`), each a single exact value; anything else throws `AssessmentRequestQueryError` carrying `reason: "LIST_FILTER_INVALID"` (the controller's existing 400 path passes `details.reason`). `REVIEW_QUEUE_SORT_FIELDS` gains `inStage` (entry instant ascending, unrecorded last, then `id`), sorted longest first when chosen. `AnalystReviewRead` gains `stage?` and `ageBand?`.
- **Queue SQL.** The repository's list and count apply `joinLatestStage(query, "r.id")`, then `whereCurrentStage` and `whereAgeBand(query, band, today)` when given; `today` is read once per call (`utcToday(options.now?.() ?? new Date())`). Each row projects `inStage` through `projectInStage`.
- **My requests.** `listMyRequests` rows add `inStage` from the same join and projection; no other field, filter or grant changes.
- **Contract.** Spec component `RequestInStage` = `{ stage: RequestStage, enteredAt: date-time, workingDays: integer, calendarDays: integer }` with `RequestStage` the eight-value enum; the queue operation gains `filters[stage]`, `filters[ageBand]` and the `inStage` sort value; the queue row and the My requests row gain `inStage: RequestInStage | null`; the queue's 400 lists `LIST_FILTER_INVALID`.
- **Web.** `assessment-schema.ts` parses `inStage` (closed, nullable) and the two filters; `parseAssessmentRequestReviewQuery` reads `stage` and `ageBand` from the URL and passes them as `filters`; `assessment-list.tsx` adds the column (header `In stage`, right-aligned `formatWorkingDays` over the stage label, `Not recorded` in `--color-text-secondary`), the `Stage` and `Time in stage` selects with chips per UI-SPEC §7, the phone card line, and on a 400 the filtered error `One of these filters isn’t recognised.` with `Clear filters`. `request-schema.ts` parses `inStage`; `own-requests-panel.tsx` line 2 reads `{number} · With the Security Analyst for {formatCalendarDays(n)}` (`since today` at 0) for an analyst-held item with `inStage`, else 11's `Submitted {n} days ago`; `request-table.tsx`'s Next step cell adds the caption `For {n} days, since {date}` (X-3 date) when `inStage` is present.
