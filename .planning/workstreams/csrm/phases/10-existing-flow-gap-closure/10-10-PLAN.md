## Recorded planning decisions

- **Which text an event keeps.** Added and edited events keep the question's text after the change; removed events keep the text the question had when removed; answer-cleared events keep the text of the question whose answer was cleared.
- **Projection inputs.** `findWorkflowOne` already populates `questions` (`controllers/csrm-questionnaire.ts:363-370`) and the document carries `generatedDate`, so resolution needs no extra read and no controller change.
- **The key stays in the analyst projection** (the contract has it) but is never rendered.
- **Bounds in the spec:** `questionText` is `{ "type": "string", "minLength": 1, "maxLength": 500 }` in both `QuestionnaireGovernanceEvent` and `QuestionnaireRevisionTimelineEvent`, and the web schemas mirror exactly that.
