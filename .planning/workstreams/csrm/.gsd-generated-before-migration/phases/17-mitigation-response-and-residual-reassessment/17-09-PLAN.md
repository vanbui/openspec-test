## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: the assigned analyst releases the assessment for response once it is ready, which opens a numbered round, writes the never-cleared gate and pauses editing; and closes the round when the responses are in.

<objective>
Serve "Release for response" and "Close round {n}" (D-09, D-12, D-13) to the assigned analyst only, with release-for-response readiness (D-10), the gate pair on the first round (D-11), one requested response per participant, the round lock in the one guard (D-43 to D-45), the v1 release refusals and readiness additions (D-14, D-41), and the analyst and participant detail shapes that show all of it (D-06, D-37). Publish, grant and prove both operations (D-02, D-64).

Purpose: ASSESS-11 criteria 1 and 2, SC1's "release for response is refused while any risk has no owner", and the gate every participant read depends on. Responses (17-11), notifications (17-12) and decisions (17-13) build on these rows.
Output: one service, two operations with their contract chain, the guard's round rule, release readiness additions and the two detail shapes.

Edits Strapi source? Yes (dev servers stopped). Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>
