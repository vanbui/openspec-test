## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: the Requestor and the risk owner read the whole assessment on one page that says what stage it is in and what their part is, and tell the analyst, risk by risk, which controls they have or plan, without ever losing what they typed.

<objective>
Make the participant page stage-aware (D-25, D-27, D-69; 17-UI-SPEC §6), add the participant form of the round panel and the response form with autosave, the phone sheet and submission (D-31 to D-35; §6.3, §6.4, §7; X-36, X-37), the analyst-participant's response panel (D-37; §5.3), the report route's state before release (D-29; UI-D20) and families U3 and U7, at the G-4 bar (D-70) with the route budget (D-72).

Purpose: SC2's reading half and SC3's "the Requestor adds two mitigating controls to a risk, naming a control owner found by name, and submits", in the UI; UAT-025.
Output: the participant typed layer for the response, the response form and its autosave, the stage-aware page, the analyst-participant panel and the report state.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (`accessibility.spec.ts`, `performance.spec.ts`); regenerates the served inventory (new web callers).
</objective>
