## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: the analyst reads what the first line proposed under each risk, adds or rejects each proposal with a reason, asks the AI to reassess residual risk from the recorded controls, and confirms each proposed value.

<objective>
Build the analyst's decisions and the reassessment in the editor: the Responses section under each risk (D-36; X-38), the add form, reject row and group reject with Undo and rollback (D-38, D-39; UI-D1 to UI-D3), the no-lost-input rule on Escape (orchestrator amendment 1), and "Reassess residual risk" with its proposals on Phase 16's AI proposals section (D-46 to D-50; UI-D18, UI-D19), with families U4 and U5.

Purpose: SC3's "the analyst accepts one and rejects one with a reason" and SC4's "'Reassess residual risk' proposes new residual values citing the accepted controls, and the analyst accepts them per field (visible 'Confirm')", in the UI.
Output: the rounds read and decisions in the typed layer, two components, the reassessment form and proposals, and the refusal copy.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (`performance.spec.ts`); regenerates the served inventory (new web callers).
</objective>
