# Phase 17.2 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for contest points and rework rounds (COLLAB-04, ASSESS-26, NOTIFY-04). It covers:
> - the contest values and the markers beside contested content (D-13, D-16);
> - raising a point, with its target picker (D-04, D-06);
> - the points panel, the point sheet, its thread, the analyst's answer, and resolve, withdraw and undo (D-05, D-07 to D-10);
> - sending back and the returned round, for both actors (D-11, D-13);
> - readiness with open points, and the rework order (D-12);
> - round n+1's "stands" and "decide again", with the before-and-after comparison (D-14);
> - the history view per actor (D-15);
> - lists, Homes, lifecycle and notifications (D-13, D-16 to D-18), and the refusals (family W).
>
> It adds no colour, type size, weight, family, radius, shadow or duration. Five patterns are proposed as CSRM extensions X-48 to X-52.

## Voice, formats and shared copy rules

Phase 11's, 13's, 17's and 17.1's rules apply unchanged. In addition:

- **Terms (D-25):** `Contest`, `Contest point`, `Contest point {k}` (in running text) and `Point {k}` (in lists and identifiers), `Raise contest point`, `Reason`, `Reply`, `Answer`, `Changed`, `Kept`, `Resolve`, `Withdraw`, `Send back`, `Returned`, `History`. Never `Comment`, `Dispute`, `Objection`, `Decline` or `Reject` for a point.
- **Targets are named as the reader saw them:** `Assessment details · {field label}`, `{risk number}` (the whole risk), `{risk number} · {field label}`, `{risk number} · {control name}`. Field labels are served from the target vocabulary (D-04), never composed by the web.
- **Points are plain text:** reasons, replies, answers and messages show as typed, wrapping anywhere, never as HTML or Markdown, never auto-linked (Phase 13 §8.2).
- **Status words come from the mapping (§1).** Copy never says a point was "closed" by the analyst: only its raiser resolves or withdraws it.

## 2. Markers beside contested content (D-16, D-23; X-48)

Participants and analysts see the same marker; it renders only where the served per-target counts are above zero.

| Part | Contract |
|---|---|
| Placement | Inline after the target's label: a field's `.micro-label` in a definition list (10 §15.2), a Risk card's h3 (after its rating and X-39 tags), a control implementation's name in its row. One marker per target; nothing renders for a target with no points |
| Anatomy | A `RecordLink`-styled button: 12px `MessageSquareWarning` (`aria-hidden`), then `--text-caption` 500 text: `{o} open · {a} answered` (zero parts omitted), or `{c} closed` when every point is resolved or withdrawn. | Phones | Field markers wrap under their label; the card summary shows the roll-up only |
| Print | Markers print as text |

## 3. Raising a contest point (D-04, D-06; X-49)

### 3.1 Where `Contest` appears

Only for a current participant while an acceptance round is open; no control renders for anyone else (17.1 §7.4's rule: words, never a disabled control).

| Place | Control | Pre-selected target |
|---|---|---|
| `Assessment overview` header | `Contest` (ghost small, `MessageSquareWarning`), accessible name `Contest the assessment details` | `Assessment details`, no field |
| Each Risk card header | `Contest` (ghost small), accessible name `Contest {risk number}` | The whole risk |
| Each control implementation row | `Contest` (ghost small, icon-only below 768px with the accessible name `Contest {control name} on {risk number}`) | That control |
| 17.1 §7.1 "Accept not offered" line | Appends ` If you think this is wrong, contest it.` with the link `Contest the rating` | `{risk number} · Residual risk rating` |
| 17.1 §7.1 High approval (approver) | `Contest` (outline small) and `Ask for another approver` (ghost small) after the checkbox | `{risk number}` / `{risk number} · Executive approver` |
| Points panel (§4) | `Raise a contest point` (outline) | None |
| An answered point's sheet (raiser, round open) | `Raise a follow-up point` (ghost small) | The answered point's target, with `followsUp` |

`Ask for another approver` is a contest point on the risk's `Executive approver` field: its form hint adds `The Security Analyst can name another executive approver once the assessment is sent back or the round recalled.`

### 3.2 The raise form

A side sheet (X-13) from 768px; a full-screen sheet below (17 amendment 2). Loaded on demand. Title `Raise a contest point`; description `on {assessment number}`.

| Part | Contract |
|---|---|
| `Where` | `Select`, label `Where`: `Assessment details`, then each readable active risk `{risk number} · {risk statement}` (clamped to one line). Pre-set from §3.1 |
| `What` | `RadioGroup`, legend `What are you contesting?`: for a risk, `The whole risk`, then its served fields, then `Control: {control name}` for each control implementation; for `Assessment details`, its served fields (`Summary`, `Overall rating` …). Pre-set from §3.1 |
| What it shows now | Micro-label `What you’re contesting`, then the target's current values, read-only, as the page renders them (a rating stays its badge). This is what the snapshot keeps |
| Earlier points | When the target already has points: `{risk number} already has {n} contest {point|points}.` with figure link `Show {them|it}` to the panel filtered to that target |
| Follow-up | `Follows up Point {k}`, with its answer quoted (2 lines, `Show full answer`) |
| `Reason` | `Textarea`, required, label `Reason`, hint `Say what you disagree with and why. The Security Analyst and everyone taking part will read it. A contest point can’t be changed once raised; you can withdraw it.` Three rows, growing to twelve. Errors on blur and on submit: `Write why you’re contesting this.` and `Keep the reason to 2,000 characters or fewer. It has {n}.` |
| Action | `Raise contest point` (primary; optimistic, so no pending label), X-7 hint `Ctrl` `Enter`; `Cancel` (outline) closes and keeps the text |

**Apply** (optimistic, D-06): the sheet closes, the point appears at once in the panel at the §5 pending opacity, the target's marker updates, and an Undo toast reads `Contest point {k} raised on {target}. The Security Analyst is told.` `Undo` withdraws it (D-06's fixed note) and confirms `Contest point {k} withdrawn.` Focus returns to the control that opened the form. Rollback: the pending point leaves, the form reopens with its text and the W1 copy.

**No lost input:** the reason and the chosen target are kept in the session store per person, assessment and target (Phase 13 §9.3) under the navigation guard; `Escape` and `Cancel` keep them; a restored draft shows `Restored from earlier in this session.`

## 5. The point sheet (D-05, D-07 to D-10; X-49, X-50)

A side sheet (X-13) from 768px; a full-screen sheet with title row, `Close` and a pinned action footer below. Addressable as `#point-{k}`; notifications and markers open it. Loaded on demand. Title `Contest point {k}`; under it the badge and `Raised by {name} · {part} · Round {n} · {instant}`.

### 5.1 Order

1. **Target:** micro-label `Contested`, then `{target}` (a `RecordLink` to the target on the page while it is part of the assessment).
2. **Comparison (§5.2).**
3. **Reason:** micro-label `Reason`, the text in typographic quotes.
4. **Follows up:** `Follows up Point {j}` link, when set.
5. **Thread (§5.3)**, then the composer or the closed line.
6. **Actions (§5.4, §5.5)** in the footer.

### 5.2 Comparison: when raised against now (D-14; X-50)

X-24's comparison, read-only, with X-50's headings: `When raised ({date})` and `Now`. Unchanged fields collapse behind `Show {n} unchanged {field|fields}`; when nothing differs, one line replaces the table: `Nothing has changed here since the point was raised.` Control implementations added, removed or changed show as rows captioned `Added`, `Removed` or `Changed`. A field absent from the snapshot's version reads `Not recorded`. Below 768px fields stack (X-24).

### 5.3 The thread (X-16, with X-49's entry kinds)

An `ol` named `Thread on Point {k}`, oldest first; 20 entries render, earlier ones behind `Show earlier entries ({m})` (13 §8.2).

| Entry kind | Contract |
|---|---|
| Reply | X-16's note: name (500), `{part} · {instant}`, body |
| Answer | A well on `--color-bg-inset`, `--radius-lg`, 12px padding: label `Answer: Changed` or `Answer: Kept` (`--text-body` 500, `MessageSquareQuote` 16px), `{name} · Security Analyst · {instant}`, body. An earlier answer is captioned `Replaced by a later answer {instant}`; nothing is removed |
| Resolved, withdrawn | One line (`--text-caption`, `--color-text-secondary`, its §1 icon 12px): `{name} resolved this point · {instant}` / `{name} withdrew this point · {instant}`; the raise's Undo reads `Withdrawn with Undo straight after it was raised · {instant}` |
| Undo | `{name} undid the {resolution|withdrawal} · {instant}` (`Undo2`) |

**Composer** (13 §8.3 with): label `Reply`; hint (participant) `Everyone taking part and the Security Analysts can read replies. A reply can’t be changed once posted.`; (requesting analyst) 13 §8.3's requesting-analyst hint with `reply` for `note`; action `Post reply` (primary), `Ctrl`/`⌘` `Enter`. Validation: `Write a reply before posting it.`; `Keep the reply to 5,000 characters or fewer. It has {n}.` Posting, rollback, one in flight and unsent text follow 13 §9.1 and §9.3 (keyed by point). No toast.

**Closed line** (13 §8.4's form, `Lock`): resolved or withdrawn, `This point is {resolved|withdrawn}, so its thread is closed. It stays here as part of the record.`; released, `The assessment is released, so contest points are closed. They stay here as part of the record.` Unsent text then follows 13 §9.4.

### 5.4 The analyst's answer (D-08)

For a human Security Analyst who may answer, while the point is open or answered and unreleased: an h3 `Answer`, above the reply composer.

| Part | Contract |
|---|---|
| `Outcome` | `SegmentedControl` `Changed` \| `Kept`, no default |
| Round open | No segmented control; `Kept` is the only outcome, with the line (`Info`) `Acceptance round {n} is open, so the assessment can’t change yet. You can answer Kept now, or answer once the round is returned or recalled.` |
| Text | `Changed`: label `What changed`, hint `Say what you changed and where. {raiser} and everyone taking part will read it.`; `Kept`: label `Why it’s kept`, hint `Say why the assessment stays as it is. {raiser} and everyone taking part will read it.` Both add `An answer can’t be changed; you can answer again.` Errors `Write what changed.` / `Write why it’s kept.` and `Keep the answer to 2,000 characters or fewer. It has {n}.` |
| Target unchanged | With `Changed` chosen and §5.2 showing no change: caption `{target} itself hasn’t changed. Say what changed elsewhere.` (D-08: never blocking) |
| Answered already | Caption `You’re answering again. Your answer replaces the earlier one, which stays in the thread.` |
| Action | `Post answer` (primary), `Ctrl`/`⌘` `Enter` |

**Apply** (optimistic): the answer appears as a pending well, the badge turns `Answered`, the panel count updates; a polite status says `Answer posted. {raiser} is told.` Rollback per 13 §9.1 with W2's copy. Answer text is kept in the session store per point.

**Who sees no answer form** (one X-15 line in its place, `Lock`): a participant (no line); the requesting analyst, `You raised this request, so another Security Analyst answers its contest points.`; a named owner or approver who is an analyst, `You’re a {risk owner|executive approver} on this assessment, so another Security Analyst answers its contest points.`

### 5.5 The raiser's acts (D-09)

Footer actions for the raiser only:

| Status | Actions |
|---|---|
| Open | `Withdraw` (outline) |
| Answered | `Resolve` (primary, `MessageSquareCheck`), `Withdraw` (outline); in an open round also `Raise a follow-up point` (ghost small) |
| Resolved, withdrawn | None |

Accessible names carry the point: `Resolve Point {k}`. Each applies at once: the badge and the closed line update, and an Undo toast reads `Point {k} resolved.` or `Point {k} withdrawn.`; `Undo` (shown only in the toast, while the server's window lasts) confirms `Point {k} is {answered|open} again.` Focus moves to the sheet's title. Rollback restores the previous state with W2's copy.

Anyone else sees no resolve or withdraw control; an analyst viewing a participant's point reads nothing extra (D-19).

### 5.6 Targets that left the assessment (D-10)

The sheet and the panel row carry the neutral tag `No longer part of this assessment`; §5.2 shows the snapshot alone under `When raised ({date})`, with the line `This {risk|control} is no longer part of the assessment. The Security Analyst’s answer says what happened.` The target is plain text, never a link. No withdrawal reason, actor or time renders for participants. A reinstated risk shows its live comparison again.
