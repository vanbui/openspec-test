## Recorded planning decisions

- **Service, schema, actions.** `lists-service.ts` (`import 'server-only'`): `listDivisions()`, `createDivision(label)`, `updateDivision(id, { label } | { position })`, `retireDivision(id)`, `reinstateDivision(id)`, `createBusinessUnit(divisionId, label)`, `updateBusinessUnit(id, …)`, `retireBusinessUnit(id)`, `reinstateBusinessUnit(id)`, each registered for parity; `lists-schema.ts` closed parsers; `lists-actions.ts` `'use server'` actions returning the AdministrationActionResult shape, then `revalidatePath("/app/admin/divisions")`. `request-service.ts` gains `getRequestFormMetadata()` and `request-schema.ts` its parser.
- **Editor (`list-editor.tsx`, client).** UI-SPEC §7.1 and §7.2 exactly: optimistic move with rollback toast `We couldn’t move “{name}”. It’s back where it was.` · `Try again`; polite announcement `{name} moved to position {p} of {n}.`; retire and reinstate apply at once with the Undo toasts (the division's `It and its business units are no longer offered on requests.`), Undo calling the inverse action; `Rename` drawn only when `usedOn` is 0; the caption under the description; `Show` `Active ({n})` · `Retired ({n})` kept in the address.
- **Request form.** The new and edit pages load `getRequestFormMetadata()` and pass the lists; `RequestForm` renders `Division` (active divisions in order, `Not set`) and `Business unit` (the chosen division's active units; disabled with `Choose a division first.`; changing the division clears it and announces `Business unit cleared.`); a stored value not among active entries stays selected as `{value} (retired)` (found among retired entries) or `{value} (not on the list)` with `No longer offered. It stays unless you choose another.`; an empty list shows `No divisions are listed yet. Leave this as Not set.`; the suggestion fields are removed from the form only.
- **Copy.** Family AA2 in `refusal-copy.ts` per UI-SPEC §12, the field kinds keeping input.
