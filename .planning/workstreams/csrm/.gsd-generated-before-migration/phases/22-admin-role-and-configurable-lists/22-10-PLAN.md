## Recorded planning decisions

- **Operations** (`apps/backend/src/api/csrm-division/operations.ts`, `CSRM_REQUEST_LIST_OPERATIONS`): the nine of 22-01 "Catalogue and grants" (`listDivisions`, `createDivision`, `updateDivision`, `retireDivision`, `reinstateDivision`, `createBusinessUnit`, `updateBusinessUnit`, `retireBusinessUnit`, `reinstateBusinessUnit`) with `requireAdminScope`, plus `getRequestFormMetadata` `GET:/api/v1/assessment_requests/form_metadata` with the default policy config (Requestor birthright). Router `routes/request-lists.ts`; controller `controllers/request-lists.ts`; operationIds under tag `configuration`, the form metadata `assessment-workflow/get-request-form-metadata`.
- **Bodies.** Create `{ label }`; update `{ label }` or `{ position }` (exactly one, else 400 `CONFIGURATION_INVALID` at the root); retire and reinstate `{}`. Responses: the Admin's read `{ data: RequestListDivision[] }` ordered by position (active first, then retired by label) and each act's affected division in that shape.
- **Service (`services/request-lists.ts`).** Every act runs in `strapi.db.transaction` with the division row locked `forUpdate`; labels through `normaliseListLabel` (400 `CONFIGURATION_INVALID` at `label` with `required` or `too-long`), clash `LIST_LABEL_TAKEN` (409); a rename of an entry whose `usedOn` is above 0 is 409 `LIST_LABEL_IN_USE` with `details.usedOn`; `usedOn` counts requests whose trimmed, case-folded `division` (and `businessUnit` within that division for a unit) equals the label key; positions are renumbered 0..n-1 among active siblings; retire sets `isActive: false` and appends the entry at the end of the retired group; a division retire also retires its active units with `retiredWithDivision: true`; a division reinstate reinstates the units marked `retiredWithDivision` and clears the mark; a unit reinstate needs nothing of its division (a unit under a retired division is not offered). Change rows: area `request-lists`, subject `division` or `business-unit` with the label, actions `created`, `renamed`, `moved` (fields `position`), `retired`, `reinstated`; a cascade writes one row per unit beside the division's row. `requestFormLists()` returns the form metadata shape (active entries by position, then retired ones by label, each with `isActive`; units likewise within their division); the request rule reads its active entries.
- **Request rule.** `request-workflow.ts`'s create and update preparation calls `checkRequestListValues` with the active entries of `requestFormLists()` when the body names `division` or `businessUnit`, writes the returned values, and refuses 400 with `details.reason` `DIVISION_NOT_ON_LIST` or `BUSINESS_UNIT_NOT_ON_LIST` and `details.field`.
- **Suggestions.** `function.risk_assessment_requests.field_suggestions_api` keeps `role.security_analyst` only; the record rule in `request-field-suggestions.ts` refuses any caller who is not a human Security Analyst (403) as defence in depth.
- **Fixtures.** Test labels start with `Fixture ` so 22-11's development list seed (Technology, Customer and Digital) never collides with them.
- **Catalogue.** `function.configuration.request_lists_api` per 22-01; the design note records the served form metadata; the served inventory drops form metadata from the unserved list.
