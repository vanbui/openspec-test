## Recorded planning decisions

- **Page.** The page receives `searchParams` and passes `riskFilter` (`active` unless `risks=withdrawn`) to `AssessmentEditor`; it calls `listRiskAiProposals(documentId)` beside the assessment read; a 403 with `OWN_REQUEST` sets `viewerRaisedRequest: true` and passes no proposals; any other failure passes `proposalsError: true` (the section shows its error state, readiness still shows the server's item). Control implementation reads stay as they are (analysts read a withdrawn risk's implementations).
- **Editor.** `AssessmentEditor` gains props `riskFilter`, `proposals`, `viewerRaisedRequest`, `proposalsError`; it renders `AiProvenanceHeader` with `openProposals` (hidden for the requesting analyst) linking to `#ai-proposals`, the readiness card with `releaseReadinessSummary`'s new item, `AiProposalsSection` (with `AiProposalsSectionLoader`) before `Identified Risks`, a `SegmentedControl` labelled `Show` with `Active ({n})` and `Withdrawn ({w})` from `showCounts` that replaces the address's `risks` parameter without moving focus, the active Risk cards or the `WithdrawnRiskCard`s, and `Add Risk` only under `Active`; the §6.3 empty states; `useRiskWithdrawal` for both lists; withdrawn cards print only under `Withdrawn`.
- **Risk card.** `RiskEditor` gains `actions` (a `RiskCardActions` with the `RewriteForm` trigger unless released, withdrawn or `viewerRaisedRequest`), `rewrite` (the `RiskRewriteSection` placed first in the card, above Phase 12's review bar), the `{k} AI changes to review` link beside the number from `openByRisk`, and reports its unsaved state to the rewrite section and the withdraw form. In create mode it gains `Discard new risk` (ghost, after `Add risk`): with input it closes the form and toasts `New risk discarded.` with `Undo` that reopens the form with the typed values and focus in the first field; untouched it closes with no toast; nothing is sent.
- **Readiness.** `releaseReadinessSummary` presents the served `aiProposalsDecided` item with the link to `#ai-proposals` and the caption `{n} open` from the proposals read, or, for the requesting analyst, `This assessment has open AI proposals. Another Security Analyst decides them before release.` with no link.
- **Hint.** Under `Overall rating`, while editable, `overallRatingHint` drives the line (named by the select's `aria-describedby`) and `Use {rating}` (outline, small), which sets the field's value without saving (X-1's unsaved state applies) and focuses the field.
- **Released.** When the assessment is released, the editor passes `editable: false` everywhere: no `Withdraw risk`, `Reinstate risk`, `Rewrite this risk`, `Suggest more risks`, AI rewrite section, decision or `Discard new risk`; the `Show` filter, withdrawn cards (without `Reinstate risk`), `History` and `Decided proposals` remain.
- **Copy guard.** `interface-copy-guard.test.ts` adds `src/features/risk-curation` to its scanned folders and a check that no button or menu item there has the visible text `Accept`, `Accept all`, `Approve`, `Delete` or `Remove`.
- **Deliberate end-to-end checks.** Before editing, grep `assessment-release-workflow.spec.ts` for the readiness items it asserts; change it only where the new met item `Decisions on AI proposals` changes an asserted list, and record the change. `performance.spec.ts`, `accessibility.spec.ts` and `design-contract.spec.ts` stay green unchanged; a route-budget overrun is fixed by lazy loading, or recorded as an exception with its reason in the summary.
