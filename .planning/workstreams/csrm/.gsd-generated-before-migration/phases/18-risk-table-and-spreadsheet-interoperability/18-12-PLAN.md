## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want** an assessment's risks in a table I can edit, copy and paste like Excel or a SharePoint list, **so that** reviewing and updating many risks takes minutes rather than a long scroll.

This plan's slice: every cell can be edited with the right editor, ten risks' treatment owner, date and status set by keyboard and saved in one batch in under a minute, ratings set on the matrix, rows added, duplicated, withdrawn and reinstated, and nothing typed is lost.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Scale domain (`risk-scale-domain.ts`, zod-free).** `deriveFromServedScale(scale, likelihood, consequence)` (band of score × score, null when either is off the scale or empty), `ratingReading(scale, { likelihood, consequence, rating, reason, released })` returning `{ kind: "derived" | "overridden" | "missing-input" | "off-scale" | "released"; line }` with UI-SPEC §4.3's cell-line sentences, and `resolveRatingEdit(scale, stage, current, incoming)` mirroring 18-02's `resolveRatingWrite` for the change set and the cell errors. `risk-scale-parity.test.ts` reads `apps/backend/tests/fixtures/risk-table-parity.json` by relative path and asserts every `derivations` case.
- **Editors.** `basic-cell-editors.tsx` (default export map for `next/dynamic`): `TextCellEditor` (18-03), `LongTextCellEditor` (a popover textarea at the column's width, at most `28rem`, growing to ten lines then scrolling; `Shift+Enter` breaks lines), `PickListCellEditor` (`Command` in a `Popover`: type to filter, arrows, Enter), `DateCellEditor` (input accepting the three formats through the same parsing rules as 18-02's date converter, mirrored in `risk-table-domain.ts` as `parseTypedDate`, `Calendar` on `Alt+ArrowDown`, value `YYYY-MM-DD`, display X-3). `picker-cell-editors.tsx`: `PersonCellEditor` (`OwnerCombobox`), `RecordCellEditor` (`RelationPicker` for Risk Type, system, asset), `ControlCellEditor` (`RelationPicker` over the control search; adds a planned control as a `controls` entry; never removes). `risk-grid-cell.tsx` picks the editor by column kind; each loads with `next/dynamic` and shows a `Skeleton` after 300ms; editors prefetch on grid focus (research Pitfall 3).
- **Matrix picker (X-57).** `MatrixPicker({ scale, stage, riskNumber, current, onApply, onCancel })`: a `Popover` anchored to the cell from 768px; title `{Inherent|Residual} rating for {risk number}`; a 5 × 5 `RadioGroup` named by the title, likelihood rising up the vertical axis, consequence along the horizontal, axis labels `{label} ({score})`; 40px cells with the band's §3.6 tint, score and 12px icon, accessible name `Likelihood {label}, consequence {label}: {score}, {band}`; arrows move in two dimensions and check; the focused cell's likelihood and consequence descriptions below (each impact area as a definition list); `Derived rating` with its badge; `Override the rating` (ghost small) revealing `Rating` (pick list of the four bands) and `Reason for the override` (required, 1 to 500, hint `Say why this rating differs from the matrix. Analysts and the people taking part see it.`); `Apply rating` (primary small) and `Cancel rating` (ghost small). Typing a level label or score into a likelihood or consequence cell converts on commit; typing a rating that differs from the derived one opens the picker with the override revealed.
- **Columns.** `risk-table-columns.ts` marks each column's kind and editability (identifier, control summary and markers read-only; exception fields absent before release); adds `mitigationControls` (`{n} {control|controls}` as a figure link to the sheet's controls group; the cell line lists `{control number} · {title} · {status}`), the two rating columns with `ratingReading`, and the computed `Decision`, `Contest points`, `Responses awaiting a decision` columns from 17.1, 17.2 and 17 facts on the read, plus `AI values to confirm` (`aiValuesToConfirm`, the count of the row's AI-proposed cells awaiting confirmation, analysts only) and `Not on the scale` (`notOnScale`, the headers of the row's off-scale fields), both offered in the chooser and hidden by default, so the Overview's figures (18-17) open exact sets (no due marker, G-16).
- **Rows and bulk actions.** Domain: `addNewRow(set)` (key `new-{n}`, `New` in the number cell, active on its statement), `duplicateRow(set, row)` (fields only: never number, controls or AI provenance), `removeNewRow`. Bar selection group: `{k} selected`, `Withdraw {k} {risk|risks}` (`Archive`) and `Reinstate {k} {risk|risks}` (`ArchiveRestore`) with X-27's required reason in a popover anchored to the bar, applying through 16-11's actions with Undo and no confirmation, not offered while the selection has unsaved changes (`Save or discard the changes to {risk numbers} first.`); `Confirm AI values ({a})` (`Check`) when the selection holds AI-proposed cells and the actor may confirm; `Duplicate` (`CopyPlus`, one row); `Clear selection`. Toolbar `Add row` (`Plus`).
- **Persistence and leaving.** `SessionDraftKind` gains `risk-table-changes`; the change set is written on every change under `{assessmentId}` inside try and catch and restored on mount when its rows still exist (dropping entries for rows that no longer exist, announced once); `useLeaveGuard` is armed while the set is non-empty, allowing navigation to the record's own routes (`/app/review/assessments/{id}` and `/risks`, with query strings) and warning elsewhere with 10 §6.3's dialog: title `Leave this assessment?`, description `You have {n} unsaved {change|changes} to risks. If you leave now, they’ll be lost.`, confirm `Leave assessment`, dismiss `Keep editing`.
- **Fixture builder.** `createRiskTableAssessment` adds its risks in one `POST /api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/risk_changes` call (creates), and gains `riskTableUatC(request)` returning ten UAT-C-like risks (D-29).
- **SC1 spec.** `describe('SC1 keyboard batch')` (desktop): a `press` wrapper counts key presses; from the grid caption, select the ten Treatment owner cells, type `Ja`, choose `Jane Citizen`, Ctrl+Enter; the ten Target date cells, type `30/11/2026`, Ctrl+Enter; the ten Treatment status cells, choose a status, Ctrl+Enter; Ctrl+S; assert at most 80 presses, exactly one `next-action` request during the save, the toast `Saved 30 changes to 10 risks.`, and the values after reload.
