## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want** an assessment's risks in a table I can edit, copy and paste like Excel or a SharePoint list, **so that** reviewing and updating many risks takes minutes rather than a long scroll.

This plan's slice: every journey Phases 12 to 17.2 built keeps working through the table. The release, AI, curation, response, acceptance and contest specs, and the post-demo journey, reach each risk through Risks and its sheet before the Overview drops its cards.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Helper.** `openRiskSheet(page, { assessmentDocumentId, riskNumber })` in `e2e/helpers/assessment-release.ts` navigates to `/app/review/assessments/{id}/risks?risk={riskNumber}` (or, when already on Risks, presses Enter on the row's Risk number) and returns the sheet's `dialog` locator named `{risk number}`; `completeRiskForRelease` takes that locator as its form scope.
- **Rule for every spec.** Replace the Overview card locators (`Identified Risks` region, the per-risk `article` or form scoped by risk number) with `openRiskSheet`; keep every assertion about what the surfaces do and say; where a step asserted the card on the Overview as a destination (for example a figure or link opening the card), it asserts the sheet opened by `?risk=`; desktop-only steps stay desktop-only; mobile steps use the phone card (18-18 supplies it), so until then they open the sheet by URL.
- **Journey.** `risk-owner-journey.spec.ts`'s analyst steps inside `sliceNominateAndRelease`, `sliceDecideAndReassess` and the 17.1 and 17.2 slices that open a risk card use `openRiskSheet`; `JOURNEY_SLICE_BUDGETS_MS` is unchanged unless a measured slice exceeds it, recorded in the summary with the measurement.
</context>
