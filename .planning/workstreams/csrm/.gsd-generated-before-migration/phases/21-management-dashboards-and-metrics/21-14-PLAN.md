## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager or leader, **I want** trends, ageing, cycle times and a heat map, **so that** I can see whether risk and the assessment process are improving.

This plan's slice: a Security Analyst sees the AI runs that failed or were interrupted in the last seven days, with which later completed on retry, and how AI assistance is doing overall, every count opening the runs behind it and none showing what the AI was given or proposed.
