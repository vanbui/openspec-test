## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager or leader, **I want** trends, ageing, cycle times and a heat map, **so that** I can see whether risk and the assessment process are improving.

This plan's slice: a Security Analyst filters the Risk register and switches it to a heat map of inherent and residual positions for exactly that set, readable without colour, and opens any cell's risks as a list.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Domain (`risk-domain.ts`).** `registerDisplays = ['table', 'heat-map']`, `parseRegisterDisplay(value)` (default `table`); `positionFilterKeys` (`inherentLikelihood`, `inherentConsequence`, `residualLikelihood`, `residualConsequence`) with labels `Inherent likelihood`, `Inherent consequence`, `Residual likelihood`, `Residual consequence`; option builders from the served scale's labels plus `not-on-scale` labelled `Not on the scale`; `exception` with `With an exception`; chip labels `{Filter}: {value}`; `stripDisplay(query)` removes `display` before any Strapi read.
- **Display (`risk-heat-map.tsx`, Server Component).** `RiskRegisterHeatMap({ heatMap, query })`: the scope line (`Risks matching: {chip labels joined by ·}.` or `All Risks in the register.`) with `{total} Risks`, then `RiskMatrixPair` with scope captions, cell hrefs `/app/risks?{same filters}&display=table&{side}Likelihood={label}&{side}Consequence={label}`, off-scale hrefs with `not-on-scale` on one axis; `available: false` renders `Not available` with the scope line; zero total renders the locked filtered empty. `RiskRegisterHeatMapSkeleton` draws two 5 × 5 inset blocks at cell size.
- **Register and page.** `risk-register.tsx` puts `Show as` first in the toolbar (links keeping the query, `aria-current` on the checked segment); in heat-map display it hides `View:`, `Columns`, density and pagination. `risks/page.tsx` parses `display`; with `heat-map` it calls `getRiskHeatMap(stripDisplay(query))` and renders the display; otherwise the list as today; 403 keeps the shipped permission state; 400 renders `listFilterRefusal` (21-10).
- **Filters.** `risk-register-filters.tsx` adds the five selects to More filters and the phone sheet (18 §11), with chips and `Clear filters`.
