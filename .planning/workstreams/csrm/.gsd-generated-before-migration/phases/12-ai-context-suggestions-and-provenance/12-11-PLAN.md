## Recorded planning decisions

- **Guard reason.** `assertMutableAssessment` keeps its predicate and now throws `AssessmentWorkflowError(409, "CSRM assessment is not mutable.", "ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE")`. `AssessmentWorkflowError` gains an optional third constructor argument `reason?: ConflictReason` (the 10-06 pattern of the other workflow errors). Every caller of the guard therefore carries the reason.
- **Controller transport.** The assessment controller's `safeError(ctx, status, message, reason?)` passes `reason ? { reason } : undefined` as the responder's second argument and adds `details` to its fallback body (10-06's pattern); every `AssessmentWorkflowError` catch passes `error.reason`. The risk and control-implementation controllers keep their bodies in this phase.
- **Transition option.** `withMutableAssessmentMutation({ …, transition?: "accepted-edit" | "none" | ((result: T) => "accepted-edit" | "none") })`, default `"accepted-edit"`. The pre-edit readiness is computed before `mutate`; `transitionForAcceptedEdit` is evaluated and written only when the resolved transition is `accepted-edit`. Only the decision workflow (12-12) passes a function; no existing caller changes.
- **Vocabulary, once.** `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`, `AI_PROPOSAL_ALREADY_DECIDED`, `AI_SUGGESTION_CONTROL_CHANGED` and `AI_ACCEPT_WITH_PLANNED_CONTROL` join `CONFLICT_REASONS` in `apps/backend/src/csrm/conflict-reasons.ts` and `apps/csrm/src/lib/api/conflict-reasons.ts`, and the `reason` enum under `AssessmentWorkflowErrorResponse.error.details` and `QuestionnaireWorkflowErrorResponse.error.details`, in the lists' sort order. They are added together because 10-06's parity tests keep the three lists equal and each later addition would touch the same five files; 12-12 produces and tests `AI_PROPOSAL_ALREADY_DECIDED` and `AI_ACCEPT_WITH_PLANNED_CONTROL`, and 12-13 `AI_SUGGESTION_CONTROL_CHANGED`.
- **Why `AI_ACCEPT_WITH_PLANNED_CONTROL`.** One reason per cause lets the family J copy name the next step. Phase 16's research proposes `AI_ACCEPT_NOT_PERMITTED` for an Accept on a High or Critical residual at acceptance; that stays Phase 16's to add, and Phase 16 reuses this reason for the planned-control condition (Phase 16 D-38 and its 2026-10-01 confirmation).
