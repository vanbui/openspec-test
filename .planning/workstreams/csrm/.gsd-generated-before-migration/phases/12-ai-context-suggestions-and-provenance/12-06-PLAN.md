This plan's slice: a draft built from the whole request that links each risk to the request's own systems and assets, uses the whole Risk Type library, derives its ratings, cites its sources and suggests controls, with every value marked as an AI proposal.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Internal proposal v2.** Root: `schemaVersion: 2`, `summary`, `summarySources`, `overallRating` (derived), `overallRiskRationale`, `overallRiskRationaleSources`, `recommendationsSummary`, `recommendationsSummarySources`, `adjustments`, `risks`. Each risk: `proposalKey` (`risk-1` …), `riskTypeName` (string or null), `issueStatement`, `riskStatement`, `riskCategory`, `riskSubCategory`, `riskCause`, `potentialImpact`, `informationSystem` (documentId or null), `informationAsset` (documentId or null), `dataClassification` (the asset's, or null), `inherentLikelihood`, `highestInherentConsequence`, `inherentRating`, `inherentRatingRationale`, `residualLikelihood`, `highestResidualConsequence`, `residualRating`, `residualRatingRationale`, `treatmentAction` (`Accept` or `Treat`), `treatmentPlan`, `sources` (the seven statement fields, each a string array), `uncitedClaims` (at most 5, each at most 300 characters), `suggestedControls` (at most 5 of `{ controlNumber, control, rationale }`, rationale at most 500 characters), `adjustments`. With no risks, `overallRating` is `Low` and release stays blocked by "At least one Risk".
- **Parser signature.** `parseAssessmentDraftProposal(value, allowed: AssessmentContextAllowedSets)`; checks closed keys, type names in `allowed.riskTypes` or null, documentIds in `allowed.systems` and `allowed.assets`, classification equal to the asset's, scale values, every rating equal to `deriveRating`, the overall rating equal to `highestRating` of the residuals, the Accept rule (Low or Moderate residual, a non-blank treatment plan and no suggested control), sources within `allowed.sourceKeys`, suggestions within `allowed.controlCandidates` and unique, the shared order and `risk-N` keys in order.
- **Wire v2 adds the adjustments:** `accept-not-permitted`, `accept-with-suggested-controls`, `sources-dropped`, `suggestions-dropped`, `suggestions-capped`. The Accept rule runs after the suggestion filter, so an Accept whose only suggestions were dropped keeps its Accept. More than five valid suggestions keep the first five; an unknown system or asset key, an extra key (for example a rating) or a scale value outside the vocabulary is `PROVIDER_OUTPUT_INVALID`.
- **Deterministic v2 rules** (`version: "2"`; each over the context records; statements of the three existing answer-driven risks kept verbatim):
  - Internet Exposure (inherent Likely × Major, residual Possible × Major): today's answer rule, or any selected system with `internetFacing`; linked to the first internet-facing system; cites the answers used and that system.
  - Personal Information Handling (Possible × Major, or Likely × Severe when the personal data types include `sensitive` or `financial`): today's answer rule, or any selected asset with `containsPersonalData`; linked to that asset with its classification.
  - A second Personal Information Handling risk on regulatory obligations (Possible × Major) when the request's regulatory requirements contain `privacy` or `personal` (any case); cites `request.regulatoryRequirements`.
  - Third-Party Dependency (Possible × Moderate = Moderate) proposing `Accept` with a rationale in the treatment plan and no suggested control (G-14): today's answer rule (the `third-party-involvement` answer is `true` or `yes`), or the request's `thirdPartyInvolvement`; cites `request.thirdPartyDetails` and the answers.
  - An untested-recovery risk with no Risk Type (Possible × Major) when the `recovery-tested` answer is `no` or `not-sure`; cites that answer except on `potentialImpact`, which cites nothing, and carries one uncited claim: `Recovery time objectives for the service are not documented.`
  - Suggestions: each typed risk proposing `Treat` suggests up to five of its type's applicable controls among the candidates (the Accept risk suggests none, G-14), by control number, rationale `Applicable to {Risk Type} risks: {control name}.`; the untyped risk suggests the first two library-shortlist candidates.
  - Summary sentence format unchanged (`Deterministic assessment assistance identified {n} reviewable risk {proposal|proposals}.`); the treatment plan for Treat reads `Review the suggested controls and record the analyst-approved treatment plan.`
- **Release fixture effect.** For `RAR-DEV-QUES-001` (answers: personal data `contact` and `identity`, hosting `cloud`, third-party providers; request: third-party involvement, regulatory requirements naming the Privacy Act) the deterministic draft has four risks in the order Internet Exposure, Personal Information Handling, Personal Information Handling, Third-Party Dependency, and the summary reads `identified 4 reviewable risk proposals`. Task 3 confirms this by running the journey and records the actual list in the summary.
- **Persistence.** The assessment stores `aiProvenance` v1 (`proposalKey: "assessment"`, fields `summary`, `overallRating`, `overallRiskRationale`, `recommendationsSummary` proposed, sources on the three text fields). Each risk stores `riskType`, `informationSystemImpacted`, `informationAssetImpacted`, `dataClassification` and the other fields, plus `aiProvenance` v1 marking `proposed` every field the proposal sets to a value, and `riskType` also when the proposal leaves it null. A helper `initialRiskAiProvenance` (and its assessment counterpart) is added to `ai-provenance.ts`'s callers here, not to the envelope module's API.
