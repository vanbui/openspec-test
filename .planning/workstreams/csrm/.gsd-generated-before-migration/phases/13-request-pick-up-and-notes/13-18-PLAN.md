## Recorded planning decisions

- **Signal block** (`ai-inputs-changed.tsx`, no `'use client'`, no hooks): `AiInputsChangedBlock({ target: "questionnaire" | "draft", requestId, signal })` renders nothing for `null`; otherwise the heading line (`--text-body` at 500 with a 16px `Sparkles`, `aria-hidden`) `AI inputs changed since this {questionnaire|draft} was generated`, a `ul` named by the heading with one item per note (a `RecordLink` `{Requestor|Security Analyst} note · {LocalInstant}` to `/app/review/requests/{requestId}#note-{noteId}`, then `Marked since` or `Unmarked since` in `--text-caption`), then `The AI hasn’t seen these changes.`. At 1440 each item is one line with ` · ` before the caption; at 375 each item stacks, the link wrapping anywhere.
- **Placement.** The provenance header (12-13) renders the block as its last block; the questionnaire card renders it above its idle action or state line from `request.aiInputs.questionnaire`, and the draft card from `request.aiInputs.assessment`, both passed down by `AssessmentDetail`.
- **Sheet group.** `ai-run-sheet.tsx` gains a `Notes` group after `Request`: `{n} {note|notes} included` and one row per `contextSummary.notes.included` entry, `{part} note · {LocalInstant} · {characters} characters`, its first part a link that closes the sheet and then navigates to the note; `None included` when empty (including v1 runs).
- **Request context lines** (`request-context-lines.tsx`, a Server Component): `RequestContextLines({ requestId, assignment, notes })` renders, in `--text-caption` / `--color-text-secondary`, `Assigned to {name}`, or `Not picked up` followed by ` · ` and the link `Pick up on the request page` to `/app/review/requests/{id}#assignment` when `pickUp === "allowed"`; then a figure link `{n} {note|notes}` with the X-17 `{k} new` to `#notes`, or `No notes`. It renders nothing when `assignment` and `notes` are null (a Requestor's read). The questionnaire and assessment pages (Server Components) render it and pass it to `AssessmentRequestLink` and the questionnaire's request relationship through a `requestContext?: ReactNode` prop, so no client module imports it.
- **Domain copy** in `ai-assistance-domain.ts`: `aiInputsChangedHeading(target)`, `aiInputsChangedItem(note)`, `aiInputsChangedCaption`, `sheetNotesHeading(n)`, `sheetNoteRow(note)`, verbatim from §10.2 and §10.3.
