## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: the assigned analyst submits the final assessment, which opens a numbered acceptance round, tells each decider, and locks every analyst edit by any route; a recall with a reason reopens editing, and the next submission voids exactly the decisions whose risks changed.

<objective>
Serve "Submit final assessment" (D-19 to D-22, D-38, D-54) and "Recall round {n}" (D-25) to the assigned analyst only, and the acceptance read for analysts and participants (D-22, D-47 as amended). Put the acceptance-round lock into Phase 16's one guard (D-23, D-24) and prove it on every analyst content route, with release-for-response readiness gaining "No acceptance round open" and at most one open round of either kind (Phase 17's seam). Publish, grant and prove the three operations (D-02, D-59, D-68).

Purpose: SC3 ("After 'Submit final assessment', Strapi refuses the analyst's edits while round 1 is open; a recall with a reason reopens editing, and in round 2 an unchanged risk's decision stands while a changed risk's decision is voided and its owner is asked again") and ASSESS-24 criteria 1 to 5.
Output: one service, one projector, three routes, two Functions, the lock and its route inventory proof.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

- **Recipients at opening.** A risk owner with an `awaiting-owner` risk gets `acceptance-requested` (`riskCount`), or `decision-voided` (`voidedCount`, `newCount`) when any of their acts was voided now, never both; an executive approver with an `awaiting-executive` risk gets `executive-approval-requested` per risk; the actor, the service identity and inactive users never.
- **Recall** `POST …/acceptance_rounds/{roundNumber}/recall`, body `{ reason }` through `parseReasonText`. In `withAssessmentLock` (serialise, transaction, lock) without the acceptance-round check: `assertAssignedAnalyst`; not editable → `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`; the actor named on a risk → the G-11 refusals; `roundNumber` not the open acceptance round → `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_NOT_OPEN`; then close it (`outcome: "recalled"`, `outcomeReason`, `closedAt`, `closedBy`), nothing on decisions or `acceptanceState`, the `recall-acceptance-round` reversal reads, and `acceptance-round-recalled` to each participant. 200 `{ data: AcceptanceRound }`.
