## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: a Security Analyst names the executive approver of each High or Critical risk, or one for all of them, and that person can never act as an analyst on the assessment; a risk that drops below High loses its approver honestly.

- **Batch** `PUT /api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/executive_approvers`, body `{ items: [{ riskId, approverId | null }] }`, 1–200 unique active risks of the assessment (a missing risk: 404 for the whole batch, Phase 16's rule; a withdrawn one: `RISK_WITHDRAWN`), all or nothing in one transaction under `withAssessmentMutation` with the accepted-edit transition and descriptor `{ target: "risk", kind: "executive-approver" }`; result `{ data: { applied: [{ riskId, approverId }], unchanged: [{ riskId }] } }` (the shape the Undo replays with the previous approvers). - **Risk update.** `executiveApprover` (a documentId or null) joins `RISK_WRITABLE_FIELDS` and the update parser in `assessment-workflow.ts`; `risk-workflow.ts` validates it through `assertEligibleExecutiveApprover` (exported from `executive-approvers.ts`, the one validation). - **Eligibility.** 17.1-02's `executiveApproverEligibility` with facts loaded in the transaction (the risk's owner, the assignee, `reviewedBy`, `peerApprover`); refusal 409 `EXECUTIVE_APPROVER_NOT_ELIGIBLE` with `ExecutiveApproverRefusalErrorResponse` details `{ riskId, eligibility }`; a risk whose current residual rating does not need one: 409 `EXECUTIVE_APPROVER_NOT_NEEDED`. `assertEligibleRiskOwner` (17-05) gains "not the risk's executive approver" (`OWNER_NOT_ELIGIBLE`).
- **Clearing.** In `updateRiskWithinMutation`, after the rating is derived: when `executiveApproverRequired(newResidualRating)` is false and an approver is named, set `executiveApprover` null in the same write; Phase 16's history event for that edit lists both field changes. - **Guard.** `assertAssessmentEditable({ assessment, actor })` order after this plan: `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`, `RESPONSE_ROUND_OPEN`, `ACTOR_IS_RISK_OWNER`, `ACTOR_IS_EXECUTIVE_APPROVER` (17.1-07 inserts `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_OPEN` after `RESPONSE_ROUND_OPEN`).
