## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: the risk manager opens the register's "Project" or "BAU" view, filters the queue and lists by type, and from any system, asset, control or Risk Type sees where it is used, each figure opening its list.

<objective>
Build the register's views, columns and new filters (D-22, D-23; X-59, X-61), the queue's and assessment list's type filters and column (D-22), and the "Used in" panels and list counts (D-24; X-65), with the register and administration specs changed deliberately.

Purpose: SC1's "Project" and "BAU" saved views and SC4's "a system's page lists its requests and open risks by residual rating" in the UI; UAT-045 and UAT-047.
Output: the register's views and filters, the queue and list filters, the Used in panel and counts, their tests and two specs.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (`risk-register-workflow.spec.ts`, `administration-workflow.spec.ts`).
</objective>
