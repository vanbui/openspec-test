The model is the Three Lines: the business owns treatment and accepts residual risk, and Security Analysts assess and own the method. The assessment follows the ISO/IEC 27005 process, and risk owners approve the treatment plan and accept residual risk (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 6.1.3 f). Headline choices:
  - a 5 × 5 matrix, with ratings as the product of the two scores banded Low, Moderate, High and Critical, and acceptance authority and review intervals by rating;
  - no hard delete of persisted risks;
  - analyst re-rating after release as a recorded review;
  - separation of duties on an analyst's own request;
  - peer approval before release when any residual risk is Critical;
  - per-file AI opt-in for attachments, under a classification ceiling;
  - control titles loaded in Phase 10;
  - notifications in the app (Phase 17), then reminders and email (Phase 20);
  - quick find with Ctrl+K or ⌘K;
  - a premium experience bar for every UI-SPEC.

  Three requirements were added: ASSESS-20, NOTIFY-02 and UX-10, making 46. Phase 17.1, "Review, peer approval and risk acceptance", was inserted. Contract-gap records are approved by the orchestrator after review (G-6).

### Phase 06.9 (complete 2026-09-23; gap-closed and re-verified 2026-09-24)

### Decisions locked this phase (D-1..D-6 in 06.9-CONTEXT.md)

- The originating request is **served, not inferred**: `/v1/risk_assessments/{id}` projects a closed `{documentId, requestNumber, title, requestStatus}` after the authorization decision. No content-type schema change — the `assessmentRequest` relation already existed.
- `requestStatus` is declared as the seven-value request-status enum rather than an open string, so `requestStatusPresentation` lookups are total. An open string would have rendered a confident label from `undefined` — the same failure class as the Phase 06.8 questionnaire `state` field.
- Link destination comes from the served `actor` discriminant, never the route: analysts to `/app/review/requests/{id}`, Requestors to `/app/requests/{id}`. `releasedAssessmentDetailForActorSchema` had been stripping `actor` in its transform and now preserves it.
- The stepper is read-only and carries no action control; it explains state and is not a second workflow-control surface.
- Queue and status filters are orthogonal — a queue choice changes which records return, not which statuses the filter offers.

### Notes carried forward

- **Strapi answers an unauthorised assessment read with 404, not 403.** That is deliberate and stronger: a permission notice would confirm to an unrelated Requestor that the record exists. The route renders not-found, and both the integration test and the browser journey assert it. Since 08-09 that is the in-shell `Page not found` (`app/(app)/not-found.tsx`), not the framework's `This page could not be found.`. Any future spec expecting a permission state on that path is asserting the wrong thing.
- **The nonce Content-Security-Policy needs every page rendered per request** (08-10). The root layout is `force-dynamic`. A page that opts into static rendering (`force-static`, fully static params, Partial Prerendering, Cache Components) would ship its scripts without the nonce and be blocked. A new third-party source goes into `src/lib/security/content-security-policy.ts` with its reason in `docs/security/web-headers.md`, and `script-src` never gains `'unsafe-inline'` or a host list. Browser proofs that watch for CSP messages use `e2e/helpers/csp.ts`: Chromium 145 words a block "… violates the following Content Security Policy directive …", so a filter on "Refused to" alone passes vacuously.
- **`instrumentation-client.ts` loads on every page, so it imports narrowly** (08-10): `config` from `zod/v4/core`, not the `z` namespace, which added 64,677 bytes gzip to every first load.
- **A `PageHeader` takes `status` for facts beside the title (badges, ratings) and `action` for controls** (08-09). Controls sit in a `display: contents` wrapper marked `data-print="hidden"` and do not print; status prints. A status badge passed as `action` would vanish from a printed page.
- **Development sign-in now has a third fixture identity, `Secondary Requestor` (`maya.requestor@idambox.com`).** It exists solely to prove record-level denial with a second authenticated Requestor; it carries a capability grant identical to `Requestor`, lives only in the development session adapter, and its seed is gated on `IDAMBOX_SEED_CSRM` plus the development identity flags. Keycloak remains untouched.
- **The IAM content types and the generic reference-data finds mount no HTTP route** (08-03, D-36 and D-44). Policies, the identity middleware and every seed reach them through the Document Service; a route or a web caller for `/idambox-users`, `/roles`, `/functions`, `/function-apis`, `/applications` or `/csrm-information-{systems,assets}` is a regression, and the inventory test and the web chain guard fail on it. The users-permissions role routes mount under `/api/users-permissions`, so `GET /api/roles` never collided.
- **Any unmounted `/api` path answers a mutation with 405 and `Allow: HEAD, GET`, and a `GET` with 404** (koa's generic answer under Strapi's defaults), so a test that expects 404 for every method on a removed route is asserting the wrong thing; assert 404 for `GET` and 404 or 405 for mutations.
- **Regenerate the served-operations inventory only through `SERVED_OPERATIONS_WRITE=1 pnpm --filter @csrm/strapi served-operations:write`** with the dev servers stopped; a run that changes only `generatedAt` should be discarded, since that field is excluded from the drift comparison.
- **Every response parser is checked at the contract's string edges** (08-04, D-05 and D-40). A parser stricter than the contract (`.trim()`, `.min()`, `.max()` without a declared bound) fails the parity guard until the bound is declared in `docs/references/specification.json` (only where the server guarantees it: documentIds, domain numbers, deterministic ids) or the path is registered in `narrowedPaths` with its reason (free-text caps). New parsers never enter `boundaryGrandfathered`; its 43 entries are the backlog and the asserted count changes only when an entry is resolved. It held 33 until the G1 gap closure (2026-09-26, `0a98c21`, `390247e`, `4eb9ffd`, `de40347`) made ten hidden pre-Phase-8 parsers visible: every registered parser now reports a rejection at the field that caused it, so no wrapper can hide a parser behind a root issue, and a narrowing names a field, never a whole payload. Since the G2 closure (`fc6a791`, `f3dc30f`) the same holds inside a question's answer envelope, and the guard fails any narrowing that hides a rejection below the path it names.
- **Every served operation and every plugin route has a row in the authorization matrix** (08-05, D-16). A plan that mounts a route adds a row to `apps/strapi/tests/fixtures/authorization-matrix.ts` stating all six outcomes. If the route mutates, the row needs a persistence getter; if an actor succeeds, the row either exercises that path or cites an existing test by file and exact title. Otherwise the suite fails with `MATRIX_ROW_MISSING_…`. Regenerate `served-operations.json` first. The forced-500 plumbing is test-only (`startAuthorizationHttpHarness({ internalErrorMiddleware: true })` and `x-csrm-test-internal-error: 1`), and a guard test fails if the sentinel ever appears under `apps/strapi/src`.
- **A Playwright run leaves the tree clean** (D-12, since 08-02). An isolated build uses `tsconfig.e2e.json`, so a run never rewrites `apps/web/tsconfig.json`, and `apps/web/next-env.d.ts` is untracked, regenerated by `next typegen` before `tsc`. `git status --porcelain` was empty after every Phase 8 run, the 08-14 gate included. A dirty tree after a run is a harness defect to fix at its source, never something to restore with `git checkout --`.
- **On the production build, streamed content is revealed after the document's `load` event.** `page.goto` resolves before a Suspense boundary's content is swapped in, so a one-shot read right after a navigation (`allTextContents()`, `count()`, `.all()`) can see nothing; assert with a retrying expectation on the destination first (`expectSettled` for scans).
- **Act only inside the asserted destination.** A `getByText` that the page being left can also satisfy (a textarea's text, a heading shared by both routes) does not prove a navigation finished; wait for the destination URL and the region you act in, then click within that region. The production server navigates fast enough to turn that race into a lost click.
- **A URL assertion is not a sign-in assertion.** `toHaveURL(//app$/)` also matches `/sign-in?returnTo=/app`, so a click that never left the sign-in page passed and the failure surfaced fifty lines later as a misleading "element not found". E2E sign-in helpers must assert the shell — the Dashboard heading and the role in the banner. Two specs' helpers had this defect; the Phase 06.9 one is fixed, and `accessibility.spec.ts` already asserted the banner correctly.
- **Read console state in a fresh browser tab.** An accumulated tab buffer surfaced a stale `ReferenceError` captured during HMR recompiles and cost a full bisect (stash wave 2, revert wave 1 files) to disprove. A clean tab showed zero errors at every stage.
- **Prefer screenshots over `get_page_text` on this app.** The reader repeatedly picked up an off-screen leftover "Loading workspace…" node from a prior route and made two healthy pages look hung.
- **Windows flake:** `request-workflow.test.tsx:48` intermittently fails with `EPERM: rename` inside vitest's temp cache when the suite runs alongside other disk-heavy work. It is the `import(/* @vite-ignore */ …)` dynamic import; it passes in isolation and in quiet full runs.
