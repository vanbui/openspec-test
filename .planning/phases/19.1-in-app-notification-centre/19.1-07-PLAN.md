## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor, a risk owner or a Security Analyst, **I want to** be told in the app when work waits for me and to open it in one step, **so that** nothing waits unseen between the people in the flow.

This plan's slice: the header shows how many notifications wait for the person, at every width. The capability, the header control with its exact count from the layout, and Phase 11's header changes that make room for it.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Capability.** `capabilities` gains `'notification:read'`; `capabilityAnchors['notification:read'] = ['GET:/api/v1/notifications', 'GET:/api/v1/notifications/summary']`; the development map adds it to all four roles.
- **Layout.** After `granted` is known, `const notificationSummary = can('notification:read', granted) ? getNotificationSummary().then((result) => (result.error ? null : result.data), () => null) : Promise.resolve(null)`, never awaited, passed to both `AppShell` renders (the permission branch passes `Promise.resolve(null)`). Type `Promise<NotificationSummary | null>` from `notifications-domain.ts`.
- **Trigger.** `components/shell/notification-trigger.tsx` (`'use client'`, no zod, no date library) exports `NotificationTrigger({ summary }: { summary: Promise<NotificationSummary | null> })`: a `button` with class `header-notifications`, a 16px `Bell` (`aria-hidden`), visually hidden text `Notifications` plus `, {n} unread` when n > 0, and inside `<Suspense fallback={null}>` a child that reads `use(summary)` and renders the capsule `<span class="notification-count" aria-hidden="true">` with `1` to `99` or `99+`. Null or 0 renders no capsule. Plan 19.1-08 adds `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, the panel and the client store to this component.
- **Shell.** `AppShellProps.notificationSummary: Promise<NotificationSummary | null>`; `AppShell` renders `{can('notification:read', granted) ? <NotificationTrigger summary={notificationSummary} /> : null}` directly after the quick-find control and before the role tag and the account menu.
- **CSS (X-33).** `.header-notifications`: outline circle at the icon-button size from 768px and the touch-target size below, `position: relative`, `--color-bg-inset` fill when `[aria-expanded="true"]`; `.notification-count`: `--radius-full`, `--color-primary` fill, `--color-on-primary` text, `--text-caption` at 500, `tabular-nums`, min 16px high and wide, 4px horizontal padding, 2px `--color-bg-page` border, placed 4px outside the circle's top end corner (`inset-inline-end`); below 768px the header gap is 8px and every header control, including the `Menu` trigger, is an icon-only circle at the touch-target size with its label in `.sr-only`.
- **Phone fit and the longest persona.** UI-SPEC §1.3's arithmetic used the Secondary Requestor (19 characters) as the longest role, but Phase 13 adds `Second Security Analyst` (23 characters, about 209px as a tag), which needs about 22px more than 375px offers beside three 44px circles. The role tag therefore keeps Phase 11's ellipsis truncation whenever the row lacks room (it shrinks first; the full text stays in the DOM): at 375px the Requestor, Secondary Requestor and Security Analyst tags are not truncated, and the Second Security Analyst tag may truncate; no width from 320 to 1440 scrolls horizontally. Recorded for the orchestrator as a UI-SPEC §1.3 correction.
- **Icons.** In `navigation.ts`, the analyst profile's `Alerts` item uses `AlarmClock` instead of `Bell`; nothing else changes.
