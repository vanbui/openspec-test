## Recorded planning decisions

- **Actions.** `markAllNotificationsReadAction(input: unknown)` (zod `.strict()`: `asOf` an ISO instant) and `undoMarkAllNotificationsAction(input: unknown)` (`markedAt` an ISO instant), each calling `markNotifications` with the `{ all: true, asOf }` or `{ undo: true, markedAt }` form and returning the mark result; no revalidation.
- **Marks hook.** `notification-marks.tsx` (`'use client'`): `useMarkAllWithUndo({ items, asOf, onApplied })` applies read to every shown unread item and a −n overlay at once, calls the action, settles with the returned `unread` and `asOf`, and when `changed > 0` shows the X-14 toast `Marked {n} notification as read` or `Marked {n} notifications as read` with `Undo`; Undo applies unread to the same items and +n at once, calls the undo action with `markedAt`, and confirms `Marked {n} notification as unread` or `… notifications as unread`; failures roll back with T3 or T4. `useMarkNotification()` marks one item read or unread with the overlay, a polite status `Marked as read` or `Marked as unread`, rollback with T1 or T2, and T6 with removal on 404 (used by the page in 19.1-10).
- **Refresh points.** In `NotificationCountProvider`: a `usePathname()` effect that skips the first render and, on each change, calls `getNotificationSummaryAction()` single-flight (a change while one is in flight queues one more, never more), adopting by `asOf` and calling `fail()` on an error; a `window` listener for `NOTIFICATIONS_STALE_EVENT` that triggers the same re-read. No `setInterval`, `setTimeout` loop or polling. `notifications-domain.ts` exports `NOTIFICATIONS_STALE_EVENT = 'csrm:notifications-stale'` and `signalNotificationsMayHaveChanged()`, which dispatches it.
- **Notes thread.** `notes-thread.tsx` calls `signalNotificationsMayHaveChanged()` after `markRequestNotesReadAction` resolves `ok`; nothing else in the thread changes (Phase 13 UI-D15 keeps the read silent).
