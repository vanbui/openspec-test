## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: every surface that says whose turn it is tells the truth during the response stage. The lifecycle shows the response step, the holder knows when the work waits on a risk owner, the queues and Homes follow, and nobody's Home says "With the Security Analyst" while the work waits on someone else.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Facts.** `deriveRequestLifecycle` takes `responseRounds: { openNumber: number | null; closedCount: number; requestorSubmitted: boolean; outstandingNames: string[]; undecidedCount: number }` (from the request's assessment; empty when none). The step `{ key: "released-for-response", label: "Released for response" }` is inserted after `assessment-drafted`. Its state: `current` when `openNumber` is set; `completed` when no round is open and `closedCount > 0`; `skipped` when no round ever opened and the highest milestone is beyond it; `future` otherwise. With a round open the current step is `released-for-response`; otherwise the existing highest-milestone rule decides, so exactly one step is current.
- **Holder.** `holderFor(lifecycle)` (from 11-08's `holderFor(key)`; callers switched): while a round is open, `requestor` when the Requestor's response is not submitted, `participants` when it is and another participant's is not, `analyst` when all are; otherwise the existing rule. `RequestHolder` gains `participants`. The My requests SQL holder adds the same rule with `EXISTS` over `csrm_round_responses` joined to the open round (link tables from metadata), and the equivalence test gains every round case. `byHolder.participants` and the `holder` filter value `participants` follow.
- **Sentences (17-UI-SPEC §14.4, verbatim).** Requestor: `Respond to round {n}.` (href `/app/assessments/{assessmentId}#your-response`), `Waiting for {names} to respond to round {n}.`, `{name} is reviewing the responses to round {n}.`, `{name} is deciding the responses and updating your risk assessment.`; analyst: `Waiting for {names} to respond to round {n}.`, `Close round {n} and decide the responses.` (href `/app/review/assessments/{assessmentId}#response-round`), `Decide {u} {proposal|proposals} from round {n}.` (href to the first undecided risk's `#risk-{id}-responses`). `{names}`: one name, `A and B`, or `{k} participants` for three or more; an unpicked request uses `A Security Analyst` (13 §12.1).
- **Queue.** `selectReviewQueueBucket`: an open round with any response not submitted → `awaiting-requestor`; an open round with all submitted → `requires-attention`; otherwise unchanged. `applyBucket` adds the equal SQL; the bucket oracle test gains both cases. The queue option and the `Queue:` line label for `awaiting-requestor` become `Awaiting the business` (UI-D15) wherever 11-06 and 13-12 render it.
- **Contract.** `AssessmentRequestLifecycleStep.state` gains `skipped`; every `holder` enum (next action, My requests item, filter) gains `participants`; `MyRequestHolderCounts` gains `participants`. Web: `request-schema.ts` and `request-domain.ts` (`requestHolders`, the step state) change in the same commit.
- **Stepper (X-41).** A `skipped` step renders as a future step with its label and the caption `Not used` in `--text-caption` / `--color-text-secondary` with a 12px `SkipForward`; the compact form counts it (`Step {n} of 9`); the heading reads `Current lifecycle state: Released for response` while it is current.
- **Homes and lists (17-UI-SPEC §14.2, §14.4).** Requestor Home: `With the Security Analyst` becomes `With others` (`Hourglass`), description `Requests a Security Analyst or another participant is working on.`, items holder `participants` first then `analyst`, up to five, line 3 the server sentence; footer links `View {a} with the Security Analyst` → `/app/requests?holder=analyst` and `View {p} with other participants` → `/app/requests?holder=participants`, each when its count is above zero; cleared `No requests with others` · `Requests appear here while a Security Analyst or another participant works on them.`; the partial fixture keeps its selector. Analyst `Your own requests` gains the row `With other participants` {n} → `/app/requests?holder=participants`. My requests' holder line and `Show` select gain `With other participants` between `Needs your action` and `With the Security Analyst`. Phase 13's assign picker's no-eligible line becomes `Only active Security Analysts who didn’t raise this request and own none of its risks can be assigned.` (17-UI-SPEC U6; the rule is 17-06's).
