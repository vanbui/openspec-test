## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: the whole flow proven in the browser at both widths, with an unrelated Requestor refused at every step, as the first slice of the post-demo journey that Phases 17.1, 17.2 and 20 extend, and the phase's full gate.
