## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: participants tell the analyst which controls they have or plan. Each writes one response per round on the risks they may respond on, autosaved and never lost, submits it once, and everyone taking part reads the submitted responses.

<objective>
Serve the participant's own response (D-31 to D-35, D-37): read, save and submit, with the draft envelope parser and completeness rules as a pure module, the participation rule per item (D-32), items created only at submission, and the rounds read with every submitted response (D-36). Seed the next round from a not-submitted draft (D-34). Refuse everyone else (D-24) and the service identity (D-32). Publish, grant and prove the four operations (D-02, D-64).

Purpose: ASSESS-12 criteria 1 and 3, ASSESS-11 criterion 2's "edit only their responses", and SC3's "the Requestor adds two mitigating controls to a risk … and submits". Notifications are 17-12's; decisions 17-13's.
Output: one pure module, one service, four operations with their contract chain, and carry-forward.

Edits Strapi source? Yes (dev servers stopped). Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Pure module (`response-drafts.ts`).** Bounds from 17-01 as exported constants (`RESPONSE_CONTROLS_PER_RISK_MAX = 10`, `RESPONSE_TITLE_MAX_LENGTH = 200`, `RESPONSE_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH = 2000`, `RESPONSE_REASON_MAX_LENGTH = 500`, `RESPONSE_ITEMS_MAX = 500`, `RESPONSE_DRAFT_MAX_BYTES = 262144`, `PLANNED_DUE_DATE_MAX_YEARS = 5`). `parseResponseDraft(value)`: the closed draft v1 envelope; strings trimmed; any key outside it, a wrong type, an over-long text, more than 10 controls on a risk, a duplicate control key or risk id, or more than the item or byte bound → null (the save answers 400); incomplete controls are accepted. `responseCompletenessIssues(draft, { permittedRiskIds, todayUtc })` returns `{ riskId, key?, field }[]`: a control without `title` or `ownerId`; `Planned` without a `dueDate` or with one before today (UTC) or more than five years ahead; `Existing` without `evidence`; a proposed owner without a reason; and, when the draft holds no item, `{ field: "nothingToAdd" }` unless `nothingToAdd` is true. `responseDraftToItems(draft)` maps to item inputs in risk then entry order (`proposed-control`, then `proposed-owner`, then `comment`) with `position`. `carryForwardDraft(previous, { permittedRiskIds, eligibleOwnerIds })` keeps only permitted risks and clears ineligible owner ids. `emptyResponseDraft()`.
- **Service (`round-responses.ts`).** Caller resolution: the viewer from `ctx.state`; `findReadableAssessment` with `{ kind: "participant" }`; not a participant → 403 `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` when `csrmAuthorization.humanAnalyst` is true, else 404. `getMyResponse`: the viewer's response in the open round, else in the latest round, else `{ round: null, … }`; returns `{ round: { number, openedAt, closedAt } | null, status, savedAt, submittedAt, nothingToAdd, carriedFromRound, draft, permittedRiskIds }`. `saveMyResponse({ expectedSavedAt, draft })` and `submitMyResponse({ expectedSavedAt })`: `withRequestLock` then the assessment lock; the open round (else 409 `RESPONSE_ROUND_NOT_OPEN`); the viewer's response in it (a person not asked at opening has none → 404); `submitted` → 409 `RESPONSE_ALREADY_SUBMITTED`; `savedAt` must equal `expectedSavedAt` (both null on the first save) else 409 `RESPONSE_CHANGED`; every risk id an active risk of the assessment (else 404 for the whole save) and `mayRespondOn` (else 403 `RESPONSE_NOT_PERMITTED`); every control owner `isEligiblePerson` and every proposed owner `assertEligibleRiskOwner` (409). Save writes `draft`, `status: "draft"`, `savedAt` now. Submit runs the completeness rules (409 `RESPONSE_INCOMPLETE` with `issues`), creates one `CsrmResponseItem` per item input (with `round`, `assessment`, `risk`, `position`, `kind`, `proposal` item v1), sets `status: "submitted"`, `submittedAt`, `nothingToAdd`, and keeps `draft` frozen. `listResponseRounds`: every round newest first, each with its submitted responses (attributed with the responder's display name, `parts` from `partsAtOpening`, `former: true` when the person is no longer a participant), items in `position` order with owner display names resolved through `resolvePeople`, and `decision` null until 17-13 fills it; participants never receive items on withdrawn risks.
- **Carry-forward.** `openResponseRound` (17-09) seeds each new `requested` response with `carryForwardDraft` of the same person's `not-submitted` draft from the previous round, and sets `carriedFromRound`.
- **Contract.** Paths `GET /v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/response_rounds` (`response-rounds/list-response-rounds`), `GET` and `PUT /v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/my_response` (`response-rounds/get-my-response`, `response-rounds/save-my-response`), `POST /v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/my_response/submit` (`response-rounds/submit-my-response`); components `ResponseDraft`, `ResponseDraftRisk`, `ResponseDraftControl`, `MyResponse`, `MyResponseResponse`, `MyResponseSaveRequest`, `MyResponseSubmitRequest`, `ResponseRoundsView`, `ResponseRoundView`, `SubmittedResponse`, `ResponseItemView`, `ResponseItemDecisionView` (nullable on the item); errors 400, 403 (`AssessmentWorkflowErrorResponse` with `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` or `RESPONSE_NOT_PERMITTED`), 404, 409 (`AssessmentWorkflowErrorResponse` or `ResponseIncompleteErrorResponse`). Routes registered with `…/my_response/submit` before `…/my_response`. Catalogue: `function.response_rounds.read_api` (`Response Rounds Read API`, `role.security_analyst` and `role.requestor`, `.api.01` `List Response Rounds`) and `function.response_rounds.my_response_api` (`My Response API`, `role.requestor`, `.api.01` `Get My Response`, `.api.02` `Save My Response`, `.api.03` `Submit My Response`); the four literals join `SERVICE_IDENTITY_REFUSED_OPERATIONS` and its mirror.
- **Matrix.** Rounds read on the participant fixture: owner 200, other 404, analyst 200, serviceAgent 403, riskOwner 200, noIdentity 403. `my_response` read, save and submit on the participant fixture: owner (Jane, already submitted) 200 for the read and 409 for save and submit, other 404, analyst (Bob, not a participant) 403, serviceAgent 403, riskOwner 200 for read and save (the save probe sends their current `savedAt`), noIdentity 403; persistence guards snapshot responses and items.
