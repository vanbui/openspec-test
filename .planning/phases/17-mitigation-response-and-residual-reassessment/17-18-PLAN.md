## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or a nominated risk owner, **I want to** see the assessment once it is released for response and tell the analyst which controls we have or plan, and as a Security Analyst I want to nominate each risk's owner and have the AI reassess residual risk from the responses, **so that** the final assessment reflects the business's real mitigations and the people who must accept it have seen it.

This plan's slice: a risk owner finds the assessment without being sent a link. "My risk assessments" lists it with their part and stage, the sidebar counts what waits for them, and Home puts the awaited response first.

<objective>
Build the list and landing surfaces of the participant read (D-28): "My risk assessments" with stage, part, filters and empty states (17-UI-SPEC §14.1), the sidebar count of awaited responses (§14.2; X-10), the Requestor Home's awaited responses in "Needs your action" (§14.2; UI-D13), and the analyst lists' stage line (§14.3; D-06; UI-D17), changing the specs that assert the old heading and description.

Output: the list page, the count, the Home section and the analyst stage line, with their tests and the changed specs.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (three changed specs).
</objective>
