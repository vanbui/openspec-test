## Recorded planning decisions

- **State.** `manualStartStateFor(strapi, { kind, request, questionnaire?, viewer })` returns `ManualStartState` (22-01): `available` and `reason` from `manualStartAvailability` (a `because` of `record-exists` or `job-running` reads `available: false`, `reason: null`); `blockedBy` from 13's order for the viewer (`OWN_REQUEST`, `REQUEST_UNASSIGNED`, `NOT_ASSIGNED_ANALYST`, else null); `viewerMayStart = available && blockedBy === null`; `assignee` `{ documentId, displayName }` or null. `aiPolicy.manualStart` = `{ questionnaire, assessmentDraft }`, each null when its card is not at its lifecycle point.
- **Facts.** The analyst `generation` block of the questionnaire read gains `manualStart: { by: { documentId, displayName }, at, reason } | null` from the newest `started-without-ai` governance entry; the assessment's from `aiProvenance.start`. Neither is projected to Requestors or participants.
- **Contract.** `AiPolicyStatus.manualStart` with `ManualStartState` (`reason` enum `AI_CLASSIFICATION_ABOVE_CEILING`, `AI_POLICY_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `GENERATION_FAILED`, `GENERATION_INTERRUPTED`, `CONTEXT_TOO_LARGE`); `ManualStartFact` on both analyst `generation` components; the web parsers in `assessment-schema.ts` (jobs and assessment) and `questionnaire-schema.ts`, closed.
