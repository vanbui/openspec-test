## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** have a sectioned sidebar and a Home that leads with my next action, **so that** I always know where my work is and what to do next.

This plan's slice: every sidebar label is the heading of the page it opens, so a person who clicks "Requests" lands on "Requests".

<objective>
Introduce one heading string per navigation destination (`page-headings.ts`) and make the navigation label, the `PageHeader` title and the route title read it (D-09, UI-SPEC §2.1). Rename the headings that differ today, repeat them in each route's loading and error boundary, change the visible "Risk Register" strings to sentence case (§2.2), and update the copy that names those pages (§2.3, including Phase 10's gate copy). Change every unit and E2E assertion that names an old heading, label or title in this plan (D-56; UI-SPEC §14.1 E10 to E12).

Purpose: UAT-002 (labels and headings disagreed) and UX-07 criterion 2.
Output: the heading module, the renamed headings and titles across nine destinations, the copy changes, the label-equals-heading proofs in unit and E2E.

Edits Strapi? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (the changed specs, then the full suite: headings and labels appear in most journeys). Alone in wave 4 (D-05).
</objective>
