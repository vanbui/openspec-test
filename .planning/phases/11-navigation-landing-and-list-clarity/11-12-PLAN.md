## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** have a sectioned sidebar and a Home that leads with my next action, **so that** I always know where my work is and what to do next.

This plan's slice: the Requestor's Home leads with what needs them, apart from what is with the Security Analyst, on a phone's first screen; the analyst's Home ends with their own requests.

<objective>
Rebuild the Requestor Home (D-19 to D-24) on `GET /v1/my_requests`'s holder: Needs your action, With the Security Analyst and Released assessments, each bounded with its total and an equal list link (D-35), with its five states and the new partial fixture (D-23). Add the analyst Home's "Your own requests" panel (D-18 item 7). Retire the old Requestor dashboard and its last core-list read, move the shared widget frame into `features/home`, and change the E2E assertions on the old dashboard (UI-SPEC §14.1 E6, E14 to E17).

Purpose: UAT-003 (the split by stored status misled), UAT-004 (on a phone the action panel was below seven tiles), and UX-08 criteria 1 and 2.
Output: the Requestor Home modules and tests, the analyst panel, the shared frame, the fixture selector, the regenerated served-operations inventory, the Home proofs at 375 and 1440.

Edits Strapi source? No (only the generated `served-operations.json` is regenerated, with the dev servers stopped). Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (the Home specs, then the full suite). Alone in wave 12 (D-05).
</objective>
