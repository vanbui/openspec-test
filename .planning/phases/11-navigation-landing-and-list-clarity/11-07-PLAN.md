**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** have a sectioned sidebar and a Home that leads with my next action, **so that** I always know where my work is and what to do next.

This plan's slice: assessment lists identify each assessment by its request's title and show how much risk it holds, and anyone looking for an assessment finds it by the request's title.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Server.** `assertCollectionKeys` allows `overallRating`, `approvedFrom`, `approvedTo`; each is parsed closed (rating in the ladder; dates matching `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$` and real calendar dates; `approvedFrom` ≤ `approvedTo` when both are present, else 400). Predicates: `q` → `$or` of `assessmentNumber`, `summary` and `assessmentRequest.title` `$containsi`; `overallRating` → `$eq`; `approvedFrom` → `approvalDate $gte {from}T00:00:00.000Z`; `approvedTo` → `approvalDate $lt {to + 1 day}T00:00:00.000Z`. The owner scope's released predicate is still pushed for `scope === 'owner'`, so filters only narrow.
- **Aggregate.** In the assessment controller, after the page is read, one knex query over `csrm_risks_assessment_lnk as l` joined to `csrm_risks as r` where `l.csrm_assessment_id IN (<page ids>)`, grouped by `l.csrm_assessment_id`, selecting `COUNT(DISTINCT r.id)` and `MAX(CASE r.residual_rating WHEN 'Low' THEN 1 WHEN 'Moderate' THEN 2 WHEN 'High' THEN 3 WHEN 'Critical' THEN 4 END)`; counts read through `Number()` (PostgreSQL returns strings). `projectAssessmentSummary(document, riskSummary)` maps the rank back to the rating name; no row means `riskCount: 0`, `highestResidualRating: null`.
- **Web.** `collectionQuerySchema` gains `overallRating` (optional, catch undefined), `approvedFrom` and `approvedTo` (optional `YYYY-MM-DD`, catch undefined); `listAnalystAssessments` and `listMyReleasedAssessments` send them. The analyst page and `/app/assessments` parse them from the URL.
- **Picker.** `listRiskRegisterAssessments(search?: string)` sends `{ q: search, pageSize: 25 }` to `GET /v1/risk_assessments`; `parseRiskPickerPayload('assessments', …)` labels `{assessmentNumber} · {requestTitle}` (X-4), or the number when the title is null; the action stops filtering assessments on the client and returns the server's first 20. The chip's label is the Risk register's server-side `selectedLabels`, changed in plan 11-15.
