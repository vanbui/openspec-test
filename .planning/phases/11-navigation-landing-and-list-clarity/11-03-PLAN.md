## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** have a sectioned sidebar and a Home that leads with my next action, **so that** I always know where my work is and what to do next.

This plan's slice: on a phone the analyst's Home opens on their queue, its activity reads in local time, and every recovery link names Home.

<objective>
Reorder the analyst Home to lead with the queue, deadlines and alerts (D-18) and set its tiles two by two below xl so the queue is on the first 375px screen (UI-D20). Render Recent activity dates through Phase 10's `LocalInstant` (D-55). Then retire "workspace" from the product's copy (D-25, UI-SPEC §2.4): recovery links say `Go to Home`, the generic loading and error states name no page, the permission description names Home, and the interface copy guard scans the state and not-found files for the term (§11.2).

Purpose: UAT-001's second half (on a phone the analyst saw tiles, not the queue) and the D-25 copy that would otherwise point at a page that no longer exists.
Output: the reordered analyst Home, local Recent activity, the state copy, the guard extension, and the changed unit and E2E assertions (§14.1 E6 and E7).

Edits Strapi? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (the changed specs, then the full suite, because the recovery copy appears across many journeys). Alone in wave 3 (D-05).
</objective>
