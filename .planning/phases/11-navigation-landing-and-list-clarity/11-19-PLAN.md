## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** have a sectioned sidebar and a Home that leads with my next action, **so that** I always know where my work is and what to do next.

This plan's slice: anyone can press Ctrl+K or ⌘K, type a number or a word of a title, and open the record they may read.

<objective>
Build quick find (D-47 to D-52, X-7, X-8): the header search control and the shortcut, a lazily loaded dialog on the source-owned shadcn `Command` (cmdk 1.1.1, already installed), one validated Server Action that runs the viewer's existing list searches in parallel, the debounced single-flight client, grouped options with totals and "Show all", and opening a result through Phase 10's navigation guard's new programmatic entry point. Plan 11-20 completes the states, the phone sheet, focus rules and the scope and accessibility proofs.

Purpose: UX-10 and G-5; UAT-048 (records could not be found by title).
Output: the domain, action, dialog and trigger modules with unit tests; the guard's `navigate`; the shell wiring and header CSS; the first browser journey.

Edits Strapi? No. Changes the specification? No (every read is an existing operation through an existing service file, so `served-operations.json` is unchanged). Runs Playwright? Yes. Alone in wave 19 (D-05).
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Reads (Planner's Discretion).** Each list is read at page 1 with page size 10 (the smallest size every list accepts; the Risk register allows only 10, 25 or 50) and the action keeps the first five with the served total: Requestor `listMyRequests({ q, …, pageSize: 10 })`, `listMyQuestionnaires({ q, …, pageSize: 10 })`, `listMyReleasedAssessments({ q, …, pageSize: 10 })`; Security Analyst `listAssessmentRequests({ q, queue: 'all', …, pageSize: 10 })`, `listQuestionnairesForReview({ q, …, pageSize: 10 })`, `listAnalystAssessments({ q, …, pageSize: 10 })`, `listRiskRegister({ q, …, pageSize: 10 })` (only with `risk:manage`) and `listMyRequests`. A group whose read fails returns `status: 'failed'` with no options; a 403 group is omitted.
- **Option fields (UI-SPEC §4.3).** Request `{requestNumber} · {title}`, line 2 its status label (`statusLabel('request', …)`, so `Released` for approved), href `/app/review/requests/{id}` or `/app/requests/{id}` by group; questionnaire `{questionnaireNumber} · {title}` with its status label, href by group; assessment `{assessmentNumber} · {requestTitle}` with its status label; Risk `{riskNumber} · {statement}` with `Residual rating: {rating|Not rated} · {Released|Under assessment}`, href `/app/risks/{id}`.
- **Single flight.** The dialog keeps `latestTerm`, `inFlight` and `pendingTerm`; after the debounce it calls the action only when nothing is in flight, otherwise stores `pendingTerm`; on return it applies the result only if its term equals `latestTerm`, then dispatches `pendingTerm` if it differs.
- **Shortcut and focus.** `QuickFindTrigger` registers one `keydown` listener on `window` (capture phase) for `(event.ctrlKey || event.metaKey) && event.key.toLowerCase() === 'k'`, skipping `event.isComposing` and `event.repeat`, and skipping while another `[role="dialog"]` or `[role="alertdialog"]` other than quick find is open; on open it stores `document.activeElement` for plan 11-20's focus return.
- **Header CSS (X-7).** `.header-search` (outline pill from 768px, icon-only circle below at the touch-target size) and `.shortcut-key` (`kbd`: `--text-micro`, `--color-text-secondary`, 1px `--color-border-default`, `--radius-sm`, 4px horizontal padding) in `globals.css`; the role tag may truncate below 375px with an ellipsis (UI-D10).
