## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** have a sectioned sidebar and a Home that leads with my next action, **so that** I always know where my work is and what to do next.

This plan's slice: the request's next action says who holds the work, in words that name them, so the Requestor knows whether to act or wait.

<objective>
Deliver D-02 item 5: the lifecycle's `nextAction` gains `holder`, and `request-lifecycle.ts`'s sentences name the holder (D-20, D-21, D-27, UI-SPEC §6.1), through the specification, the closed web parser and the hub's link rule. The holder also feeds My requests and both Homes (plans 11-10 to 11-12) and the phone stepper (plan 11-09).

Purpose: UAT-003's wording ("the other party") and UX-08 criterion 2's "naming who holds each item".
Output: the holder and sentences in Strapi with unit and integration proofs; the specification and client; the parser and hub; the browser sentence.

Edits Strapi? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes, alone in its wave (D-05). Runs Playwright? Yes (one spec).
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Sentences (UI-SPEC §6.1, verbatim, with the typographic apostrophe).** Requestor at `submitted`: `The Security Analyst is reviewing your request and will send you a questionnaire to answer.`; `questionnaire-generated`: `The Security Analyst is checking your questionnaire before sending it to you.`; `questionnaire-submitted`: `The Security Analyst is reviewing your answers to assess the request’s risk.`; `assessment-drafted`: `The Security Analyst is preparing your risk assessment.`; `assessment-reviewed`: `The Security Analyst is finalising your risk assessment before releasing it to you.` Analyst at `draft`: `The Requestor hasn’t submitted this request yet.`; `questionnaire-released`: `The Requestor is answering the questionnaire.` Every verb-first sentence and `No action is required.` are unchanged.
- **Holder.** `holderFor(currentKey)` is a module-level map in `request-lifecycle.ts` exported for plan 11-10's equivalence test; `projectRequestLifecycle` sets `nextAction = { actor, holder, summary, href? }`; a closed request keeps `nextAction: null`.
- **Hub rule.** `request-hub.tsx` renders the summary as a `RecordLink` only when `nextAction.holder === nextAction.actor` and `nextAction.href` is present; otherwise plain text. Phase 10's `nextActionOverride` still replaces the summary as plain text.
