## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** contest anything in an assessment I am asked to accept and see each point answered, and as a Security Analyst I want to rework the whole assessment and resubmit it, **so that** acceptance rests on content both lines have challenged and agreed.

This plan's slice: a risk owner or executive approver with an open point of their own sends the assessment back; the round closes as returned through the path recall already uses, and the analyst can change everything again.

<objective>
Serve "Send back" (D-02 item 6, D-11) to current risk owners and executive approvers with an open contest point of their own, closing the acceptance round as `returned` and reopening the whole assessment through one function shared with 17.1's recall (17.1 D-25; orchestrator constraint), with every refusal (D-19) and the one-transaction proof. Move recall onto the shared path and prove that one path writes every early close (the assumption-delta invariant). Publish, grant and prove the operation (D-20, D-22).

Purpose: ASSESS-26 criterion 1 and criterion 5's send-back refusals, SC1's "sends the assessment back; round 1 closes as 'returned'", SC5's "the Requestor who owns no risk can raise a point but not send back; the analyst cannot send back".
Output: one operation, the shared reopening path, one Function API, a matrix row, the parser change and two test files.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Send back** `POST:/api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/acceptance_rounds/{roundNumber}/return` (`CSRM_ACCEPTANCE_OPERATIONS.returnAcceptanceRound`, operationId `return-acceptance-round`), body `{ message?: string | null }` (trimmed; empty reads as null; at most `SEND_BACK_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH`). `returnAcceptanceRound({ strapi, assessmentDocumentId, roundNumber, message, viewer })` runs in `withAssessmentLock` (no editable guard: the round lock would refuse it): `loadContestViewer`; `loadOpenAcceptanceRound` in `trx`; the sender's own open points in that round (`raisedBy` the viewer, `round` the open round, `status` `open`); `sendBackDecision`; then `closeAcceptanceRoundAndReopen`. 200 `{ data: AcceptanceRound }`.
- **The shared path** `closeAcceptanceRoundAndReopen({ strapi, trx, assessment, round, outcome: "recalled" | "returned", actor, actorParts, text })`, exported from `acceptance-rounds.ts` and called only inside an open `withAssessmentLock` transaction: (1) update the round with `outcome`, `outcomeReason` (`text`), `closedAt` (the transaction's clock), `closedBy`, and `closedByParts` `{ schemaVersion: 1, parts }` on `returned` only; (2) write nothing on any decision row, `acceptanceState` or review fact (review currency goes stale through 17.1-02's `latestAcceptanceRoundClosedAt`); (3) the reversal reads 17.1-07's recall runs today (its `NOTIFICATION_REVERSAL_READS` entry), for both outcomes; (4) `notify` `acceptance-round-recalled` to each participant on `recalled` (17.1's behaviour, unchanged); `returned` gains its notification in 17.2-09. `recallAcceptanceRound` keeps its own checks (assigned analyst, reason) and calls the shared path for the close.
- **Read and parser.** `projectAcceptanceRound` adds `closedByParts` (array of parts, or null); the specification's `AcceptanceRound.outcome` enum gains `returned` and the schema gains `closedByParts`; `ReturnAcceptanceRoundRequest` is new. The web parser in `acceptance-schema.ts` accepts both (06.9 lesson: the parser changes with the shape).
- **Function** `function.contest_points.raise_api` `.api.05` in both catalogues; the literal in `SERVICE_IDENTITY_REFUSED_OPERATIONS`; one matrix row.
- **Forced failure.** The test makes a step after the close throw through the service's test seam, in the style of `questionnaire-workflow.integration.test.ts`'s rollback case, and asserts the round, the assessment and every notification row deep-equal to before.
- **Invariant (assumption delta, accepted).** `acceptance-round-close.unit.test.ts` scans `apps/backend/src` and asserts that `"recalled"` and `"returned"` are written to a round's `outcome` only inside `closeAcceptanceRoundAndReopen`, and that `recallAcceptanceRound` and `returnAcceptanceRound` each call it.
