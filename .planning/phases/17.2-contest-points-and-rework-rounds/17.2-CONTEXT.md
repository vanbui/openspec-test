## Phase Boundary

While an acceptance round is open (Phase 17.1), any participant contests anything they can read with a contest point: a reason, a reply thread and an analyst's answer, then resolved or withdrawn by its raiser. A risk owner or executive approver sends the assessment back, closing the round as "returned" and reopening all of it. The analyst reworks the assessment, answers every point, marks it reviewed again and resubmits. In round n+1 each decider sees which decisions stand and, for each voided one, what changed. One history view shows the rounds, decisions, voids, points, replies and answers, with who and when. People are told in the app. The phase answers PD-3 (with 17.1's recall and voids), closes no UAT finding, and inserts the post-demo journey's third slice.

### In scope
- Contest points: targets and snapshots, the thread, statuses, raising, replies, answers, resolution and withdrawal (D-01 to D-10).
- Sending back, readiness, and how a return reads in the stage, lifecycle, holder and queues (D-11 to D-13).
- The before-and-after comparison and the history view (D-14, D-15).
- Reads, notifications, separation of duties, grants, fixtures, interface and tests, including the third slice of `apps/csrm/e2e/risk-owner-journey.spec.ts` (D-16 to D-28).

### Out of scope
- Rounds, recall, the decision record, fingerprints, voids, and release and close: Phase 17.1 builds them, and this phase uses them.
- Treatment tracking, reminders and email: Phase 20 (G-16, G-18).
- AI on contest points (D-21), files on points, and the contest record in the export and the A4 report: deferred.
- Any stored status value on the assessment or request, a new IdamBox Role, Keycloak, and live AI in any suite.

### Scope and contract boundary

     - who and where: `assessment`, `round` (the acceptance round it was raised in, Phase 17's `CsrmAssessmentRound`), `number` (per assessment from 1, never reused), `raisedBy`, `raiserParts` (the parts held at raising, as Phase 17 D-01 records responders' parts) and `raisedAt`;

     - the cached `status` (D-05).
  2. **`CsrmContestEntry`:** one immutable row per act on a point: `point`, `sequence` (per point), `kind` (`reply`, `answer`, `resolved`, `withdrawn` or `undo`), `author`, `authorParts`, `body`, `answerOutcome` (`changed` or `kept`, on answers) and `postedAt`.
  3. **Phase 17's round entity, as 17.1 D-01 item 3 amends it:** the outcome `returned`, with the sender's parts. The optional message goes in 17.1's `outcomeReason`.

They compete with no JDL entity (catalogue design note 6; G-15: "their own recorded concept"). **Reversibility:** one-way. Two tables and a round vocabulary value hold the record of who challenged what and how it was answered.

  3. Reply.
  4. Answer.
  5. Resolve, withdraw and undo.
  6. Send back: `POST …/acceptance_rounds/{number}/return`, mirroring 17.1's recall path.
  7. The history: `GET …/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/history`.
  8. Reads that change shape (D-13, D-14, D-16): submission and release-and-close readiness, the participant projection, the analyst detail, 17.1's rounds-and-decisions read (the void comparisons), the lifecycle, the holder, the queue, "My risk assessments" and the analyst assessment list.
  9. Notification kinds go through Phase 19.1's service, with no new operation.
  10. ### Contest points

- **D-04:** The point and its target (G-15; COLLAB-04 criterion 1).
  - **Targets:** exactly G-15's list: an assessment field, a whole risk, one risk field, or a control implementation.
    - **Field vocabulary:** derived from the one participant projection allowlist (Phase 17 D-25 as 17.1 D-47 widens it, with the recommendation, the owner and the approver). Each rating is one target with its likelihood, consequence and rationale, and the overall rating is one target with its rationale. A field the raiser cannot read can never be targeted.
    - **Not targets:** the request summary, the questionnaire, notes, responses, decisions or another point. A disagreement with a decision is raised on its risk (17.1 D-29).
  - **Refusals:** a withdrawn risk, a record of another assessment, or an unknown id is 404. A field outside the vocabulary is 400.
  - **`targetSnapshot`:** the target exactly as the raiser's projection showed it (labels, values, people by display name), versioned, and never more than the projection discloses. It keeps what was contested after the content changes or a control implementation is hard-deleted (`deleteControlImplementation` today; Phase 16 D-19), and it feeds the comparison (D-14).
  - **Reason:** required plain text, trimmed, 1 to 2,000 characters (17.1's rationale bound), never rendered as HTML or Markdown.
  - **`followsUp`:** an optional earlier point of the same assessment. "Raise a follow-up point" on an answered point pre-fills its target. Bounds such as points per person per round are research's.
- **D-05:** The thread, the status and concurrency (G-15; COLLAB-04 criteria 2 and 5).
  - **Status:**
    - `open`, then `answered` after an answer;
    - `resolved`, set by the raiser, and only from `answered`;
    - `withdrawn`, set by the raiser, from `open` or `answered`;
    - `undo` reverts the resolution or withdrawal immediately before it.

    There is no reopen. A raiser who disagrees replies, and in an open round raises a follow-up point (D-11).
  - **Recording:** entries are append-only, with no edit or delete for anyone. `status` is the cached result of one pure derivation over the entries, written in the same transaction, and tests prove they agree (17.1 D-33's posture). Gates and lists read the cached fact.
  - **Concurrency:** every write (a raise, an entry, a send-back) runs under the assessment's serialisation and row lock (17.1 D-21), so a raise and a release never interleave.
    - Acts carry the round number the caller saw (`ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_CHANGED`) and, on a point, its last entry sequence (`CONTEST_POINT_CHANGED`).
    - A refusal keeps the prepared text (brief §32, optimistic concurrency).
  - **After release** every point and entry is frozen (`ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`; decision 9).
- **D-06:** Raising (G-15; COLLAB-04 criterion 1).
  - **When and who:** only while an acceptance round is open, by a current participant (Phase 17 D-22 with 17.1 D-18), on anything they can read, not only their own risks. The Requestor who owns no risk may raise.
  - **Refusals:** a Security Analyst who is not a participant gets 403 `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (Phase 17 D-24). An analyst who is a participant raises from their own participant panel (Phase 17 D-37). The service identity gets 403, and anyone else 404.
  - **Effect:** it notifies the assigned analyst (D-17). It applies at once with an Undo toast; Undo withdraws the point with a fixed note, recorded.
- **D-07:** Replies (G-15; Phase 13 X-16).
  - **Who:** participants, and human Security Analysts who may read the assessment. A requesting analyst replies in the `requestor` part (19.1 D-08).
  - **When:** while the point is open or answered and the assessment is unreleased, whether or not a round is open (17.1 D-24).
  - **Form:** plain text of 1 to 5,000 characters (Phase 13 D-21's bound), attributed with parts and local time, append-only, and posted at once with optimistic feedback.
  - **Closed threads:** a resolved or withdrawn point's thread is read-only (`CONTEST_POINT_CLOSED`).
- **D-08:** Answers (G-15; ASSESS-26 criterion 2).
  - **Who:** any human Security Analyst who may act on the assessment as an analyst, as for Phase 17 D-38's response decisions (decision 10). Never the request's requestor (403 `OWN_REQUEST`, because they take part as its Requestor; G-1), and never a named owner or approver (17.1 D-23's actor codes; G-11). Each answer records who gave it; the assigned analyst stays accountable through resubmission. Participants hold no grant (403).
  - **`changed`**, saying what changed, needs no acceptance round open (`ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_OPEN`), because content is locked while one is (17.1 D-23).
  - **`kept`**, with a reason, may be given at any time before release (17.1 D-24). Both texts are required, at 1 to 2,000 characters.
  - **Answering again:** an unresolved point may be answered again. The later answer supersedes, both stay in the thread, and the raiser is told each time.
  - **Not a content edit:** an answer never withdraws "Mark reviewed" (Phase 16 D-23; 17.1 D-07). It passes only the guard's actor rules, not its round lock.
  - **No change check:** Strapi does not check that a "changed" target differs, because the change may be elsewhere. The answer form shows the target's comparison (D-14) and says when the target itself is unchanged.
- **D-09:** Resolving and withdrawing (G-15; success criterion 5).
  - **Who:** only the raiser. Another participant gets 403 `CONTEST_POINT_NOT_RAISER`, and an analyst 403 `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`.
  - **When:** resolve only from `answered` (`CONTEST_POINT_NOT_ANSWERED` otherwise); withdraw from `open` or `answered`; either at any time before release.
  - **Undo:** each applies at once with an Undo toast. Strapi accepts the undo only from the same person, as the next act on the point, within a short window that research fixes. It corrects a slip; it is not a reopen.
  - **A raiser who stops being a participant** (G-12) cannot act. Their points stay, an open one still needs an answer, and an answered one stays answered.
- **D-10:** Targets that leave the assessment. The analyst may withdraw a contested risk or remove a contested control implementation between rounds.
  - **What stays:** the points stay visible to every participant and analyst, showing the snapshot and "No longer part of this assessment", never the withdrawal's reason, actor or time. The analyst's answer says what happened.
  - **The reading:** this is a narrow reading of Phase 16 D-08 and 17.1 D-39. Participants have already read the risk they contested, and hiding their own challenge would erase the record.
  - **Unchanged:** decisions and voids on a withdrawn risk stay hidden from participants (17.1 D-39). A reinstated risk shows its points' live target again.

### Sending back and rework

- **D-11:** Sending back (G-15; ASSESS-26 criterion 1; success criteria 1 and 5).
  - **Who:** a current risk owner or executive approver of the assessment, while the round is open. The Requestor qualifies only by owning or approving a risk.
  - **Refusals:** a Requestor who does neither gets 403 `SEND_BACK_NOT_PERMITTED`; an analyst gets 403 `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`; the service identity gets 403; anyone else gets 404.
  - **Grounds:** at least one open point that the sender raised in that round (`SEND_BACK_NEEDS_OPEN_POINT`). This reads G-15's "with their points" so that every send-back carries a challenge that must be answered before resubmission (D-12). A sender whose points were all answered "kept" raises a follow-up point, so the disagreement gets its own recorded answer.
  - **Confirmation** (G-4): it lists the sender's points, says that recorded decisions stand only while their risks stay unchanged (17.1 D-38), and says that the analyst answers every point, reviews again and resubmits. It takes an optional message (at most 2,000 characters) and the round number the sender saw.
  - **Effect,** in one transaction under the lock:
    - the round closes `returned`, with the sender, their parts, the time and the message;
    - the assessment reopens through 17.1 D-25's reopening path, shared with recall: decisions are untouched, and the review stops being current (17.1 D-09);
    - notifications are written and read (D-17, D-18).
- **D-12:** Readiness: no open point (G-15; 17.1 D-19 and D-41). One predicate, "no contest point of the assessment is open", serves both readiness projections.
  - **Submission readiness:** "Every contest point answered", listing each open point with its destination. "Mark reviewed" reads the same projection without its last two items (17.1 D-05), so it waits too, and the order is: rework, answer, mark reviewed (with peer approval where needed), submit.
  - **Release-and-close readiness:** "No contest point open", re-checked in the transaction. A refusal is `RELEASE_NOT_READY`, with the open points by number.
  - **Scope:** points of a recalled round count like those of a returned one. An answered point that was not resolved never blocks.
- **D-13:** Stage, lifecycle, holder and queues. No stored value or stage is added (Phase 17 D-05 to D-08; 17.1 D-49).
  - **Stage:** after a return the stage is `with-security-analyst`, and the round summary carries the returned facts.
  - **Stepper:** it follows 17.1 D-49; the review step reopens because the review is no longer current.
  - **Next actions** (`request-lifecycle.ts`): the analyst reads "Answer {n} contest points" before "Mark the assessment reviewed" and the submit step, or "Answer {n} contest points, then release and close" when only points block release. Participants read who sent the assessment back and that the analyst is reworking it.
  - **Holder and queues:** the holder is `analyst` after a return and whenever only points block release. The queue bucket after a return is `requires-attention`; its SQL and oracle change together (Phase 7 D-21 and D-30).
  - **Labels** go through the one status mapping (the G-13 seam):
    - a new `contestPoint` family, "Open", "Answered", "Resolved" and "Withdrawn", which never collides with a withdrawn risk;
    - the round outcome "Returned";
    - proposed tones: open is warning (it waits on the analyst), answered is info, resolved and withdrawn are neutral, and returned is warning. The UI-SPEC confirms them.

### Before and after, and history

- **D-14:** Before and after (ASSESS-26 criterion 3; success criterion 3; 17.1 D-37 and D-40).
  - **The comparison:** one server-built comparison of a stored snapshot against the participant-visible content now. It shows each changed field before and after, and control implementations added, removed or changed. It works across snapshot versions, where a field absent from one version reads as not recorded. The web formats the typed values but never diffs.
  - **What it serves:**
    - a voided decision: 17.1's decision snapshot against the risk now;
    - a contest point: its target snapshot against the target now (or D-10's line).
  - **The decider's view:** in round n+1, each decider's view marks each of their risks:
    - "stands", with their round-n acts, still revisable (17.1 D-35);
    - or "decide again", with the comparison.

    The re-decision form pre-fills from the recommendation (17.1 D-34) and offers "Start from your round {n} decision". Voided acts and their comparisons also show in each risk's decision trail and in the history, for everyone who reads it.
  - **The analyst's marker:** between rounds, the analyst's "will stand or be voided" marker (17.1 D-40) opens the same comparison.
  - **UI:** X-24's comparison (Phase 15's `field-comparison` module), with headings that name the two moments. If X-24's recorded wording fixes "Current" and "Proposed", the UI-SPEC proposes a dated amendment rather than a new pattern.
- **D-15:** The history view (G-15 "History"; ASSESS-26 criterion 4; success criterion 4).
  - **The read:** one read of structured events in a closed kind vocabulary. The server resolves people and labels, and the web renders each kind with the UI-SPEC's sentence. Events are grouped and paginated by round, and each carries who, part and when:
    - response rounds (Phase 17): opened, responses submitted, closed;
    - acceptance rounds: opened by submission with the message, recalled, returned or completed;
    - decisions, revisions and voids (17.1 D-32);
    - points raised, replies, answers, resolutions, withdrawals and undos.
  - **Readers:** participants and analysts read it from the gate on, through the released record (G-12).
    - Participants never see 17.1's review log, analyst-only facts, or events on withdrawn risks (17.1 D-39; D-10 excepted), and they never read Phase 16's risk history.
    - Analysts also see the review log events and, for each rework period, the risks changed, linked to their Phase 16 history.
  - **Where:** a route per actor (for example `/app/assessments/{documentId}/history` and `/app/review/assessments/{documentId}/history`), linked from the assessment header. It prints whole under the direction's print rules.
  - **After release** it stays readable and read-only. The released export and the A4 report gain nothing (deferred).

### Reads and notifications

- **D-16:** What participants and analysts read (Phase 17 D-25 and D-28; 17.1 D-47, D-48 and D-50).
  - **The participant projection** gains per-target counts by status (the markers beside content), the latest round's outcome facts (returned by whom, part, when and message), and the viewer's contest acts (raise, send back), with a reason when one is not open.
  - **The analyst detail** gains per-target open counts, the viewer's answer permission with its reason, and the readiness items (D-12).
  - **"My risk assessments"** items gain the open points and the viewer's answered points.
  - **The analyst assessment list** gains the open count and a closed `contestPoints=open` filter. The analyst Home's "Contest points to answer" figure opens it for the viewer's assigned assessments (X-10).
  - **Home:** answered points never enter "Needs your action", because resolving is optional.
- **D-17:** Notification kinds go through Phase 19.1's service (NOTIFY-04; G-18; 19.1 D-05, D-06, D-11 and D-12). Each event writes one notification per recipient, never to the actor, the service identity or an inactive user. Names are proposals.

  | Kind | Event | Recipients |
  |---|---|---|
  | `contest-point-raised` | a participant raises a point | the assigned analyst |
  | `contest-point-answered` | an analyst answers a point, every time | the point's raiser |
  | `contest-point-replied` | a reply is posted | the raiser, the assigned analyst and any other analyst who answered the point |
  | `assessment-returned` | an owner or approver sends the assessment back | the assigned analyst and every other participant |

  `contest-point-replied` extends G-18's list under decision 18's rule: an analyst's clarifying question waits on the raiser, and a raiser's rebuttal waits on the analyst (17.1 D-54's precedent). The orchestrator records it in NOTIFY-04.
- **D-18:** Reads, readability and destinations (19.1 D-09, D-13, D-17, D-20 and D-21).
  - **Readability** is the assessment's read when the list and the count are read: Phase 17 D-22 for the participant parts (`requestor`, `risk-owner`, `executive-approver`), and the analyst read for `security-analyst`.
  - **Facts** hold ids and numbers only (point, round, entry sequence, answer outcome).
  - **Destinations** open the point on the part's page, or the assessment. The web's known-shape parser is extended.
  - **Read by acting:**
    - an answer reads the answerer's `-raised` and `-replied` for that point, and an analyst's reply reads their own `-replied`;
    - the raiser's reply, resolution or withdrawal reads their `-answered` and `-replied` for it;
    - the next submission reads the assigned analyst's `assessment-returned`.
  - **Read by reversal:**
    - a withdrawal, including the raise's Undo, reads the assigned analyst's `-raised`;
    - a send-back reads every participant's unread `acceptance-requested`, `decision-voided` and `executive-approval-requested` of that round, on the path 17.1's recall shares;
    - round n+1's opening reads the participants' `assessment-returned` for round n.

### Authorisation

- **D-19:** Separation of duties (G-1, G-11; 17.1 D-56), each rule enforced in Strapi against the resolved IdamBox User:

  | Person | Cannot |
  |---|---|
  | A Security Analyst acting as one | raise, resolve, withdraw, undo or send back (403 `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`, unless also a participant) |
  | The request's requestor, an analyst included | answer (403 `OWN_REQUEST`) |
  | A named owner or approver holding the analyst role | answer (409 `ACTOR_IS_RISK_OWNER` or `ACTOR_IS_EXECUTIVE_APPROVER`) |
  | A participant who did not raise the point | resolve, withdraw or undo it (403 `CONTEST_POINT_NOT_RAISER`) |
  | The Requestor who owns and approves no risk | send back (403 `SEND_BACK_NOT_PERMITTED`) |
  | Any participant | answer (403, no grant) |
  | The service identity | any contest-point or history operation, reads included (403) |
  | Anyone else | anything here (404) |
- **D-20:** Grants. Three API-only Function families, in both catalogue copies, byte-identical, none granted to `role.security_analyst_agent`:
  1. the reads, replies and history, to analysts and the Requestor birthright, with the participant rule;
  2. raise, resolve, withdraw, undo and send back, to the birthright, with the participant rule;

## Authorisation Model and Negative Cases

Keycloak authenticates. Strapi enforces Role → Function → FunctionAPI, then the record rule, against the resolved IdamBox User in `ctx.state`. The development identity bridge and a Keycloak-verified user are handled identically, and authorisation never branches on the auth mode (POST-DEMO-BRIEF §2). Navigation visibility is not authorisation. Grants are D-20; the rules are D-19.

**Positive cases:**
- current participants raise in an open round, reply, and resolve or withdraw their own points;
- current owners and approvers send back with an open point of their own;
- human analysts who may act on the assessment answer and reply;
- participants and analysts read the points, the comparisons and the history from the gate on, through the released record.

**Negative cases** (each proven in the API by an integration test or a matrix row, and in the UI by Vitest or Playwright):

| Case | API | UI |
|---|---|---|
| Maya (unrelated) lists, reads, raises, replies, resolves, sends back or reads the history, at any stage | 404, persistence unchanged | Not-found page; nothing in lists, Home or notifications |
| A former participant does any of these | 404 | Not listed; their notifications disappear |
| An analyst who is not a participant raises, resolves, withdraws or sends back | 403 `NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` | No control |
| The Requestor who owns and approves no risk sends back | 403 `SEND_BACK_NOT_PERMITTED` | No send-back control; the panel says who can |
| A send-back with no open point of the sender's own, no round open, or a stale round | 409 `SEND_BACK_NEEDS_OPEN_POINT`, `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_NOT_OPEN` or `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_CHANGED` | "Raise a contest point first"; the view refreshes |
| A participant resolves or withdraws another's point | 403 `CONTEST_POINT_NOT_RAISER` | No control |
| Resolving an open point; acting on a resolved or withdrawn one; a stale sequence | 409 `CONTEST_POINT_NOT_ANSWERED`, `CONTEST_POINT_CLOSED` or `CONTEST_POINT_CHANGED` | Not offered; text kept |
| A participant answers | 403 (no grant) | No control |
| The request's requestor (an analyst) answers | 403 `OWN_REQUEST` | Not offered, with the reason |
