## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the touched web files by path; `typecheck` when types change; `api:check` after a spec change; the touched Strapi files by path, with dev servers stopped.
- **After every plan wave:** the full web suite, `typecheck`, `lint` and `api:check`; the full Strapi suite after every Strapi plan; each UI plan's targeted Playwright spec once, at its end.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** the full suite must be green (the phase gate). Counts are recorded against the baseline (web 2,048, Strapi 578 and Playwright 84 at `678b117`, plus every phase executed since).
- **Max feedback latency:** 90 seconds at task level.
