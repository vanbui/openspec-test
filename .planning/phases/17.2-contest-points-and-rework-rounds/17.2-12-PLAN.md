## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** contest anything in an assessment I am asked to accept and see each point answered, and as a Security Analyst I want to rework the whole assessment and resubmit it, **so that** acceptance rests on content both lines have challenged and agreed.

This plan's slice: a participant contests anything they read from where they read it, the Security Analyst answers, the raiser resolves or withdraws, and every contested element carries a marker.

<objective>
Build every act on a point in the UI: the raise form with its target picker and every entry point (D-04, D-06; 17.2-UI-SPEC §3; amendment 1's "Close and keep draft"), including 17.1's decision-view seams ("Contest the rating"; the approver's "Contest" and "Ask for another approver", D-23; UI-D5), the analyst's answer form (D-08; §5.4), the raiser's resolve, withdraw and undo (D-09; §5.5; amendment 5), and the X-48 markers beside contested content on both pages (D-16; §2), with every absence rule of D-19 at the G-4 bar (D-24).

Purpose: SC1 ("raises two contest points … each with a reason") and SC2 ("answers that point 'changed', answers the other 'kept'") and SC5's UI refusals ("the analyst cannot … resolve a participant's point").
Output: three components, the sheet and panel wiring, the controls on both pages and the decision form, and component tests.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>
