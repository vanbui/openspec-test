## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** contest anything in an assessment I am asked to accept and see each point answered, and as a Security Analyst I want to rework the whole assessment and resubmit it, **so that** acceptance rests on content both lines have challenged and agreed.

This plan's slice: the web's typed layer for every contest act and the history, the refusal copy, and the one zod-free module of wording every component uses.

<objective>
Build the web's typed layer for the ten operations (D-02; D-28 step 7): closed parsers, server-only services, Server Actions with revalidation and a closed result, the history read's parser and service, family W refusal copy (D-19; 17.2-UI-SPEC §12), and the zod-free `contest-domain.ts` (D-24, D-25), registered in the parity, chain and parser-free guards and the served inventory.

Purpose: AGENTS.md's typed API layer and "no business logic in JSX"; every UI plan (17.2-11 to 17.2-14) consumes only these modules.
Output: seven contest modules and tests, three history modules, the refusal copy, the guard registrations and the regenerated inventory.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Parsers** (`contest-schema.ts`, zod as the existing `*-schema.ts` modules use it): `contestPointSummarySchema`, `contestPointListSchema`, `contestPointDetailSchema`, `contestEntrySchema`, `snapshotComparisonSchema`, `contestEntryResponseSchema`, `acceptanceRoundResponseSchema` reuse from `features/acceptance/acceptance-schema.ts` for send back; every enum closed (status, entry kind, outcome, target kind, parts).
- **Services** (`contest-service.ts`, `import 'server-only'`): `listContestPoints(assessmentId, query)`, `getContestPoint(assessmentId, pointNumber, query)`, `raiseContestPoint(assessmentId, body)`, `replyToContestPoint`, `answerContestPoint`, `resolveContestPoint`, `withdrawContestPoint`, `undoContestPointAct`, `returnAcceptanceRound(assessmentId, roundNumber, body)`; each returns the parsed data or a typed refusal `{ status, reason, details }`.
- **Actions** (`contest-actions.ts`, `'use server'`): `raiseContestPointAction`, `replyToContestPointAction`, `answerContestPointAction`, `resolveContestPointAction`, `withdrawContestPointAction` (with `viaUndo` for the raise's Undo), `undoContestPointAction`, `sendBackAction`, and the reads `loadContestPointsAction` and `loadContestPointAction` for client panels loaded on demand; each returns `{ ok: true, data } | { ok: false, refusal: { kind: "field" | "blocked" | "retryable", message, reason } }` and calls `revalidatePath` for `/app/assessments/{documentId}`, `/app/review/assessments/{documentId}` and both history routes after a write.
- **History** (`history-schema.ts`, `history-service.ts`): `assessmentHistorySchema` (closed event kinds from the specification), `getAssessmentHistory(assessmentId, { rounds, show })`.
- **Refusal copy** (`refusal-copy.ts`): `contestRefusal(reason, status, context)` returning family W's copy and kind exactly as 17.2-UI-SPEC §12 W1 to W3 words them (with `{n}`, `{k}`, `{name}`, `{target}` from `context`), and W4: V3 `SUBMISSION_NOT_READY` lists `Every contest point answered`; V5 `RELEASE_NOT_READY` appends `; {o} contest {point is|points are} open: Point {k}, …`.
- **Domain** (`contest-domain.ts`, zod-free, registered in `parser-free-modules.ts`): `contestMarkerText({ open, answered, closed })` (`{o} open · {a} answered`, zero parts omitted, or `{c} closed`); `contestMarkerAccessibleName(targetLabel, counts)` (`{n} contest {point|points} on {target}: {o} open, {a} answered`); `pointCountLine(counts)`; `openPointsLabel(o)` (`{o} open contest {point|points}`); `answeredOfYoursLabel(a)` (`{a} of your points answered`); `contestEntryLine(entry)` (the §5.3 resolved, withdrawn, undo and `Withdrawn with Undo straight after it was raised` lines); `answerLabel(outcome)` (`Answer: Changed`, `Answer: Kept`); `panelFilterFromSearchParams` and `panelFilterToSearchParams` (`points`, `round`, `mine`, `target`; defaults `open` for analysts and `all` for participants); `pointHash(k)` (`#point-{k}`); `sendBackLine(sendBack)` (§6.1's three lines by reason); `answerFormMode(answer)` (offered outcomes, the round-open line, the who-sees-no-form line by reason); `undoAvailable(until, now)`; `contestDraftKey({ personId, assessmentId, scope })` for the session store; `CONTEST_COPY_FORBIDDEN_WORDS` and `assertContestCopy(text)`.
- **Guards and inventory.** The chain guard lists the ten operations with their web callers; the parity guard covers the new response schemas; `SERVED_OPERATIONS_WRITE=1 pnpm --dir apps/backend served-operations:write` (dev servers stopped) regenerates the inventory.
- **Scope note (the pilot's pacing rule, recorded for the plan check).** 16 files in three tasks: the contest typed layer and the history typed layer share the guards and the inventory every web caller must update together; splitting them adds a plan serialised on the same four guard files.
