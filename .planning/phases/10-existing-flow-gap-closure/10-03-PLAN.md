## Recorded planning decisions

- **Handler shape.** `selectAdjacentRadio(event: React.KeyboardEvent<HTMLButtonElement>)` is exported from `radio-group.tsx`. `RadioGroupItem` composes it after any `onKeyDown` the caller passes: the caller runs first, and the handler returns when `event.defaultPrevented` is already true. It acts only on `ArrowDown`, `ArrowRight`, `ArrowUp` and `ArrowLeft` with no Ctrl, Alt, Meta or Shift; it calls `event.preventDefault()`, finds the enabled `[role="radio"]` elements of `event.currentTarget.closest('[role="radiogroup"]')`, and focuses then clicks the next one (wrapping). The Slot runs the child's handler before the roving one, and the roving item skips its handler when default was prevented (`@radix-ui/react-slot@1.3.3` lines 88-93, `@radix-ui/react-roving-focus@1.1.19` line 176, `@radix-ui/primitive@1.1.7` lines 6-13), so focus moves once.
- **Enabled means** not `disabled` and not `data-disabled`.
- **`segmented-control.test.tsx` stays unchanged** (UI-SPEC §19.2): its held-key case keeps passing, and the quick-press case for the segmented control lives in the new `radio-group.test.tsx`.
