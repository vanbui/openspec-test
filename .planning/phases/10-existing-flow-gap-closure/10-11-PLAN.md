## Recorded planning decisions

- **The explanation text is user-facing and fixed by UI-SPEC §3.4:** `A newly generated questionnaire replaced {n} {question|questions}. {m} analyst {change was|changes were} discarded.`, or `… No analyst changes were discarded.` when `m` is 0. `n` counts the replaced questionnaire's active questions; `m` is the count above, computed from the log before the append.
- **The Requestor projection of `regenerated`** keeps `kind` and `at` and drops `explanation`, which describes analyst work (UI-SPEC §4.3 shows no detail for it).
- **A full log** (1,000 events) makes the append throw; the job then fails with the registry's safe generic failure and the questionnaire is unchanged, because the append happens inside the transaction.
- **Job failure code.** The new in-job failure uses the registry's existing `QuestionnaireGenerationJobFailure` with code `QUESTIONNAIRE_RELEASED`, matching `^[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{0,63}$` (`questionnaire-generation-registry.ts:12`).
