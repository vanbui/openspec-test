## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst or Requestor, **I want to** have the existing request, questionnaire, assessment and administration screens work as they say, **so that** today's flow completes without workarounds, lost input or misleading messages.

This plan's slice: the analyst can add and edit questions on any questionnaire, including AI-generated ones, in a form that opens where they clicked, and can read what was released while the Requestor answers.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **One read, two parses.** `getQuestionnaireGovernanceQuestions(documentId)` makes the existing `GET /v1/questionnaires/{questionnaireId}/questions` once and returns `{ definition, placements }`: the definition through the existing filtered parser, the placements through a new parser registered as `getQuestionnaireQuestionPlacements` against `QuestionnaireQuestionsResponse`. The placement parser reads `documentId`, `questionText`, `options.questionKey`, `options.section` (`key`, `title`, optional `description`, `order`) and `options.order`, and marks a question removed when either the top-level `deleted` or `options.deleted` is present. Removed questions are served with `options.schemaVersion: 2` and a `deleted` block the contract does not declare; the parser accepts both as optional and requires neither, and it declares no bound, so it passes all five parity modes without joining `boundaryGrandfathered` or needing `narrowedPaths`. It is added to the parity guard's "reads every question" list.
- **Key derivation.** `section-{slug}` from the new title (lowercase, runs of other characters to `-`, trimmed; `section` when empty), cut to fit the mutation's 120-character key bound, with `-2`, `-3` … until unique across every question.
- **Duplicate section titles** read `{title} (section {n})` in the chooser (UI-SPEC §1.2).
- **Shared read-only rows.** `questionnaire-governance.tsx` exports `ReleasedQuestionList` (the governance rows without Edit, Delete or any answer), used by the released questions section.
- **One form at a time (UI-D3).** While a form is open, every other `Add question`, `Edit`, `Delete` and `Release questionnaire` is disabled, with the §1.1 caption under Release.
