## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: every person finds the risks they own, approve, treat or control in one list, sees what needs them first, exports it, and reaches each risk's page from the list or from the released assessment.

<objective>
Build "My risks and treatments" (D-20; 20-UI-SPEC §2): the Server Component page, the list with filters, sort, pagination, cards and states, the export through Phase 19's dialog (CSV-01), the `my-risks:read` capability (ADR 0004), the route heading, and the released view's "Track this risk" link and caption on the viewer's own risks (D-21; §8).

Purpose: SC3 ("a risk owner sees their risks, their risks' treatments and the decisions awaiting them or due for review; a treatment owner sees their treatments with due dates; neither sees anybody else's") in the UI, and UAT-058.
Output: the my-risks feature's four modules, the route, the capability, the export key, the heading and the released link, with Vitest proofs.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

- **Layers.** `my-risks-schema.ts` (zod for `MyRiskItem`, the list and summary responses); `my-risks-service.ts` (`server-only`: `listMyRisks(query)`, `myRisksSummary()`, `trackableRiskIds(assessmentId)` reading `my_risks?assessment={id}` across pages); `my-risks-domain.ts` (zod-free: `partsLabel(parts)` joined by ` · `, `nextStepLabel(step)` → `Decide`, `Approve`, `Decide again`, `Update the treatment`, `Review acceptance`, `Renew exception`, null → `Nothing now`; `treatmentCaption(item, today)` via 20-16's `duePhrase`; `buildMyRisksQuery(searchParams)` closed to the list's parameters, `show` default `open`).
