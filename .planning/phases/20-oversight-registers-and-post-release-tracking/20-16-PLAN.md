## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: a treatment owner or risk owner opens their risk's page, sees where its treatment stands, sets it in progress and marks it complete on evidence, with Undo, and sees nothing they may not use.

<objective>
Build the owner risk page (D-20, D-29; 20-UI-SPEC §3) and its tracking panel (X-62): progress with optimistic Undo (D-08), completion on evidence with Undo and reopen (D-10), the refusal and stale states (D-12, D-13, D-21), on the typed API layer with a zod-free domain module. Change the one status mapping per X-63 and UI-D2 (G-13), with Phase 19's label vocabulary in both copies.

Purpose: SC2's Not Started → In Progress → Complete and SC6's refusals in the UI; the core of UAT-046 and UAT-058.
Output: the route, the treatment feature's schema, service, actions and domain, the page view, the panel and completion form, the status mapping, and Vitest proofs.

Edits Strapi source? No (the vocabulary JSON only). Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

- **Page.** `page.tsx` (Server Component) reads `getMyRisk`; 404 → `notFound()` (the in-shell `(app)/not-found.tsx`); 403 `NOT_RISK_PERSON` → 10 §10.2's permission state with Y1's copy; 409 `RISK_NOT_RELEASED` → a link to 17.1's decision view. `owner-risk-view.tsx` lays out §3.1, with the Decision review, Progress notes and Evidence sections as slots 20-17 fills. `loading.tsx` is §16's skeleton; `error.tsx` reads `We couldn’t load this risk` · `Try again`.
- **Panel.** `tracking-panel.tsx` (client) per §3.2 and §4.1: the SegmentedControl `Progress` with `Not started` | `In progress`, optimistic with rollback and the Undo toast (X-14); the completion record or `Mark complete` (primary); `Reopen treatment` (outline small) opening the in-place reopen form (§4.3); change actions are slots 20-17 fills, drawn only when `allowedActs` includes them; the accepted line, the closed `Alert` and the older-release caption. `completion-form.tsx` per §4.2 with `next/dynamic` loading, Phase 10's guard, `Ctrl`/`⌘` `Enter`, focus to the status line on success, the 120-second Undo through `reopenTreatment({ undo: true })`.
