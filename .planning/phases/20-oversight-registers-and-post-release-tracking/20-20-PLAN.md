## Recorded planning decisions

- **Field.** `suggestion-field.tsx` (client): an input with `role="combobox"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and a listbox labelled `In use`; after one typed letter it calls `suggestRequestFieldValuesAction(field, q)` (debounced, last request wins), shows at most eight options, arrows move, Enter chooses, Escape closes and keeps the text; it never shows a count.
- **Typed layer.** `request-service.ts` `listRequestFieldSuggestions(field, q)` (`server-only`) through the typed client; `request-schema.ts` parses `{ data: string[] }`; `request-actions.ts` `suggestRequestFieldValuesAction` returns `string[]` or `[]` on refusal.
- **Form.** `request-form.tsx`'s `Division` and `Business unit` use the field with their existing labels and hints plus `Choose a value in use, or type a new one.`
- **Reports.** `report-filters.tsx` renders the filter row under the description of risk exposure and assessment throughput (an `Assessment type` select with `All`, `Project`, `BAU`, `Other`, `Not set`, and the two suggestion fields), chips and `Clear filters`, writing the parameters into the address; `report-service.ts` passes them; `report-presentation.ts` builds the two new sections' rows and links (`/app/risks?state=open&stage=released&assessmentType={type}&residualRating={rating}` and the period list with the type) and the new copy; `risk-exposure-report.tsx` and `assessment-throughput-report.tsx` render `Residual rating by assessment type` and `Released by assessment type`; `report-table.tsx` gains a phone card-list mode below 768px (row header as the card title, figures as links) used by the new sections; `treatment-performance-report.tsx` and `report-copy.json` carry the copy of §10.
