## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: the notification centre learns Phase 20's kinds. A person named treatment owner is told, an analyst learns an acceptance was not confirmed, and the system can write reminders with no actor to people it names, each readable only while the recipient may still read the risk.

Publish the kind vocabulary and the web's icons.

Purpose: NOTIFY-02's in-app half and 17.1's deferred notice to a named treatment owner; the reminder run (20-14) writes through this service.
Output: the amended service, registry, facts, projection and repository, three emitters, the published vocabulary and the web icons, with unit and integration proofs.

Edits Strapi source? Yes (dev servers stopped). Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Kind spec additions.** `NotificationKindSpec` gains `actor: "person" | "system"` (every existing kind `person`), `recipients` gains `"explicit"`, `subject` gains `"risk"`, and `email: boolean` (true for the six periodic kinds). Rows: `treatment-due-soon`, `treatment-overdue` (parts `treatment-owner`; subject `risk`; explicit; system; never names); `treatment-overdue-escalated` (parts `security-analyst`, `risk-owner`; subject `risk`; explicit; system; the analyst sentence names the treatment owner from the facts when the analyst's read shows them); `decision-waiting` (parts `risk-owner`, `executive-approver`; subject `assessment`; explicit; system); `decision-review-due` (parts `risk-owner`, `executive-approver`; subject `risk`; explicit; system); `assessment-review-due` (part `security-analyst`; subject `assessment`; explicit; system); `treatment-assigned` (part `treatment-owner`; subject `risk`; explicit; person; never names; no email); `decision-review-not-confirmed` (part `security-analyst`; subject `risk`; explicit; person; names the actor to the analyst; no email). `NOTIFICATION_PARTS` gains `treatment-owner`.
- **Facts (v1, closed per kind).** System kinds: `{ schemaVersion: 1, actorDocumentId: null, … }` with `dueDate` (`treatment-due-soon`, `treatment-overdue`, `treatment-overdue-escalated` plus `treatmentOwnerDocumentId`), `roundNumber` and `daysWaiting` (`decision-waiting`), `reviewDate`, `stage` (`lead` | `due` | `overdue`) and `subjectKind` (`acceptance` | `exception`) (`decision-review-due`), `reviewDate` (`assessment-review-due`); person kinds: `treatment-assigned` `{ schemaVersion: 1, actorDocumentId }`, `decision-review-not-confirmed` `{ schemaVersion: 1, actorDocumentId, reviewerPart }`. `buildNotificationFacts` throws on a null actor for a person kind and a set actor for a system kind.
- **Service.** `notify(strapi, event)` accepts `actorDocumentId: string | null` and, for explicit kinds, `recipients: { userDocumentId; part }[]`; it still refuses outside a transaction, drops the actor, the service identity and inactive users, keeps one per person, sets `request`, `assessment` and, for risk subjects, `risk`; it returns `{ recipientDocumentId, notificationId }[]` (existing callers that read the count use `.length`).
- **Projection.** 20-UI-SPEC §13's sentences verbatim, record `{risk number} · {request title}` or `{assessment number} · {request title}`; dates as X-3 calendar dates; destinations: owner parts → `/app/my-risks/{riskId}#treatment` (or `#decision-review`); `security-analyst` on a risk → `/app/risks/{riskId}#treatment` (or `#decision-review`); `decision-waiting` → `/app/assessments/{assessmentId}#your-decisions`; `assessment-review-due` → `/app/review/assessments/{assessmentId}`; `encodeURIComponent` on every id.
- **Readability (repository's one SQL builder).** `treatment-owner`, `risk-owner` and `executive-approver` on a risk subject: the recipient currently holds that part on the released risk (the part's link table, from metadata, as `applyRiskPersonPredicate` builds it); `decision-waiting`: the participant SQL twin ("readable now", 17-07); `security-analyst`: 19.1's analyst rule with the kind's analyst read operation (`getDetail` of the register, or the assessment's findOne). An unknown kind or part matches nothing.
- **Emitters.** `releaseAssessment` (17.1's release and close): after the risk fields are written, for each active Treat risk with a `treatmentOwner`, one `treatment-assigned` (actor the releasing analyst, part `treatment-owner`). `changeTreatment` with `field: "treatmentOwner"`: one to the new owner. `reviewDecision` with `not-confirmed`: one `decision-review-not-confirmed` to `assignedAnalystOf` when not null. All in the act's transaction, after the request lock the act already holds.
- **Contract.** The notification `kind` enum and `recipientPart` enum in the spec gain the eight kinds and `treatment-owner`; `notifications-domain.ts` and `notification-item.tsx` gain the kinds and 20-UI-SPEC §13's icons (`CalendarClock`, `CalendarX2`, `BellRing`, `Hourglass`, `CalendarSync`, `CalendarCheck2`, `UserRoundPen`, `BadgeX`).
