## Recorded planning decisions

- **Register query (`parseRiskRegisterQuery`, closed).** Adds `assessmentType` (`Project` | `BAU` | `Other` | `none`), `division` and `businessUnit` (1–200, exact after trim and case-fold), `control` and `riskType` (opaque ids), `progress` (`Not Started` | `In Progress` | `Complete`, the effective progress of applicable risks), `decisionReview` (`due-soon` = states `due-soon` and `due`, `overdue`, `not-confirmed`, `reviewer-missing`), `treatmentAction` (`Accept` | `Treat` | `none`), and sort keys `assessmentType`, `resolveDate` (the governing date, empty last) and `reviewBy`. Joins go through the risk's assessment's request; `control` is an `EXISTS` over the risk's control implementations.
- **Register row.** Adds `assessmentType`, `division`, `businessUnit`, `treatmentProgress` (effective, applicable risks only), `resolveDate`, `decisionReview: { date, state } | null`, `closedOn`, and `decision: { decidedBy: { documentId, displayName }, authority: "risk-owner" | "executive-approver" } | null` from 17.1's standing completing act (unless 17.1's rows already carry it).
- **Accepted marker.** Phase 11 D-41's marker (17.1 D-52's refinement) adds `decision_review_outcome IS DISTINCT FROM 'not-confirmed'` (portably `IS NULL OR <> 'not-confirmed'`).
- **Register detail (`getRiskRegisterDetail`).** For a released risk adds `tracking` (`projectTrackingFacts`), `decisionReview` (`projectDecisionReview`), `residualReview` (the latest `residual-reviewed` row: by, at, rationale; a narrative read), `closure`, `allowedActs` (`allowedRiskActs` for a human analyst plus the analyst acts) and `withheldReason` (`analystActWithholding`); for an unreleased risk these are null.
- **View key.** `RISK_TABLE_VIEW_KEYS` gains `risk-register`, and `saved-views.ts` parses a definition by its key: a `risk-register` definition `{ schemaVersion: 1, columns: [...], sort, filters }` is parsed with `parseRiskRegisterQuery` for filters and sort and the register's column names for columns; unknown keys drop on read (18's fail-safe).
- **Queue and list.** `parseAnalystReviewQuery` adds `assessmentType`, `division`, `businessUnit`, `informationSystem`, `informationAsset` (opaque ids; any request naming the record), applied inside the queue's existing buckets (they narrow only). `buildAssessmentCollectionQuery` (analyst scope) adds `assessmentType`, `division`, `businessUnit`; `projectAssessmentSummary` adds the three through the request.
- **Contract.** The register list's parameters and row schema, the register detail schema (`tracking`, `decisionReview`, `residualReview`, `closure`, `allowedActs`, `withheldReason`), the queue's and assessment list's parameters and summary fields, and Phase 18's view-key enum gain the values above; `risk-schema.ts` and `assessment-schema.ts` follow.
