## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: G-16's one switch. The due state and the treatment status move to the resolve date and to tracked treatments in one place, and every register row, filter, Home figure, alert and report follows with no rule of its own.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **The rule.** `TREATMENT_DUE_STATES = ["overdue", "due-soon", "later", "no-target-date", "complete-or-closed", "not-tracked"] as const` (the value `no-target-date` keeps its published name; its label becomes "No resolve date" in 20-20). `TreatmentFacts` becomes `TreatmentTrackingFacts` from `treatment-facts.ts` (every key required). `deriveTreatmentDueState(facts, today, windowDays = ALERT_DUE_SOON_WINDOW_DAYS)`: not applicable → `not-tracked`; closed or effective progress Complete → `complete-or-closed`; no governing date → `no-target-date`; date before today → `overdue`; date on or before today + window → `due-soon`; else `later`. The header comment says D-08's rule: the server writes Overdue (in the run and every act) and every read derives it here.
- **The SQL.** `TREATMENT_GOVERNING_DATE_SQL = "COALESCE(r.treatment_resolve_date, r.target_date)"` replaces `TREATMENT_DUE_DATE_COLUMN` (every importer moves to it through `whereRaw` or `connection.raw` with bound `YYYY-MM-DD` strings). `TREATMENT_EFFECTIVE_PROGRESS_SQL` = `COALESCE(r.treatment_progress, CASE WHEN r.treatment_status IN ('Not Started','In Progress','Complete') THEN r.treatment_status ELSE 'Not Started' END)`. `applyTreatmentApplicablePredicate(query)` = 11-14's `applyReleasedParent` plus `r.treatment_action = 'Treat' AND r.withdrawn_at IS NULL`; `not-tracked` is its negation (`NOT EXISTS` released parent `OR` action distinct from Treat `OR` withdrawn). `applyTreatmentDueStatePredicate(query, state, { today, windowDays })` builds each state from the applicable conjunct per research §R5. `applyTreatmentStatusPredicate(query, status, { today })` filters `Not Started`, `In Progress`, `Overdue`, `Complete` and `none` on the derived value: for applicable open risks the CASE of research §R6, for applicable closed risks the stored status (or effective progress when null), for other risks the stored column (11-15's `none` semantics kept).
- **Consumers.** `risk-register.ts`: `projectRiskRegisterItem` passes the full facts (`released` from 11-14's stage, `treatmentAction`, `withdrawnAt`, `closureDate`, `treatmentProgress`, `treatmentStatus`, `treatmentResolveDate`, `targetDate`) and projects `treatmentStatus` as `deriveTreatmentStatus` and `treatmentDueState`; the `treatmentStatus` filter calls `applyTreatmentStatusPredicate`; the register query parser accepts `dueState=not-tracked`. `alert-rules.ts`: `critical-residual-risk-untreated` reads `effectiveTreatmentProgress` (`Not Started`), never `Overdue`; treatment alert kinds come from the rule (tracked only); `control-implementation-overdue` requires `controlStatus = 'Planned'` and an open risk. `alert-repository.ts`, `report-repository.ts`, `report-definitions.ts` and `dashboard-summary.ts` use the predicates only; the treatment-performance report counts tracked treatments, and the exposure report's "Residual rating by treatment status" uses the derived status.
- **Contract.** In `specification.json`, every `dueState` / `treatmentDueState` enum gains `not-tracked` (register query parameter and row schema); descriptions that say "target date" for due figures say "resolve date (target date when none)". `api:generate`; `risk-schema.ts`'s closed enums gain `not-tracked`.
