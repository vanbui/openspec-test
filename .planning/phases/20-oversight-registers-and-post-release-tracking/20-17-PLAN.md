## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager, **I want to** registers by assessment type that stay current after release, and as a risk or treatment owner I want to track each treatment to completion on evidence, **so that** oversight reflects what is actually being done.

This plan's slice: the rest of the owner page: the reasoned date, plan and owner changes, the notes thread, the evidence files, the review of an accepted risk, and the post-release history, each shown only to the people who may use it.
