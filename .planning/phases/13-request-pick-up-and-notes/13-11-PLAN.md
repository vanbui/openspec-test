## Recorded planning decisions

- **Who sees what.** `NoteItem` renders `NoteAiInputSwitch` when `meta.canMarkAiInputs` is true; otherwise, when the note has `changesAiInputs: true` (served only to analysts), the X-11 provenance note `Changes the AI inputs` with `Sparkles`; for a Requestor (no `changesAiInputs` key) nothing.
- **Switch** (`note-ai-input-switch.tsx`, `'use client'`): the `Switch` labelled `Changes the AI inputs`, described by the note's header line (`aria-describedby`); flips at once at the pending opacity; calls `setNoteAiInputAction`; success toast per §10.4 (`Note marked as changing the AI inputs.` or `Note no longer marked as changing the AI inputs.`) with `Undo`, whose success shows `Mark removed.` or `Mark restored.`; failure rolls back and shows a danger toast titled `Mark not changed` with family M's copy; focus stays on the switch.
- **Checkbox.** When `meta.canMarkAiInputs` is true the composer shows a `Checkbox` `Changes the AI inputs`, off by default, with the hint `Include this note in what CSRM sends to the AI for this request.`, sent as `changesAiInputs` only when checked.
- **Session text.** The composer writes its text to `writeSessionDraft("note", draftOwnerKey, requestId, text)` debounced at 300ms, reads it on mount (showing `Restored from earlier in this session.`), clears it on a successful post, and arms the beforeunload-only leave guard while it holds text. Both pages pass `draftOwnerKey` (13-09's server helper) to `NotesSection`.
- **Unsent well** (`unsent-note-well.tsx`, `'use client'`): rendered by the thread under the §8.4 line when the window is closed and a draft exists (after a `NOTES_CLOSED` refusal or on a closed request with a stored draft); the text read-only on `--color-bg-inset`; `Copy text` (ghost, small) writes to the clipboard and toasts `Note text copied.`; `Discard text` removes the well and the stored draft and toasts `Note text discarded.` with `Undo`, which restores both and toasts `Note text restored.`; focus moves to the §8.4 line (`tabIndex={-1}`) on discard and to `Copy text` after Undo. On `NOTES_CLOSED` the thread calls `router.refresh()` and keeps the text in the session store so the well shows it.
- **Domain additions:** `aiMarkToast(on)`, `aiMarkUndoToast(on)`, `unsentWellCopy`, and a test that no notes string contains `Accept`.
