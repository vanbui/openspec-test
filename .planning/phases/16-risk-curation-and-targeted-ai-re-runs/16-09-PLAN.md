## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** withdraw wrong risks and ask the AI to rewrite one risk or propose missing ones, **so that** the draft ends up complete and correct without rebuilding it by hand.

This plan's slice: asking the AI. Both operations run as recorded jobs on Phase 15's registry, complete into immutable proposals under the assessment's lock, and are readable with their state; nothing reaches a risk yet.

- **Operations** (`CSRM_ASSESSMENT_OPERATIONS`): `startRiskSuggestions: "POST:/api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/risk_suggestions"`, `startRiskRewrite: "POST:/api/v1/risks/{riskId}/rewrite"`, `getRiskProposalJob: "GET:/api/v1/risk_proposal_jobs/{jobId}"`, `retryRiskProposalJob: "POST:/api/v1/risk_proposal_jobs/{jobId}/retry"`, `listRiskAiProposals: "GET:/api/v1/risk_assessments/{assessmentId}/ai_proposals"`.

Controller `controllers/risk-proposals.ts` passes `{ documentId: ctx.state.idamboxUser.documentId, humanAnalyst: ctx.state.csrmAuthorization.humanAnalyst }` and maps errors to status and `error.details.reason` with `safeError`.
- **Enqueue order** (both; leak-safe): closed body (`{ instruction? }` or `{ instruction?, focus? }`; focus a non-empty array of distinct `statement`, `ratings`, `treatment`) else 400 → the assessment (or the risk and its assessment) exists, else 404 → analyst scope → the viewer raised the request → 403 `OWN_REQUEST` → read-only load with `assessmentReleasePopulate` and `assertAssessmentEditable` → 409 `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE` → rewrite: `isActiveRisk` false → 409 `RISK_WITHDRAWN` → `parseAnalystInstruction` (400 with its reason) and `repeatsRequestorText` over `loadRequestorTextCorpus` (400 `INSTRUCTION_REPEATS_REQUESTOR_TEXT`) → completed risk-proposal runs on the assessment at `RISK_PROPOSAL_RUN_LIMIT` (100) → 409 `AI_PROPOSAL_RUN_LIMIT` → `evaluateAiDataPolicy` refusal → 409 with its reason → enqueue on the registry with the key `suggestions:{assessmentId}` or `rewrite:{riskId}`; an active job for the key is returned instead (202 either way).
- **Execution.** Re-check the guard (fail `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`), the rewrite target's state (fail `RISK_WITHDRAWN`), the run cap and the policy (fail with its reason; nothing sent); build the context (`CONTEXT_TOO_LARGE` fails with no run); `createRunningAiRun` with `operation`, `assessmentRequest`, `assessment`, `risk` (rewrite), `initiatedBy`, `instruction`, `jobId`, `attempt` and the summary; call the provider and adapter outside any lock; then complete inside `withAssessmentLock` (no guard, no transition): when `isAssessmentEditable` is false, `completeAiRun` with the proposal and append `lapsed` for every item (`by: "system"`, cause `assessment-not-editable`); when a rewrite's risk is now withdrawn, append `superseded` for every unit (`by` the risk's `withdrawnBy`, cause `risk-withdrawn`); otherwise complete, and for a rewrite with at least one unit append `superseded` (`by` the run's initiator, cause `newer-rewrite` with this run id) to every open unit of the same risk in earlier completed rewrite runs. Any failure after the run exists → `failAiRun` with a safe code.
- **Registry.** One instance of `createProposalJobRegistry` per process for both operations. If 15-06's registry returns a retained terminal job for a key, wrap its `enqueue` so only `queued` or `running` jobs are returned (Pitfall 8), and record the change in the summary.
- **Job read and retry.** Any human analyst who may read the request; the requesting analyst → 403 `OWN_REQUEST`; unknown job → 404; retry only for `failed` and not in flight (else 409), re-running the enqueue checks from the guard onwards, with the retrier as `initiatedBy` and a new run. Projection `RiskProposalJob`: `jobId`, `operation`, `assessmentId`, `riskId` (string or null), `status`, `attempt`, `queuedAt`, `startedAt`, `completedAt`, `runId`, `failureCode`, `failureMessage`, `initiatedBy` (`ActiveUserSummary` or null), `canRetry`.
- **Read.** `loadRiskProposalRuns(strapi, assessmentDocumentId)`: one Document Service `findMany` on `api::csrm-ai-run.csrm-ai-run` filtered by assessment, operation in both values and status `completed`, at most 101 rows, fields limited to what the fold and projection need. `openRiskProposalSummary(strapi, assessmentDocumentId)` returns `{ openCount, openByRisk }` from the fold; a damaged run counts no open item and is logged once (fail open; it protects no access). `readRiskAiProposals({ strapi, assessmentDocumentId, viewer })` → requesting analyst 403 `OWN_REQUEST`; returns `RiskAiProposals`: `assessmentId`, `editable`, `openCount`, `openByRisk` (`{ riskId, count }[]`), `lastAskedAt` (the latest completed run's `completedAt`, or null), `actions` (`suggest`, `rewrite`, `decide`, each `{ allowed, reason }` with `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE`, an AI policy reason or `AI_PROPOSAL_RUN_LIMIT`; deciding needs only the guard), `runs` (`runId`, `operation`, `initiatedBy`, `startedAt`, `completedAt`, `providerName`, `providerVersion`, `instruction`, `focus`, `riskId`, `outcome`, `counts` `{ items, open }`, `adjustments` `{ code, count }`), `items` (open first by run newest first, then the 50 most recently decided: `runId`, `itemKey`, `kind` `risk` or `change`, `state`, `stale` for an open change from `isUnitStale` against the risk now and null otherwise, `riskId`, `unitKey`, `fields`, `current` and `proposed` display values with relation names, `similarTo` with the resolved risk number or null, `aiAccept`, `acceptBlocked` from `aiAcceptCheck` on the risk now or null, `sources` with labels resolved as Phase 12's citations, `uncited`, `suggestedControls` labels, `decision` `{ action, by, at, reason, cause }` or null, `addedRisk` `{ riskId, riskNumber }` or null, `canUndo`), and `jobs` (the active job per target and the latest failed one still retained).
