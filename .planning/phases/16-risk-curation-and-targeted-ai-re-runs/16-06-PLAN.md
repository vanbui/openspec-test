## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** withdraw wrong risks and ask the AI to rewrite one risk or propose missing ones, **so that** the draft ends up complete and correct without rebuilding it by hand.

This plan's slice: a withdrawn risk leaves the assessment's readiness, counts and every Requestor read, while the analyst still sees it marked with its reason (success criterion 1 on the assessment side).

</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Readiness.** `validateReleaseReadiness` and `projectReleaseReadiness` each build their risk list once with `activeRisks(value.risks)` and iterate it, so `risks[${index}]` paths name the same risk in both; Phase 12's `aiProposalsReviewed` requirement iterates the same list; the release path and the pre-edit transition inherit the rule through `validateReleaseReadiness`.
- **Projection.** `projectAnalystRiskDetail(document)` adds `withdrawal`: null when `isActiveRisk(document)`, else `{ at: withdrawnAt, by: projectActiveUserSummary(withdrawnBy) or null, reason: withdrawalReason }`; the loaders (`loadAnalystAssessmentRiskDetails`, `listAssessmentRiskDocuments`) populate `withdrawnBy` with the active-user fields. The analyst detail handler computes readiness over `activeRisks` of the loaded risks and still lists every risk.
- **Requestor and shared reads.** `listAssessmentRisks` (in whichever controller serves the route; both copies if both do) adds `...ACTIVE_RISK_FILTER` to its filters for both actors; `listRiskControlImplementations` loads the risk and returns the existing 404 `CSRM Risk was not found.` to a Requestor when it is withdrawn; `projectReleasedAssessmentDetail` and the released queries keep only `activeRisks`; `relationship-scope.ts` wraps the populated `assessment.risks` with `activeRisks` before `residualRiskCounts` and `controlImplementationCount`; `riskSummariesFor` adds `activeRiskSql("r")` to its `WHERE` so both assessment lists count active risks only.
- **Spec and web.** Component `RiskWithdrawal` (closed: `at` date-time, `by` `ActiveUserSummary` or null, `reason` string 1 to 500); `AnalystRiskDetail.withdrawal` required, `RiskWithdrawal` or null. Web: `riskWithdrawalSchema` exported from `assessment-schema.ts`, the analyst risk detail parser gains `withdrawal: riskWithdrawalSchema.nullable()`; the parity guard registers the component.
