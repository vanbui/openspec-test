## Phase Goal

**As a** risk owner, **I want to** decide each of my risks, accepting it or approving its treatment, within my authority once the analyst submits the final assessment, and as a Security Analyst I want an explicit review, a colleague's approval for the most serious outcomes, and one confirmed step that releases the assessment and closes the request, **so that** every closed request carries accountable acceptance and independent review.

This plan's slice: everyone reads exactly what they need to act. The analyst sees the review, what changed, the round's progress, what each risk waits on, what will be voided and which acts they may take; each decider sees what they decide, under which authority, and their own pending acts; nobody sees what is not theirs to read.

<objective>
Extend the analyst assessment detail (D-48) and the participant projection (D-47 as amended) with the review, peer, round, decision and readiness facts, through one projector shared with the acceptance read; compute the viewer's allowed actions and pending acts from the operations' own predicates (research Pattern 2). Change the web parsers in the same plan (06.9 lesson). Put X-42's values in the one status mapping and Phase 19's label vocabulary together (D-53).

Purpose: SC4 ("the owner … accepts it … from their Home": the Home and page read these fields), SC1's "changed since review" in the API, ASSESS-20 criterion 1's recommendation pre-fill and D-40's cost of each edit before resubmission.
Output: two projections, one projector module, the parsers, the status values and their parity.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>
