# Phase 17.1 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for review, peer approval and risk acceptance (ASSESS-14, 20, 24, 25). It covers:
> - the review, peer approval, stage, decision-state and `Closed` values in the one status mapping (D-49, D-53);
> - the analyst's `Review and submit` panel: readiness, `Mark reviewed`, `Changed since review`, peer approval and `Submit final assessment` (D-05 to D-14, D-19 to D-21);
> - the peer approver's view and executive approver naming (D-12 to D-18);
> - the acceptance round panel for both actors, the editing lock, recall and `Release and close` (D-22 to D-25, D-41, D-42);
> - the decider's form, recording, revision and voids, with authority by residual rating (D-26 to D-40);
> - the released record, Homes, lists, lifecycle, queues, report, export and register (D-47 to D-53);
> - this phase's notification kinds (D-54) and the refusals (family V).
>
> It adds no colour, type size, weight, family, radius, shadow or duration. Six patterns are proposed as CSRM extensions X-42 to X-47.

## Voice, formats and shared copy rules

Phase 11's, Phase 13's and Phase 17's rules apply unchanged. In addition:

- **Terms (D-66):** `Mark reviewed`, `Changed since review`, `Peer approval`, `Ask {name} to approve`, `Approve` (peer), `Executive approver`, `Submit final assessment`, `Awaiting acceptance`, `Acceptance round {n}`, `Recall round {n}`, `Accept`, `Treat`, `Propose` (owner, Critical), `Approve decision` (executive, High), `Decide as an exception` (executive, Critical), `Treatment owner`, `Resolve by`, `Review by`, `Release and close`, `Closed`. Never `Decline`, `Sign off`, `Submit for approval` or `Reject` for a decision.
- **`Accept` is the decider's word (Pitfall 17).** As a control it renders only inside a decider's form. A recorded decision reads `Decision: Accept`. The analyst's recommendation is always worded `Recommends accepting` or `Recommends treating`, on every surface and to every reader, so it never reads as a decision.
- **Authority labels:** `risk-owner` → `Risk owner`; `risk-owner-with-executive-approval` → `Risk owner, approved by the executive approver`; `executive-exception` → `Executive approver, as a time-limited exception`.
- **Parts** add `Executive approver`; a list part reads `Executive approver · {k} {risk|risks}` and joins others with the middle dot (X-4).
- **Intervals and dates come from the server.** Copy names the served interval (`Review within {m} months`) and the served preview date (`Review by {date}`), as X-3 calendar dates; the web computes no authority, interval or date (D-58).

## 2. The analyst editor: review and submit (D-05 to D-11, D-19 to D-21; X-43, X-47)

### 2.1 The `Review and submit` panel

It replaces the `Release readiness` card in 16 §1 item 3's grid while the assessment is unreleased and no acceptance round is open (§5 takes the place while one is). | Part | Contract |
|---|---|
| Description | `Mark the assessment reviewed once it’s complete, then submit it so each risk owner decides their risks. Editing pauses while they decide.` |
| Readiness | 10 §14's anatomy over the served `submissionReadiness`: count line `{met} of {total} requirements met`, or `Ready to submit`. Labels (served; per Risk where marked): `Request assigned to a Security Analyst` · `Assessment can be changed` · `No response round open` · `No acceptance round open` · `No AI request running` · `Summary` · `Overall rating` · `Overall rationale` · `At least one Risk` · `Review of AI proposals` · `Decisions on AI proposals` · `Responses decided` · `Risk statement`* · `Inherent risk rating`* · `Residual risk rating`* · `Eligible risk owner`* · `Recommended treatment`* · `Executive approver`* · `Marked reviewed` · `Peer approval` (only when the policy needs it) |
| Steps | An ordered list of three h3 steps, 16px apart, each with its badge (§1) and one action: **1 `Review`**, **2 `Peer approval`** (only when required, §2.3), **3 `Submit`** |
| Step 1, not reviewed | Badge `Not reviewed`. `Mark reviewed` (primary, `BadgeCheck`) once every item except the last two is met; until then the line `Mark it reviewed once the other requirements are met.` |
| Step 1, reviewed | Badge `Reviewed`; facts (`--text-caption`) `Reviewed {instant} by {name}`. No action |
| Step 1, withdrawn | Badge `Changed since review`; line `{name} changed {what} {instant}, which withdrew the review.` (`{what}` is `the assessment details` or `{risk number}`, with the edit's kind, served); figure link (X-10) `{c} changed {section|sections}` to the first marked section (§2.2); `Mark reviewed` (primary) |
| Step 1, no longer counts | Badge `Review needed again`; the served reason: `Reviewed by {name} before the request was reassigned.` or `Reviewed before acceptance round {n} was recalled.`; `Mark reviewed` (primary) |
| Step 3 | `Submit final assessment` (primary, `Gavel`) once ready; until then `Submit once every requirement is met.` Only one primary renders in the panel: the earliest step with an action |
| Earlier rounds | When acceptance rounds ran: caption `Acceptance round {n} was recalled {date}. Submitting opens round {n+1}.` |

`Recommended treatment` outstanding carries the served caption when a planned control blocks a recommended accept: `Recommends accepting, but a planned control counts in its residual rating. Recommend treating instead.`

**Mark reviewed** applies at once (§5 Optimistic UI): step 1 shows `Reviewed`, focus stays on the step's h3, and an Undo toast (X-14) reads `{assessment number} marked reviewed.`; `Undo` withdraws it and confirms `Review undone.` Not offered while any round is open.

### 2.2 What changed since review, and what a resubmission voids (D-08, D-40; X-47)

- **Section markers.** While the review is withdrawn, each section whose content differs carries a `Changed since review` badge after its heading: `Assessment details`, and each Risk card's h3. A Risk added since reads `Added since review`; one withdrawn since reads `Withdrawn since review` on its X-27 card. Same tone and icon (§1). A reverted edit leaves step 1's line and no marker.
- **Between rounds** (an acceptance round was recalled), each Risk card with a recorded decision shows one line under its h3: `{name}’s decision from round {n} stands if you submit again.` (`--text-caption`, `--color-text-secondary`), or a `Will be decided again` badge (warning, `RotateCcw`) with `Changed since {name} decided in round {n}: they will decide again.` The submit confirmation repeats the count (§2.4).
- Markers are analyst-only and render nowhere else.

| Picker | 13 §6's frame over the eligible-colleague read: title `Ask a colleague to approve`, X-2 search, display names only, the X-15 empty line `Only active Security Analysts who didn’t raise this request, aren’t assigned to it, didn’t review it and take no part in it can approve.` Primary `Ask {name} to approve` |
