## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** tell the AI what to add, change or remove in a questionnaire and to have it propose follow-up questions without touching answered ones, **so that** the question list keeps up with new information while I stay in control.

This plan's slice: the request hub lets the analyst steer the first generation, replaces a questionnaire only when nothing is lost, and points to open proposals; a Requestor answering at the wrong moment loses nothing.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Hub read.** `page.tsx` calls `listQuestionnaireAiProposals(questionnaireId)` when the request has a questionnaire and the viewer is an analyst; a failure leaves the lines absent (the server still gates), and an `OWN_REQUEST` refusal marks the requesting-analyst state; it passes the view to `AssessmentDetail`, which passes it to both job cards.
- **Generate card.** Idle: `InstructionField` (label `Instruction for the AI (optional)`, §3.1's Generate hint, draft key `generation-instruction:{requestId}`) above `Generate questionnaire`; the action `startQuestionnaireGenerationAction({ requestId, instruction })`; a field refusal shows beside the field; success clears it. For a requesting analyst: no field; the caption `You raised this request, so you can generate without an instruction. Another Security Analyst can give the AI one.`
- **Replace.** `replaceOffer(view)` decides: offered → the outline `Replace questionnaire` (`Sparkles`) opening Phase 10's `ConfirmDialog` whose description is always §8.3's sentence, followed by `InstructionField` (draft key `replacement-instruction:{requestId}`), confirm `Replace questionnaire` (destructive) and dismiss `Keep this questionnaire`; not offered → §8.3's line for answered, analyst changes or open proposals, with `Open AI proposals` or the figure link to `/app/review/questionnaires/{id}#ai-proposals`. Phase 10's one-change, n-change and unavailable-count descriptions are removed. Without a served view (a requesting analyst), Replace follows Phase 10's relation rule only when `analystChangesSinceGeneration` is 0, and a refusal shows family I's copy.
- **Proposals line and job.** Analysts other than a requesting analyst see `AI proposals: {n} open` (a figure link to the panel) or `AI proposals: none open`; `AiInputsChangedBlock` receives `StartProposalsButton` when `actions.start.allowed`, otherwise a caption from `panelGuidance`; a hub-started job shows `The AI is preparing follow-up questions.`, `Started by {name}.` when not the viewer, `Open AI proposals`, polling and `Check again` as §2.3, then `{n} AI {proposal is|proposals are} ready to review.` with `Review AI proposals`, or `The AI proposed nothing new for this questionnaire.`, or Q3's copy with `Retry AI proposals`.
- **Draft card.** While `openCount > 0` or a job is active, the idle `Generate assessment draft` is replaced by `draftGateLine(openCount)` linking to `/app/review/questionnaires/{id}#ai-proposals`; a refused enqueue shows the draft card's `AI_PROPOSALS_OPEN` copy.
- **Session storage for the Requestor's answers is settled.** UI-D25 is accepted (15-CONTEXT, Orchestrator Confirmations, 2026-10-01): when a save is refused, the Requestor's unsaved answers are kept in session storage through 13-09's session draft store, as Phase 13's note drafts are. The UI-SPEC "Notes carried" question is closed; this plan implements the accepted posture and asks nothing further.
- **Requestor save.** `useAnswerAutosave` treats a 409 with `QUESTIONNAIRE_NOT_ANSWERABLE` as blocked: autosave stops, `SaveStatus` shows §11.2's sentence (from `answerSaveRefusal`), the form keeps its values, and the unsaved answers are written with `writeSessionDraft` under `unsaved-answers:{questionnaireId}` as `{ questionKey, answerType, choicesSignature, value }`, guarded by `useLeaveGuard`; the submit dialog shows §11.2's submit sentence. On the next load of a released questionnaire, `restorableUnsavedAnswers(kept, questions)` returns only answers whose question has the same key, is active, has the same answer type and choices signature and holds no saved answer; the renderer applies them as unsaved edits and shows `unsavedAnswersRestoredCaption(n)`; everything else is discarded silently and the store is cleared.
