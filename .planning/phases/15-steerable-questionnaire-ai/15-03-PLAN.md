This plan's slice: the revision timeline can record and show every AI proposal and every change a confirmation makes, the Requestor's history can never carry them, and every closed reason the phase refuses with exists end to end.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Keys and their kinds** (closed in `parseGovernanceEvent`; any other combination is refused): `ai-proposal` requires `runId` (1 to 255), `scope` (`questionnaire` or `question`), `providerName` and `providerVersion` (1 to 255 each), `itemCount` (integer 0 to 30), exactly one of `instruction` (1 to `INSTRUCTION_MAX_LENGTH`) or `instructionSource` (`plain-language-preset` or `default-task`), and, for scope `question`, `questionKey` and `questionText`; `question-added`, `question-edited` and `question-deleted` may carry `runId`, `itemKey` (`i-1` to `i-30`) and `aiEdited` (boolean) together, plus optional `aiUndo` (true); `question-restored` requires `questionKey` and allows `questionText`, `runId`, `itemKey` and `aiUndo`; `regenerated` may carry `instruction` or `instructionSource` (`default-task`). The 500 bound is `INSTRUCTION_MAX_LENGTH` imported from `apps/backend/src/csrm/ai/analyst-instruction.ts` (15-RESEARCH Pitfall 12: one constant).
- **Requestor projection.** `projectGovernanceEventsForRequestor(events)` (pure, in the log module): remove `ai-proposal`; for each event carrying `runId` and `itemKey` without `aiUndo`, find a later event with the same `runId` and `itemKey` and `aiUndo: true` with no `released` event between them, and remove both; then strip every AI key. The scope module's Requestor branch of `projectGovernanceEvents` calls it before its existing `kind`, `at`, `explanation` projection; the analyst branch passes every key through with actor names resolved as today.
- **Spec.** `QuestionnaireGovernanceEvent` and `QuestionnaireRevisionTimelineEvent` gain `ai-proposal` and `question-restored` in `kind` and the optional keys `runId`, `itemKey`, `aiEdited`, `aiUndo`, `scope` (enum), `instruction` (`maxLength: 500`), `instructionSource` (enum `analyst`, `plain-language-preset`, `default-task`), `providerName`, `providerVersion` (`maxLength: 255`), `itemCount` (integer 0 to 30); both keep `additionalProperties: false`. The descriptions say the Requestor projection never carries the AI kinds or keys.
- **Timeline.** `eventPresentation` gains `ai-proposal` (`Sparkles`; label `Proposals requested from the AI` for scope `questionnaire`, `Plain-language rewrite requested from the AI` for scope `question`) and `question-restored` (`ArchiveRestore`, `Question restored`). Added lines, verbatim from UI-SPEC §10: `Instruction: “{instruction}”`, `Instruction: none - the AI suggested the questions it judged still needed` (default task), `Instruction: Rewrite in plain language (standard wording)` (preset), `Provider: {name} · Model: {version}`, `Result: {n} {proposal|proposals}` or `Result: nothing proposed`, `Question: {text}` (question scope); confirmation events add `Source: AI proposal, confirmed` or `Source: AI proposal, edited before confirming`; Undo events add `Source: undo of a confirmed AI proposal`; `regenerated` with an instruction adds `Instruction: “{instruction}”`. Links: an optional prop `proposalAnchors?: { runs: ReadonlySet<string>; items: ReadonlySet<string> }` renders `Show in AI proposals` (record link to `#ai-run-{runId}`) and `Show proposal` (record link to `#ai-proposal-{runId}-{itemKey}`) only for listed anchors; plan 15-11 passes it from the proposals read. The summary depth (the Requestor's) counts `question-restored` among changes.
- **Reasons.** Strapi `CONFLICT_REASONS` and the web list gain the eleven reasons named in the must_haves, kept in the list's existing order rule; both conflict response schemas' `reason` enum gain the same values (10-06's unit test asserts the three lists equal). The instruction reasons answer 400 and are declared on 400 responses by plans 15-08 (generation) and 15-09 (the new operations).
