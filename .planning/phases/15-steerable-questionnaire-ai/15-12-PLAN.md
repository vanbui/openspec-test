## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** tell the AI what to add, change or remove in a questionnaire and to have it propose follow-up questions without touching answered ones, **so that** the question list keeps up with new information while I stay in control.

This plan's slice: each unanswered question can be rewritten in plain language from its row, answered questions are visibly fixed, release waits for the proposals, and the header and the "What the AI used" sheet show what the AI was asked and used.

<objective>
Add the question-row parts and the provenance surfaces (UI-SPEC §4.7, §6, §7.1, §8.1, §9): the Rewrite in plain language control with its form and row states, the X-26 Answered note, the open-proposal link per row, the release line, the header's open-proposal line and third-line count, the signal's Suggest follow-up questions action, the run sheet's Instruction and Questionnaire groups, and the copy guard over the new feature.

Purpose: success criterion 2 (rewrite in plain language, accepted or rejected), success criterion 3 in the panel and sheet, D-08's release gate in the UI, and UAT-018 (an AI route once answers exist, visible on the questionnaire itself).
Output: three new components, the changed governance rows and release control, the header, the signal action, the sheet groups, and their tests; the existing questionnaire and provenance journeys run unchanged.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (existing specs, once at the end).
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Rewrite control** (`QuestionRewriteControl({ questionnaireId, question, job, openItemAnchor, nothingToSimplify })`, client): rendered by the governance rows only when the read's `actions.rewrite.allowed` is true and the question is active and not `answered`; the trigger is a ghost small button with `SpellCheck`, named `Rewrite in plain language: {question text}`, `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `data-print="hidden"`, after Edit and Delete; it opens a `Popover` from 768px and a bottom `Sheet` below, titled `Rewrite in plain language`, with `InstructionField` (hint from §6.1, draft key `question-rewrite-instruction:{questionId}`) and `Rewrite question` (`Ctrl`/`⌘` `Enter`) and `Keep as is` (`Escape`); on start the row shows `Rewriting…` with the inline spinner, focused (`tabIndex={-1}`), polls the job, and on completion refreshes the page; failure shows `The rewrite didn’t finish.` and `Retry` named `Retry rewrite: {question text}`; a no-change completion shows `The AI found nothing to simplify in this question.` until the next page view.
- **Answered note** (`AnsweredQuestionNote`, server-safe): the provenance note `Answered` with `LockKeyhole` and the visually hidden ` - the AI can’t change or remove it`, rendered from the served `answered` flag only (the server omits it while the Requestor answers).
- **Rows.** The governance rows (analyst review) and `ReleasedQuestionList` (submitted view) add the note, the control and `Open AI proposal` or `{n} open AI proposals` (a `RecordLink` to `#ai-proposal-{runId}-{itemKey}` that moves focus to the card) from the read's open items by `target.questionId`.
- **Release line.** In place of `Release questionnaire`, `releaseGateLine({ openCount, activeJob, countServed })` renders with `Lock`: the count as a figure link to `#ai-proposals`, `Go to AI proposals` for an active job, and the countless line for a requesting analyst; a release refused in a race shows family E's `AI_PROPOSALS_OPEN` copy in the dialog.
- **Header.** `AiProvenanceHeader` gains `openProposals` (a figure link line `{n} open AI {proposal|proposals}` to `#ai-proposals`, omitted at null or zero) and `headerReviewLine` counts `proposal-confirmed` and `proposal-edited` as `{c} from AI proposals` (omitted at zero). `AiInputsChangedBlock` gains an optional `action` slot; `QuestionnaireReview` passes `StartProposalsButton` (outline small `Sparkles`, `Suggest follow-up questions`, starts the default task and moves focus to the panel's job status line) when `actions.start.allowed`.
- **Sheet.** `AiRunSheet` adds the `Instruction` group (`Given by` the analyst's name, `Plain-language rewrite (standard wording)` or `None: the AI suggested the questions it judged still needed`; `Instruction` with the X-25 display rule) and the `Questionnaire` group (`Scope`, `Questions offered`, `Answered` with `(the AI couldn’t change them)`, `Answers` included or not with its reason, `Open proposals listed`, `Rejected proposals listed` as `{r} of {t}`, `Removed questions listed`) from the run read's new summary sections, via `sheetInstructionRows(run, initiatorName)` and `sheetQuestionnaireRows(summary)` in `ai-assistance-domain.ts`; the description is §9.3's sentence for questionnaire proposal runs.
- **Copy guard.** `interface-copy-guard.test.ts` adds `src/features/questionnaire-ai/*.tsx` and `src/features/ai-assistance/field-comparison.tsx` to its files and adds a check that their user-visible strings never use the three verbs UI-SPEC "Verbs" reserves away from the analyst's review act.
