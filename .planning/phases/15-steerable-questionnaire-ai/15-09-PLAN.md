## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** tell the AI what to add, change or remove in a questionnaire and to have it propose follow-up questions without touching answered ones, **so that** the question list keeps up with new information while I stay in control.

This plan's slice: the proposal operations are served through Strapi with exact grants, refused to everyone but a human Security Analyst who did not raise the request, and published in the contract.

<objective>
Serve the six operations (D-03 items 1 to 5) through routes, controller handlers and the closed specification; grant them through five new API-only Functions held by `role.security_analyst` only, in both catalogue copies, and add them to `HUMAN_ANALYST_ONLY_OPERATIONS` (D-31); build the proposals read (D-03 item 4); add the six-actor matrix rows and the catalogue test rows; regenerate the served inventory (D-02).

Purpose: QUES-11 and QUES-12 at the API boundary, with every negative case of the authorisation table proven over HTTP.
Output: six routes end to end, the read projection, the grants, the contract and the authorisation proofs. The web consumes them from plan 15-10.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.
</objective>
