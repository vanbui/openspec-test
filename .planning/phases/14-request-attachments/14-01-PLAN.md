## Phase Goal

**As a** Requestor or Security Analyst, **I want to** attach supporting files to a request at any point, **so that** the assessment can use the vendor pack, data flows and spreadsheets the business already has.

This plan's slice: the record and the design vocabulary the attachment work builds on. The five interface patterns are recorded in the direction, the glossary names the attachment, and the attachment entity, its operations and its rules are written up as a contract-gap record that the pilot approves before any schema exists.
