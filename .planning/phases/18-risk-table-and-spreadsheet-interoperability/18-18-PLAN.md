## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want** an assessment's risks in a table I can edit, copy and paste like Excel or a SharePoint list, **so that** reviewing and updating many risks takes minutes rather than a long scroll.

This plan's slice: everyone else's table. The released record, a paused assessment and every participant read the same table read-only but copyable; an unrelated Requestor sees nothing; phones get cards; keyboard users skip straight to the risks.

<objective>
Present the read-only modes from the server's mode (D-05) with their banners; give every participant the read-only, copyable grid of their projection above Phase 17's cards (D-05, D-06, D-07, UI-D17); build the phone card list and filters sheet with the detail sheet as the phone editor (D-26); add `Skip to risks` (UAT-063); prove SC4's read-only half and SC5's phone half in Playwright, with Maya refused and UAT-039's questionnaire half at 375px.

Purpose: ASSESS-17 criterion 4, ASSESS-15 criterion 3, SC4, SC5; UAT-039 and UAT-063.
Output: the banner, the participant grid, the card list, the skip link and their tests.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (dev servers stopped).
</objective>

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Banner (`risk-table-banner.tsx`).** `RiskTableBanner({ mode, assessmentNumber, roundHref })`: an info `Alert` above the toolbar when `mode.editable` is false; first line by reason: `ASSESSMENT_NOT_MUTABLE` → `{assessment number} is released, so its risks are read-only.`; `RESPONSE_ROUND_OPEN` → 17 §5.1's line with `CirclePause`; `ACCEPTANCE_ROUND_OPEN` → 17.1 §5.2's line with `CirclePause` and `Go to round {n}` linking to the Overview's round panel; `ACTOR_IS_RISK_OWNER` or `ACTOR_IS_EXECUTIVE_APPROVER` → 17 §5.2's line; second line `You can still select, copy and export the risks.`. `risk-table.tsx` applies §7.1's read-only toolbar and bar and adds `decision` to the default columns while a round is open.
- **Participant grid (`participant-risk-table.tsx`).** `ParticipantRiskTable({ assessment, risks, viewerParts })`: `RiskTable` in read-only mode with `riskTableColumns({ actor: "participant", released })` (exactly the participant projection's fields; both override reasons shown beside their ratings), the `Your risk` tag after the viewer's own risk numbers and Phase 17's own-risks `Show` filter, inside a region named `{n} risks` at most 60% of the viewport tall with sticky header and number column; each Risk number links to `#risk-{number}` and focuses that card's h3. No views menu: columns and sort are stored at `csrm:participant-risk-table:{userId}` inside try and catch. Rendered by the participant view in `released-assessment.tsx` from 768px, after the `Show` filter and before the cards; below 768px the page is unchanged.
- **Phone list (`risk-card-list.tsx`).** `RiskCardList({ rows, columns, mode, onOpen })` supplies `DataGrid`'s `renderMobileCard`: the DataTable mobile card (block link with `ChevronRight`), line 1 the risk number with the residual rating badge at the end, line 2 the statement clamped to three lines, line 3 (`--text-caption`, secondary) `Owner: {name or Not set} · {treatment status}`, line 4 the roll-ups (`AI values to confirm`, X-48's count); withdrawn cards as X-27. `RiskFiltersSheet`: `Filters and sort` (outline small, `SlidersHorizontal`) opening a full-screen `Sheet` with the view choice as a radio list, `Show`, sort and every filter, primary `Show {n} risks`. The phone toolbar is `Search risks`, then `Filters and sort`, `Export` and, for analysts in editable mode, `More` (`Add risk`, opening the sheet's form in create mode, and `Import`). A card opens the detail sheet full screen; no change set exists on phones.
- **Skip link.** `RouteSkipLinks` (client) in `app-shell.tsx` renders `Skip to risks` (href `#risk-grid`, the grid's caption id) before `Skip to main content` when the path is an analyst Risks route or a participant assessment page at 768px and above; activating it focuses the grid's caption (amendment 4).
- **Fixtures.** `risk-table-fixtures.ts` gains `openResponseRoundOn(request, fixture)` (prepares release-for-response readiness through the published operations: confirms the drafted AI values, nominates Jane as owner, then opens round 1, which opens the participant gate) and `nameRiskOwner(request, fixture, { riskNumber, userEmail })`.
- **Spec.** `describe('read-only and participants')` and `describe('phones')` in `risk-table.spec.ts`, each on its own fixture: the seeded released record's Risks is read-only with the released banner, and Ctrl+C copies a range; with round 1 open Bob's Risks shows the paused banner and no `Add row`; Sam named risk owner sees the named-analyst line; Jane's participant page shows the grid with `Your risk`, copies with Ctrl+C, and offers no edit, paste, Import, save or views; Maya gets the not-found on the participant page and the Risks route and Strapi's 404 (`expectNotFoundForMaya`, `expectApiRefusals`); `Skip to risks` focuses the grid.
