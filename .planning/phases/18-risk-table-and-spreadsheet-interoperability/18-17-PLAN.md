## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want** an assessment's risks in a table I can edit, copy and paste like Excel or a SharePoint list, **so that** reviewing and updating many risks takes minutes rather than a long scroll.

This plan's slice: the record in two routes. The Overview shows what the assessment says and how far it has come, with risk figures that open the table exactly; the Risks route holds the table; readiness takes the analyst straight to the cell that needs work.

Output: the record layout, the Risks section, readiness links and the updated title and accessibility specs.

Edits Strapi source? No. Changes the specification? No. Runs Playwright? Yes (dev servers stopped).

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Layout.** `git mv` `[documentId]/page.tsx` to `[documentId]/(record)/page.tsx` (URL unchanged); `(record)/layout.tsx` (server) reads `readAnalystAssessmentRecord(documentId)` and, when it succeeds, renders `AssessmentRecordHeader` (the editor's page header with its actions, now exported from `assessment-editor.tsx` and no longer rendered inside `AssessmentEditor`) and `RecordLocalNav` (items `Overview` and `Risks ({n})`, `{n}` the active risks; current from the segment) before `children`; on a failed read it renders `children` alone so each page renders its own not-found, permission or error state. The Risks page drops its own header and navigation (18-05). `[documentId]/loading.tsx`, `error.tsx`, `report/` and `history/` stay where they are. Document titles: Overview `{assessment number} overview | IdamBox CSRM`, Risks `{assessment number} risks | IdamBox CSRM`; h1 `Risk assessment review` on both.
- **Risks section (`risks-summary.tsx`, client).** `RisksSummary({ assessment, risks, scale })` as a `DetailSection` h2 `Risks` (region `risks-summary`) in 16 §1's item 5 position: the unsaved line (from the stored change set) with `Review changes` (`/risks?changes=1`); figure links (X-10, `KpiTile` link form from 768px, a definition list of links below), each with its filter: Active risks (`show=active`), residual Critical, High, Moderate, Low (`filter=residualRating:oneOf:{label}`, each with its §3.6 badge as label), Not rated (`filter=residualRating:isEmpty:`), Not on the scale (`filter=notOnScale:present:`), Without a risk owner (`filter=riskOwner:isEmpty:`), AI values to confirm (`filter=aiValuesToConfirm:present:`), Open contest points (`filter=contestPoints:present:`), Withdrawn (`show=withdrawn`); each count computed with `applyRiskTableView` on the same rows, so the link's total equals the figure; a zero is plain text; `Open risks` (outline pill, `Table2`); first use `No risks yet` · `Add a row, paste from Excel or import a file.` · `Open risks`.
- **Overview page.** Drops `listControls()` and the per-risk `listControlImplementations` reads (D-27); passes `riskScale` and the risks to `RisksSummary`; `AssessmentEditor` no longer renders Identified Risks, the risk cards, the withdrawn list or the risk-level toolbar actions (moved to Risks by 18-15); the `AI proposals` section stays.
- **Readiness links.** `release-readiness-summary.ts` gains `readinessHref(row, assessmentDocumentId)`: a row whose destination names a risk and field links to `/app/review/assessments/{id}/risks?cell={riskNumber}.{fieldKey}`; a row naming a set links to Risks with that set's filter; other rows keep their destination. The table activates the `cell` parameter's cell on load (focus stays on the caption until the first key; the cell line shows it).
- **Risks additions.** While open AI proposals exist, the toolbar shows `{n} open AI {proposal|proposals}` linking to `/app/review/assessments/{id}#ai-proposals`; `?changes=1` filters to changed rows.
- **Specs and tests.** `record-route-titles.test.ts`: the Overview entry's page path `(app)/app/review/assessments/[documentId]/(record)/page` and its title; a Risks entry. `performance.spec.ts`: the Overview's title and its populated locator (the `Risks` region instead of Identified Risks); both routes within their budgets. `accessibility.spec.ts` and `production-hardening.spec.ts`: the record title as `{assessment number} overview | IdamBox CSRM` (a regex for the first-record case) and the guard on `Risks` instead of `Identified Risks`. `risk-table.spec.ts` `describe('overview and readiness')`: each figure opens Risks with the same total; a readiness row opens Risks with its cell active; the local navigation keeps unsaved changes between the routes without a warning.
