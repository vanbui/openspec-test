## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst, **I want to** have a data-driven dashboard, reports, and alerts covering my review queues, high and critical risks, and overdue treatments, **so that** I can prioritise analyst work from live workload signals.

<objective>
Lay the authorisation and derivation foundation every later Phase 7 plan builds on, and nothing else.

1. **Pure derivation rules.** Every rule is Strapi-free and takes an explicit UTC `today`, so it is deterministic under test (D-02):
   - the D-04 treatment due state;
   - the alert vocabulary, urgency, id, day offset and ordering, with per-record oracle classifiers (D-10, D-11, D-29, D-31);
   - report partitions, month buckets and the median (D-13).
2. **Exact grants (D-07).** Five API-only analyst Functions carry the five served operations, and the reserved FunctionAPIs are re-pointed to them. Both catalogue copies stay byte-identical, and a design note records the five unserved operations (D-06).
3. **Human-analyst guard (D-07, D-14).** The policy refuses an automation identity, and refuses a human analyst who lacks the Risk Register or review-queue read.
4. **Seed idempotency proof (D-33).**

Purpose: plans 02, 04, 06 and 08 mount handlers against these grants and cite these rules. Nothing is published in `specification.json` here. Each operation is published by the plan that serves it, together with its closed client parser (D-08).

Output:
- five `src/csrm/insights` modules;
- the `csrm-analyst-insight` operations and scope modules;
- policy changes;
- both catalogue copies;
- three new Strapi test files, and one extended.
</objective>
