## Phase Goal

**As a** Security Analyst or risk manager, **I want to** export any list I can see and to import spreadsheets without fighting the format, **so that** CSRM fits the Excel work around it.

This plan's slice: questionnaires and alerts export. Bob exports the questionnaire review list and his alerts as they stand; anyone exports their own questionnaires.

<objective>
Serve the Questionnaires, My questionnaires and Alerts exports (D-03 group 1, D-04) over the 19-04 pipeline: export variants of each list's own query builder or parser, three views, routes beside their lists before any parametrised sibling, the policy members and underlying reads (D-13), the contract chain and catalogue entries (D-02), and the API proofs with the matrix rows (D-16, D-38).

Purpose: the remaining analyst review list and the first insight list export through the same module, with no new due-state logic (G-16).
Output: three served operations with their chain and proofs.

Edits Strapi source? Yes. Changes the specification? Yes. Runs Playwright? No.

Scope note: 19 files at four tasks. They stay together because the three operations share the catalogue Functions, the specification, the matrix and the integration file in one wave; most files take a one-route or one-function change (accepted by the pilot's pacing rule, as 14-07 was).
</objective>

- **Views.** `questionnairesExportView(strapi)` and `myQuestionnairesExportView(strapi)`: count with the builder's count query; ordered `documentId`s with `findMany` (`fields: ["documentId"]`, the builder's sort, `limit: cap + 1`); hydrate with the list's own populate in batches of 500; project with `projectQuestionnaireCollection`; extractors for every `questionnaires` or `my-questionnaires` column (`status` through `questionnaireStatus`, `assessmentRequest` the request number, `progress` the list's progress text). `alertsExportView(strapi)`: the alert list is derived on read, so `count` and `selectIds` run the alert list's own derivation for the parsed filters and sort (ids are the alerts' stable keys), and `hydrate` returns those derived alerts in batches; extractors for every `alerts` column (`urgency` through `alertUrgency`, `condition` through `alertKind`, `record` the source record number, `governingDate` the alert's governing date).
- **Specification.** `/v1/questionnaires/export`, `/v1/my_questionnaires/export` and `/v1/alerts/export` (`get`), each with its list's query parameters except `page` and `pageSize`, plus `format`, `columns`, `timeZone`, and the binary 200 and errors as 19-05 declared.
