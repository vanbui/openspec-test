## Phase Goal

**As a** risk manager or leader, **I want** trends, ageing, cycle times and a heat map, **so that** I can see whether risk and the assessment process are improving.

This plan's slice: a Security Analyst follows a stage's median from Cycle times to the page of episodes behind it, slowest first, and opens the slowest request; a stale link or a missing permission says what happened and what to do next.

## Recorded planning decisions

- **Presentation (`report-records-presentation.ts`, zod-free).** `recordsPresentation(key, request, data)` → `{ breadcrumbTitle, title, description, recordNoun: { one, other }, columns, backHref, backLabel }`. Stage episodes: title `{Stage}: time in stage`, description `Times in {stage} that ended in the last {p} months, slowest first.`, columns `Request` (or `Risk assessment` for `response`, `rework`, `acceptance`) · `Title` · `Round` (`Response round {n}` or `Acceptance round {n}`; only for round stages) · `Entered` · `Left` · `Days`, noun `episode`. Round measures: title the measure's label from 21-08's section copy, columns `Risk assessment` · `Title` · `Round` · `Opened` · `Closed` · `Days` or `Count`, noun `round` or `assessment` as the measure counts. `backHref` restores `period` and the report filters from the request.
- **Page.** `records/page.tsx` mirrors the report page: `canReadReports()` first (presentation only), then `isReportKey` (else `notFound()`), `reportRecordsRequestSchema.safeParse` over the search parameters (a failure renders the 400 state without a read), then `getReportRecords`; 403 → `PermissionDeniedState area="reports"` with the `INSIGHT_SCOPE_MISSING` description when that reason is served; 400 → Z1's drill-down copy; 404 → `notFound()`; other errors → `RetryableErrorState noun="these records"`. Metadata title `{title} | IdamBox CSRM` for a reader, `Reports | IdamBox CSRM` otherwise. `loading.tsx` renders `ReportDrillDownSkeleton`; `error.tsx` the retryable state.
- **View (`report-drill-down.tsx`, Server Component).** Breadcrumb, `PageHeader` with the `Back to {report}` outline pill (`ArrowLeft`), the total line, `DataTable` with the presentation's columns, sort cue, pagination (total, rows per page 5, 10, 25, 50, page links preserving the query), sticky first column, mobile cards (`{number}` and `{d} days`; title; `{stage or round} · Left {date}`), width `--spacing-grid-max`, and the cleared state.
- **Family Z (`refusal-copy.ts`).** `reportRecordsRefusal(status, reason, reportTitle)` and `listFilterRefusal(status, reason)` return the Z1 sentences and their kind (`blocked` or `retryable`); `INSIGHT_SCOPE_MISSING` maps to its description; unknown reasons fall back per status.
