# Phase 06.1 Plan 02: Questionnaire Reversal Summary

Implemented the protected questionnaire return-to-draft workflow while retaining the established envelope-derived lifecycle.

## Delivered

- Added an authorised, locked questionnaire reversal operation with persisted reason, time, and reverting identity, together with matching route, catalogue, OpenAPI, and generated declaration coverage.
- Reversal converts each submitted answer envelope to a draft envelope, preserves its value, and removes `submittedAt` in the same transition.
- Kept no questionnaire status attribute: the questionnaire lifecycle continues to be derived from its answer envelopes.
- Moved questionnaire reversal to its own API-only Function. It is granted to the owning Requestor and human Security Analyst, while the service analyst role holds neither aggregate’s reversal Function.
- Preserved the analyst privacy boundary: after reversal, the analyst sees awaiting-submission content and audit facts, not draft answer values.

## Verification

- Committed unit and integration coverage exercises envelope conversion, value preservation, status and relation guards, actor grants, rollback safety, and analyst masking.
- Phase-level suite and typecheck evidence is recorded in the verification report.

## Deviations from Plan

- The final grant model uses a dedicated API-only reversal Function rather than the originally proposed questionnaire-details Function, preventing service-role automation from reopening submitted questionnaires.

## Self-Check: PASSED

The questionnaire operation, envelope transformation, dedicated grant, audit projection, and regression coverage are present.
